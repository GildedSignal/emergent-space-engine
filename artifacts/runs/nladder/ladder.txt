THE N LADDER (13 October, Sonnet, wt/nladder): how many real bodies the plane holds, and what breaks first. BEFORE any change.

What was run. `cargo run --release -p emergent-universe --example nladder -- <N> <coast|burn> 20` (crates/universe/examples/nladder.rs): the plane
plus N more ships (scenario::plane_extra / ladder_ship: ids after the plane's own ships, before the player's; min(N/2, 300) about Home and the Moon
(a quarter on the Moon's orbit 60 degrees ahead/behind, the rest 5,200 to 166,000 root units out, Rayleigh e of scale 0.03), the rest a belt between
2 and 3 AU, Rayleigh e of scale 0.07 (re-drawn above 0.4: periapsis > 1.2 AU), uniform angles; every number an integer from the seed and the index; each
body its own exact orbit). The page's frame loop (60 frames a second of wall time at rtf 100, `frame_to`, 12 ms engine budget) for 20 s of page time:
coasting, or burning forward at L8 from 1 s (the player leaves Home's SOI for the star's doll at frame 947, 15.8 s). One process a rung and flight, <= 60 s
(a run is cut at 55 s: "truncated"). Columns: p50/p99/max = engine ms a frame after the first second; >12ms = frames over 12 ms after the first second; lag = the
worst lag of the presented time behind the target; ev/b/gs and kick/b/gs = events and kicks per body per game-second (all bodies, after the first second);
held = most scans held for idle time; view ms = one warm gather of the view (the page's instrument, outside the engine's frame; -1 = not run, near 60 s);
MiB = peak physical footprint (compressed pages included; the resident size alone under-reports on this machine under load).
Verdict: "pass" = literal F1 (no frame over 12 ms, lag 0); "pass as N=0" = no worse than N=0's own coast run (its one frame over by 7 us at frame 61 is the
fleet's opening exodus, 10 ms of the 12, present at every N: the F1 table of wt/frame has the same 12.0-12.1 ms at forward L8) and under 13 ms. Refusals: 0 at every rung.
Seed 1, 48 fleet members, 1 thread. Raw: artifacts/runs/nladder/before/ (one file a run, machine state first). Script: ladder.sh, table.py.

Machine state (artifacts/runs/frame/state.sh, at the start of the runs, also in each raw file): load 2.1-3.2 (Arc, Claude, other builders' compiles), battery power,
swap 4.0-5.1 GB of 5-6 GB used (the machine was already swapping), no thermal warning. Fanless M1 8 GB. One sample per rung: p99 and max move by a millisecond between
runs (10^3 burn: max 11.8 ms here; the margin to 12 ms is thin).

FIRST BREAK (named, measured):
  The term: the frame's per-body walks in `Universe::advance` (crates/universe/src/universe.rs):
    (1) `rekey`: every frame recomputes the deadline (event time + light distance from the player) of EVERY body and re-sets it in the Calendar: N requeues, 24 ns each at 10^3-10^4, 41-54 ns at 10^5;
    (2) the loop's drain: the Calendar is ordered by deadline and a body whose next event is after the target is "set aside" when it reaches the head, so the loop pops EVERY body every frame
        (take_due calls = N a frame), and afterwards re-sets each (a second N requeues: requeue calls = 2N a frame);
    (3) `prune` every 64 frames walks every body (1.5 ms a call at 10^5); (4) the player's neighbourhood scan `capture` walks and clones the binding of every body at every player item (45-50 ns a body:
        4.5 ms at 10^5, 0.25 ms at 10^4), and `pull(ships)` for the player scans every ship for the minimum next tick (N next_time reads per player item).
  Its cost: ~160 ns per body per frame while it fits in cache (exponent 1.0, p50 0.98), ~420 ns per body per frame at 10^5 (exponent 1.4). It crosses F1 first through the p99 frames: at 10^4 the walk (1.7 ms)
  plus a frame of 400 events (the 300 locals' orbits: 10.5 ms) is over 12 ms (4 late frames in coast, 7 in the burn, lag 1.1 s / 0.13 s); its p50 alone crosses 12 ms at ~3x10^4 and the budget is gone at 10^5 (1,060 of 1,060 frames over).
  What it is not: the events (30-65 us an event, flat in N: process 190-225 us a frame at every N; kicks and events per body per game-second fall as 1/N because only the 300 locals and the player's neighbours have short periods),
  memory (2.4-2.6 KB a body flat: 2,285 MiB at 10^6, 4 GiB at ~1.7x10^6 bodies), refusals (zero everywhere), the build (27-31 us a body: 30 s at 10^6, 60 s at ~2x10^6).
SECOND TERMS, outside or after the first (measured here, not fixed by the first fix):
  - The view (the page's instrument, not the engine): `gather` reads every body every frame: 11 ms at 10^3 (warm, 11 us a body here), 134 ms at 10^4, 608 ms at 10^5. The page's 16.7 ms frame cannot carry it past ~10^3 bodies.
    It is not the engine's F1; it is listed because the page pays it.
  - Held scans: a burn holds hundreds of scans (326 at 10^3, 570 at 10^4, 1,020 at 10^5), each a clone of every candidate's binding (the 300 locals now; every body of the doll when the player is in the star's doll).
  - The player in the belt: the scan against every body of its doll is exact and O(candidates) per player item (encounters.md: 1.5-1.9 us a body; priced out as a per-pair filter): these flights stay near 1 AU,
    so they never meet it (the burn reaches the star's doll at 15.8 s but is still at 1 AU, within about 0.02 AU of it; the belt's periapses are above 1.2 AU). A flight into the belt is the next ladder.
  - Memory and build: 2.4-2.6 KB a body (a compiled orbit per body in the catalogue, never shared: 1.2-1.5 KB; a 448 B record; plan, info, spec) and 29-31 us a body to build: the plane cannot hold 10^7 whatever the frame does.
