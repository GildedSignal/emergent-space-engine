## Refuted

- **"A k ≈ 16 constant neighbour read (any metric) replaces the walk at K/k ≈ 20–1,500×"**
  (20261018-1530-system-afff's proposal; the checker found the burn and dense failures; the
  second-author run closed it): k = 16 is violated by 1,447/10,559 burn hits (s1) and 3,823/4,024
  dense hits, and the swept metric rescues only 1.4%/0.9% of the burn class — the binding variable
  is μ_win, and the gain law is K/μ_win (1.0–85×), not a constant (20261018-1617-…-687c).
- **"The swept metric is the mechanism"** (the checker's proposed fix, 20261018-1530's check):
  measured inert at every regime flown — swept and at-t rank profiles coincide within 2%; the
  burn failures are density, not geometry (20261018-1617-…-687c).
- **"The scan is a no-surprise machine (≥ 90% empty answers)"** (20261018-1530-system-afff's P1):
  46.9% empty at the shipped and ladder scenes, 12.6% at the crowd — only the answer's *locality*
  is certifiable, now as the window-membership record.
- **"The ladder's belt inflates the player's scan"**: the belt is doll 0 and never a candidate;
  the inflation is the locals (Home-doll) — the walk couples to the doll population, the answer
  does not (20261018-1530-system-afff).
