# Dossier — the stall decomposed: density ÷ throughput, not a prologue floor

**Claim.** The original run's phenomenology holds — past a budget that cannot clear the front, the
plane freezes silently (no error, frames cheap, digest bit-exact, worst frame ≤ 16 ms), and the
freeze deepens with members — but both its mechanism and its headline number are wrong, now by
direct measurement: there is **no fixed per-frame prologue** (`advance(target, Budget::events(0))`
costs **15.0 µs/frame at 428 bodies**, 99% of it rekey at ~29 ns/body, 13–67× below the claimed
0.2–1 ms floor), the delivered rate is exactly **throughput ÷ event density** (the m4→m400 cliff
at 0.2 ms factors as 792× = **11.3× throughput** (2.1× pricier events × 5.3× slower frames, the
frame dominated by the *view*, 3.3 of 4.8 ms) **× 70× density** (the 400-member opening runs
~30 events/game-s against m4's 0.34)), and the "delivers 0.00×" is a median-frame artifact: the
stalled run delivers **28.8×** unpaced and **5.2×** paced — as a stutter in which **39 of 600
frames advance the clock, each by exactly 1.3 game-s**. The adoptable guard is therefore a
**density/throughput gate that sheds the target rtf** (local density × target must stay under the
budget's sustainable events/s), not an O(1)-in-N prologue audit.

**Bottleneck.** Board #4 (smoothness and the allocator), foundation 7 of docs/plane.md. The
original dossier proposed gating the allocator on an O(1)-in-N per-frame prologue; this run kills
that gate and replaces it with a measurable one. The demand arithmetic it enables: holding 3,000×
through m400's opening (30 ev/gs at ~65 µs/event) needs 5.85 s of engine per wall second — 293×
the 5 ms/60 Hz budget — so at 400 members *no* frame budget can hold the target and the allocator
must dip it deliberately; today it instead freezes silently. What this deletes: the prologue audit,
and any smoothness gate that watches `frame_ms` (the freeze never shows there: worst frame 15.95 ms
in the worst paced cell).

**Evidence** (Linux x86, 8 vCPU shared, load 0.18–0.25, 15 GB; orders of magnitude only — the
integrator re-measures on the M1). Fresh build of the author's patch plus a `--prologue-bench`
mode (saved in `work/patch.diff`); all artifacts in `data/`, tables regenerated by `tables.py`.
perf is blocked on this box (`perf_event_paranoid=4`), so the profile of `Universe::advance` is
taken the direct way: `advance(target, Budget::events(0))` **is** the per-frame prologue (rekey +
queue/horizon bookkeeping, zero events bought), timed in a loop at the bench's end state.

| members | bodies | prologue µs/frame | rekey µs | queue µs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 32 | 6.7 | 6.6 | 0.07 |
| 48 | 76 | 7.5 | 7.4 | 0.03 |
| 100 | 128 | 8.5 | 8.5 | 0.06 |
| 400 | 428 | **15.0** | 14.7 | 0.23 |

Events bought per frame e(B) at m400 (budget-bound at every budget, overflow 600/600):
**1.18 / 3.09 / 20.3 / 75.7** at 0.05 / 0.2 / 1 / 5 ms — 15–24 events per budget-ms, **no knee**;
a real 0.2–1 ms prologue would give e ≈ 0 below it. At m4 and m100 e(B) rises until the scene's
demand caps it (the run then keeps up with rtf 3,000).

The cliff at 0.2 ms, factored (delivered = T/D in every cell, 22 runs):

| | m4 | m400 | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| delivered (run-average) | 22,836× | 28.8× | **792×** |
| throughput T (events/wall-s) | 7,822 | 691 | 11.3× = 2.1× (e) × 5.3× (fps; frame p50 0.82→4.77 ms, view 3.35 of it) |
| density D over range (events/game-s) | 0.34 | 24.0 | 70× |

The scene's density profile at m400 (deterministic scene, nested coverage): **17 / 32 / 31 / 3.5
events/game-s** over game-time (0,41], (41,78], (78,413], (413,9916] — a front-loaded opening
(fleet hand-outs, battle birth) ~9× denser than the tail; m100: 6.6 / 10.0 / 2.2 / 0.63; m4 flat
0.35. "Scales with N" is this scene property, not a per-frame cost.

The paced worst cell (60 Hz, 10 s, m400): **0.2 ms → delivered 5.2×, median frame 0.00×, 93% of
frames frozen, 100% of game time in the top 10% of frames — 39 jump frames of exactly 1.3 game-s
each, worst frame 15.95 ms, digest exact; 5 ms → delivered 564×, median 618×, 6% frozen** (so even
the default budget dips to 19% of target at 400 members). Paced m4 at 0.2 ms: 956× delivered,
1% frozen — the freeze is members-driven.

The record, corrected and regenerating (all digests bit-exact against the original run's artifacts,
cross-process, fresh build): row 1 was **scripted** for 300/3000/30000 and coast for 300000 —
re-run that way the medians come back 300.0 / 3,000.0 / 22,410 / 6,837 with digests

**Reproduce** (each ≤ 60 s after `cargo build --release -p xtask`, from this folder; script
`run_grid.sh` runs the lot in ~3 min):
`$XT universe --bench --seed 1 --threads 1 --rtf 3000 --seconds 10 --unpaced --coast --members {4,100,400} --engine-ms {5,1,0.2,0.05} --series data/<name>.csv`
(grid, `data/G-*`); `… --members 400 --engine-ms {0.2,5}` without `--unpaced` (paced worst cell,
`data/P-*`); `$XT universe --prologue-bench --seed 1 --threads 1 --rtf 3000 --members {4,400} --iters 2000 --warm 600`
(`data/prologue.txt`); `$XT universe --bench --seed 1 --threads 1 --rtf {300,3000,30000} --seconds 10 --unpaced`
and `… --rtf 300000 --seconds 3 --unpaced --coast` (row 1, `data/R1-*`); `python3 tables.py` prints
every table above from `data/`.

**Predictions.** `predictions.md` was written before the grid. Held: P1 (no knee: e(0.05,400) =
1.18 ≥ 1; 1.6% of the events at 1% of the budget), P2 (the 792× cliff = 11.3× ≤ 30× throughput ×
70× ≥ 10× density — the pre-registered bands), P3 (stalled cells deliver ≥ 10×: 17.5–28.8×
unpaced, with 88–94% frozen frames and 100% of game time in the top 10%), P4 (paced worst cell
5.2×, inside the pre-registered 2–50× band, point estimate 8; median frozen; worst 15.95 ≤ 17 ms;
digest equal), P5 (prologue 15 µs at 428 bodies, inside 5–100 µs; 0.3% of engine time at 5 ms, 5%
at 0.2 ms), P6 in substance (all digests bit-exact; medians exact except the 30,000 row, −25%,
disclosed above). Nothing failed outside the disclosed load-scatter.

**What would make it wrong.** The density profile D(t) is derived *across* budgets on the
deterministic scene (nested ranges), not within one run — the per-frame series has no events
column; if the scene's event order were not budget-independent (it is: one digest per members
count across all budgets and pacing), the profile would be wrong. The prologue is measured at the
5 ms end state (428 bodies, the *upper* bound; the stalled state has 371); if rekey's cost per body
grew super-linearly past ~10⁴ handed-out bodies, the prologue audit would return — at ~29 ns/body
it needs ~7,000 bodies to reach 0.2 ms. Linux/shared-box numbers: the 30,000-row scatter shows the
medians move ±25% at the capacity edge; the digest and the decomposition do not. Cost of adopting:
small — the gate is arithmetic on quantities the dashboard already has (events per frame, ticks
presented per frame): shed the target rtf when local density × target exceeds the budget's
sustainable events/s, and keep the original run's dashboard metric (delivered-RTF lowest 1%,
ticks presented per frame) as the player-visible side. Fit with PRINCIPLES: "the simulation tells
us what's next not us" (P22) — the front's own density is the signal to shed on, not a
per-frame cost audit; "the algorithm that allocates the work to prevent those heavy tails, it's
the most important one" (P12) — the heavy tail here is the opening burst, and the allocator's
choice is dip-or-stutter; "Errors never compound" (P13) — verified again: one digest per scene
across budgets, pacing, processes and days; "don't multiply by zero" (P25) — the zero was never
the delivered rate (28.8×), it was the *median frame*, and conflating them would have bought the
wrong gate.

**The lens.** Second-authorship made me measure what the first author asserted and the checker
counter-read, and the direct prologue measurement (blocked perf turned into
`Budget::events(0)`) is the run's cleanest kill. What it may have made me miss: I took the
T/D decomposition on one seed (the checker's seed-2 cells cover the cliff only); the paced grid is
0.2/5 ms only (the 0.05 and 1 ms paced cells are unmeasured); the view gather — 70% of the frame
at m400 and the fps term of the throughput drop — is outside the engine budget and I did not test
whether parking it during overload buys back the stall; and "delivered = T/D" is an identity once
both are measured on the same run, so its content rests on the two factors being independently
grounded (e(B) flat; D a scene property across budgets) — a per-frame events column in the series
would ground D(t) inside a single run and is the one-line harness change I would hand the next
author.
