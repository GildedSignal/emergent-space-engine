candidates (δ→1; the answer bill rises to the δ=1 row only — 84.7× — and the ledger's
ordering still stands); or the capture ALL-walk count scales with N (re-ranks the ledger;
the per-frame law P6 survives). Both are one flight with 23a6's instrument.

answer bill is the kick stream priced at the read; and "the cheapest compute is compute
not done" (1) — the bound's lump-shape wins are real but no lump shape deletes the
stream.

## The lens

Composition runs inherit their constants' errors: every input here is one page's
published number, and the 96/era capture leg is an upper bound inferred from a channel
total, not a count (the 3e5 leg is counted). The 732-gs uniform-gap assumption is the
single largest lever (δ vs clustered gaps): lesson 2's flight closes it, and until then
every δ-derived 1e9 number is a band, not a constant. The δ=1 branch is kept live
everywhere, so no conclusion above depends on δ's value. One quote is re-used from an
earlier run (We Are Legion, also quoted by 7bd9) for a different decision — disclosed in
lessons.md §5. What a library lens can miss: it does not fly; if the tier arm is already
landing elsewhere this window, the ledger should be recomputed on its constants, not
adopted from here.
