**The five lessons (READWISE_TOKEN unset — all quotes UNVERIFIED, from `lab/library/goodreads.csv` + knowledge):**
1. *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator* (Lefèvre): "It never was my thinking that did the big money for me. It always was my sitting." Mechanism: frequency itself is a cost; profit sits in few big swings. **Tested and inverted for the law**: acting only on big swings biases the secular average (S2a/S2b). The lesson survives only in the allocator — magnitude gates may trigger *work*, never *kicks*. Cheapest test: this probe (done).
2. *Elon Musk* (Isaacson): "The best part is no part." Mechanism: delete before optimize. Change: the only deletion that survives is **whole-orbit** (H0190's averaging, 32→0.3 kicks/orbit away from resonance); within-orbit deletion is killed (S2a). Audit deletions per orbit/regime, never per kick. Test: H0190 + this probe (done).
3. *Structures* (Gordon): ductile structures shed load; brittle ones concentrate it. Mechanism: a correction concentrated in time is a stress raiser. **Confirmed**: mass-conserving concentration (S2b) explodes energy error ×185 at e=0.6; the engine's own r4-v2 kill of arc-integrated kicks is the same failure. Rule: shrink kick count only as error-per-kick shrinks (correctors, higher-order splits) — never by merging kicks. Test: this probe (done).
4. *The Use of Knowledge in Society* (Hayek): users act on a stale, compressed price. Mechanism: consumers of a perturbation don't need the source fresh. Change (the one lever this probe leaves untested): **moment staleness decoupled from kick cadence** — neighbours' multipoles refresh on their own quantum, bodies kick on theirs. Cheapest test: rerun C2 with the perturber's position read stale by Δt; error-vs-staleness curve (~1 h rung).
5. *Antifragile* (Taleb): the barbell — extreme safety one end, extreme risk the other, avoid the fragile middle. Mechanism: continuous medium-size correction is the fragile zone. Change: judge the kicks bottleneck by the **distribution of events per orbit** (most orbits 0 events, p99 carries the budget, per compiled frequency regime), not the mean — C2 shows flat-tail regimes where 8–16/orbit is simply the floor. Test: report B-gate passes against events/orbit histograms, not means.

**What would make it wrong.** A second-order (midpoint/DKD) kick convention could reorder S1′ vs S0 — but all four schedules share this probe's convention, so "no schedule beats uniform" is convention-robust; the C1/C2 floors sit near the estimator's 3×10⁻³ bias, so differences below that are unresolvable here (cloud x86, orders of magnitude only — re-measure on the M1). Adoption cost: ~zero — it deletes a direction and adds one histogram to the gates. PRINCIPLES fit: "Nothing is evolved that can be derived" (#3) — refined: a kick's surprise is its *time distribution*, not its magnitude; "influences below one quantum accumulate" (#6) — confirmed, but accumulation must stay continuous, quantizing its firing times is a representation error; "the right representation always just feels right" (#19).
