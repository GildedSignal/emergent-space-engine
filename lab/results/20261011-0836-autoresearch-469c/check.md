**Why lead, not silver.**
1. **The kept patch changes the law's outputs.** `bind_in` (crates/universe/src/bind.rs): the patch rebinds `let exact = in_tube_units(exact, unit)?`, so `residual = exact.position − tp` subtracts doll-unit decoded positions from tube-unit positions whenever `Unit::unity` is false (`Unit::of(host.mu + gm)` for secondary dolls with a mass mantissa ≠ 1). `decode` returns doll units (bind.rs:118–121), so the original mixed nothing; the patch mixes scales. The turn is scale-invariant (cross/dot), the residual is not. At rtf 100 — the player's flight speed, one of the author's own held-out cases — this fires and the replay digest changes on two independent seeds. The author's predictions.md says: "Any digest change is a KILL of the change, not a measurement." By that pre-registered criterion the change is dead.
2. **The dossier's headline is contradicted by the reproduction.** "P1 held exactly: all three held-out digests are identical to the pristine tree (`03dcd421…`, `5a4c42cd…`, `35f786a5…`)" — `5a4c42cd…` is the *patched* seed-7 digest; the pristine one is `29a40cdc…`. Either the pristine seed-7 run was never compared (the author reported seed 7 as unmeasurable-under-load for timing but cited a digest identity they could not have observed), or the mismatch was seen and not disclosed. Both are the exact failure mode the lab grades down: "a headline the log contradicts."
3. **Novelty is thin.** The exact-memo-0%-on-distinct-kicks finding is the taste log's 5 October correction, re-derived; the quantized 12+12-bit memo's 83–94% hit rate with orbit changes was already ruled a law change. The bench itself ships the memo instrumentation (`Shapes` in kick_bench.rs) — the author read it, not built it. The actionable conclusion ("the lever is the number of `Passage::compile`s, not their constant factor" → the slack skin, `b-encounter-skin`) is the already-adopted gold from components-4643 (92% of per-kick evals are window setup; ceiling 1.1–1.2×). What is genuinely new is the finer stage split (quadrature 57–61% inside the rebind, `old_tau` 7%) and the `profile` feature as a standing dashboard — instrumentation, not a bottleneck move; no 10×, nothing deleted.
4. The ~0.8% gain of the `node()` `cos` deletion is noise-adjacent by the author's own measurement (their repeatability ±0.6%) and is invalidated as a free win by point 1.
