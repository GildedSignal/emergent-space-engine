## Claim

self-destruction the page attributed to the DK_CAP clamp zone is instead **contact
relaxation driving a core–halo split**: 490/492 dead bodies (B) and 495/498 (C) never

process self-quenches into a collapsed core (37% of survivors inside r < 2.1q, medians
r ≈ 7.6q) plus an escaped halo.

## Bottleneck

The chaos-shadowing page's fix list ("Pass belt bodies or an exact inner zone — not
cadence, not the outer rounding") aims the engine at the wrong target for belts: an exact
inner zone would not save the belt, because the belt's deaths never touch the zone. What
the fix must decide is whether a contact-collisional belt is wanted at all — this wiring's
belt relaxes catastrophically in ~3 dynamical times (negative-heat-capacity core collapse
+ evaporation), a fate no inner-zone exactness changes. The measured laws (hazard λ,
per-contact e-step, quench structure) are the certificate the belt page needs and the
sizing rule for any re-birth cadence: at this density the belt's half-life is ~3 orbits.
