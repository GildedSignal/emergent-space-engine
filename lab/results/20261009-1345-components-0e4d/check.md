**What the author missed.**

- Part A's numbers in evidence/dossier/prediction #5 are not regenerable from the shipped patch — the exact defect the taste log records as silver-not-gold.
- 256-key maxima are the wrong statistic for a ≥99% gate: fail rates over ≥2048 keys decide, and they change the shape-axis verdict materially (0/256 vs ~51% at shape 10).
- The 10⁹ extrapolation inherits the plane's factor-1.5 belt; belt width multiplies the cold-belt cost (measured above), so "per belt" needs the width as a parameter or a stated envelope.
- The memo is ungated memory: hooking its bytes into B18's accounting would freeze this finding where the judges already look.
- The 12/12 margin at belt scale is 1.7× (5.9e-4 vs 1e-3), not the recorded 2.9× — thinner than the dossier implies, and worth stating when arguing the key cannot be coarsened.
