Second-author run on 20261008-1252-taleb-2570 (silver). The checker's named experiment for gold
is done here: the ladder rerun inside the real range (`--members` clamps at 400), ≥ 3 seeds,
bracketing 48/96/192, with the clamp and the corrected gather-share range stated, and the

## Claim

Inside the load range the bench can actually produce (members ≤ 400, i.e. ≤ 8.3× design), the
plane's wall cost saturates (p99 ≤ 14.9 ms, worst ≤ 19.4 ms, ≤ 1 frame over 16.7 ms per run)
while presented progress stalls to zero from 2× load on — median `rtf.lowest_1pct` over seeds
{1, 7, 13}: **9.96 at 1×, 0.39 at 2×, 0.029 at 4×, 0.000 at 8×** — and at every rung of that
range the canonical digest is invariant to the engine budget (0.5 vs 50 ms: overflow frames
200–765 vs 0, one digest). The frozen service gate (`rtf_low1_median ≥ 1.0`, "no frame presents
zero ticks at 4× design load") KILLs the shipped service at 4× with a passing positive (1×) and
a failing negative (8×): the smoothness gate must move from wall hitches to `rtf.lowest_1pct`.

## Bottleneck

Board bottleneck 4 (smoothness and the allocator). Gain: not speed — the retargeted gate and
the corrected cliff location (2×, not 4×; ≤ 8.3×, never 32×) are what the allocator work now
aims at. It deletes two wrong calibrations an integrator would have adopted: a kill criterion
pegged to 4×, and any rung above members 400 (the clamp makes it a rerun of 400).

## Predictions

`predictions.md` was written before the runs, including the frozen gate verbatim. All five
held: P1 the bracket (m48 all ≥ 1; m96 two of three < 1; m192 all ≤ 0.31; m384 all exactly 0),
P2 wall saturation (worst ≤ 19.4 ms, ≤ 1 frame over 16.7, p99 ≤ 14.9), P3 digest invariance at
all three real loads, P4 gather share 0.63–0.70 with max < 16 ms, P5 the rung decides KILL,
not VOID. The one nuance P1 allowed for showed up: the 2× collapse is bursty — seed 13's worst
1% stayed at 9.6× at 96 members — which is why the gate judges the median of ≥ 3 seeds.

## What would make it wrong

Locations are this box's (Linux x86, shared): on the M1 (≈ 4× faster per H0211) the cliff
moves right, toward 8×; the shape (saturating wall, stall-to-zero tail, budget-invariant
digest) is what transfers. `rtf.lowest_1pct` is the shipped instrument's 1st percentile of
per-frame progress, wall-sampled: a gate on a single seed of it can misfire in either
direction (seed 13 at 2×), so the frozen rung judges medians. What remains unverified, as the
original dossier already flagged: that a stalled frame re-presents the same tick in the http
view — the dashboard's `game_ticks` reading (dash.rs `record`) is verified in code, not yet in
the browser.

## Cost of adoption, and fit

Nothing merges: measurement, `analyse.py`, `metrics.json` and the rung draft
(`rung-zero-stall.md`) for the integrator to freeze. Costs are attention: retarget the
smoothness gate to `rtf.lowest_1pct` (the instrument already ships), calibrate the service
floor to the real range (2× is where it breaks here), and cap or fix the members knob before
any rung trusts a load above 400. Fit — PRINCIPLES: "i care about the p95/99 results i want
the game to be smooth" (the p99 passes while the service freezes: the gate was reading the
wrong tail); "Errors never compound" (digest invariant under 765-starved frames); "a test that
cannot fail is a control" (both controls fired).

## The lens

The second-author framing made me inherit the checker's experiment wholesale and not re-derive
it; its shape held, but two things it let me skip: the burstiness of the 2× stall deserves a
longer window than the 30 s kept for comparability (55 s is the bench's cap), and the
http-view confirmation of the zero-tick frame — the one link in the reading that is code-read,
not measured — is still open. And by rerunning only what was named, the digest-invariance
result stays "verified at overload" rather than novel (H0210 claims it by design; `digest_note`).
