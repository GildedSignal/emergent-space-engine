# Dossier — the family's population is the fleet, never the phase-locked belt; the fork channel closes by composition

## Claim

Composed from measured constants, the formation family's row is the **driven/fleet
population** (bytes: 113–244× off the 5,060 B plateau at F = 400; fires: 44.6× at the
plane's own 48 members, and the 10⁹ kick row swings 0.69×→6.9× budget on 1–10% fleet
share alone, closing at any share with F = 10⁴) — while the phase-locked belt stays the
compact store's (family floors at its word w = 8–32 B vs compact 2.6–5 B, missing the
≤ 6 B scoreboard row at every F), the Home/Moon locals are dead domain by the r²·H law
(11.6–17.3× beyond the 15,000-root kill radius, word forks in 1–20 kicks), and the fork
bill is ≤ 0.23% of the stream saving at the worst measured cadence — od 3 (the fork
handler as H_live) closes by composition at the belt.

## Bottleneck

Scoreboard rows "Memory per body in the flyable engine" (gap ~10^2.6–2.9) and the event
half of the CPU row; board focus area 3 (families/formations, ~10² memory, ~10 kicks).
Measured multipliers: 10² bytes on the fleet population, 44.6× fires at the plane's fleet,
and a per-population split the 10⁹ ledger must carry (the fleet share term dominates it).
What it deletes: the family as a candidate 10⁹ belt store (one whole adoption route), and
any gate flight at dead radii (locals, GEO).

## What would make it wrong

A steady disagree rate at the belt far above the fleet's 0.16% (unmeasured — od 4's rate
experiment) with a re-bind-class fork handler; the member word costing ≫ 32 B (w is a
band, not a measurement); a 10⁹ scenario whose fleet bodies never sit at doll cadence as
tubes (then the LEO event term is the lattice's, and T4's swing is void); w11 failing the
B13 encounter-phase gate (od 1, unflown — this dossier prices the payoff, not the gate).

## Cost of adoption

The one calendar-item change (the shared key, digest-neutral on the aligned grid per
b560) + w11 words + the od-1 gate flight, scoped to two populations (belt, fleet) instead
of four. Nothing here is wired; the ledger only says where the wiring pays.

## Fit with PRINCIPLES

"The right representation always just feels right" is tested, not assumed: the family is
kept only where the tide lets the word hold, and handed to the compact store where it does
not ("we know we have a fish, we need to find its water"). And "an ant cares about a
raindrop but the lion does not" (P33): the event ledger's fleet term is the raindrop the
belt's average hides.

## The lens

Composition, no flight: every number is page-cited arithmetic, so shared-harness bugs are
absent but page errors inherit. The lens may have under-weighted what only a flight shows
(the steady belt disagree rate, the word's true width); both are named above as the
falsifiers, and the gates (od 1) remain the gold experiment this ledger now scopes.
