# Predictions (written before the decisive run)

Target premise: representation.md §2 "Observables are emergent" + law.md line 53 —
"Each visible tube crosses the observer's past light cone once; Newton's method finds
the crossing. Lensing adds crossings, from each doll's memoized deflection(b)."

Attack: the view's composed image (flat retarded crossing + deflection(b) applied as a
lens correction) near compact masses. Cases below were NOT tuned on; they are the
held-out sweep.

## P1 — image placement (Schwarzschild, equatorial, M = 1)

Setup: observer static at r_o ∈ {10, 50}; source behind the hole at r_s ∈ {50, 200};
β (flat angular separation from the hole) swept over the strong-lensing range.
Construction = point-lens equation with flat trig distances and the EXACT two-point
deflection α(b) from the oracle quadrature (so α is perfect; any error is composition
error, i.e. the premise, not the memo's accuracy).

Prediction: near the Einstein ring and inside it (b_eff ≲ 10 M), the construction's
image angle is off by **more than 1%** of the image angle (the tolerance B3/B16 style
gates use for light) for a majority of β values with r_o = 10; and off by **more than
5 degrees** for at least the β-neighborhood of the shadow edge. Weak-field control
(b_eff ≥ 100): error < 0.1%, because there the thin-lens composition is legitimate.

## P2 — image count

Prediction: in the same strong-field cases the exact gather finds **2 bright images**
(primary + secondary crossing the cone from the other side), and for r_o = 10 there are
β values where a third image (one winding) is within a factor 10 in brightness-relevant
angular separation; the "one crossing + memoized deflection" mechanism as written
cannot place the secondary by deflection alone, because the secondary's geometry is
not a small correction to the flat direction. Measured: count exact roots of the
arrival-azimuth equation vs roots of the construction.

## P3 — the scalar memo cannot exist for rotating dolls (Kerr)

law.md promises the lopsided shadow (B19) and Julien wants rotating holes, but the
view memo is deflection(b), a function of one positive number. Prediction: for
a = 0.9 M, the critical impact parameters of the two senses differ by a factor
**≈ 1.8–2.0** (b_retro/b_pro), and at the same |b| = 6 M the equatorial deflections of
the two senses differ by **more than 1 radian**. So a one-argument memo cannot serve
both sides of the same hole: the key must at least carry the sense, and off the
equator (all view directions) the Carter constant Q — a 2-parameter family.

## P4 — the cone's curvature in time (softer, lower confidence)

The design memoizes the bend, never the delay. Prediction: for an emitter moving at
v = 0.5 c at r_s = 15 M, observer at r_o = 10 M, the shown-position error from
ignoring the Shapiro delay (≈ v·Δt_shapiro) reaches **at least 10%** of the
deflection displacement itself in the same scene. If it comes out below 5%, this
prediction fails and the delay gap is cosmetic for the view (still real for
navigation/ranging knowledge).
