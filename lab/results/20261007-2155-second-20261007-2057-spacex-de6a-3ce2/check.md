A steady-contact scene (a belt feeding persistent encounters) may admit a stable rate; the generalisation is asserted, not measured. Likewise the 10⁹ extrapolations are Fermi-level from one 48-member scene (honestly labelled).
- Bit-for-bit regeneration holds for digests, integer counters and scar widths; the entropy floats are ulp-unstable (HashMap order) — worth stating before anyone promises full-JSON identity.
