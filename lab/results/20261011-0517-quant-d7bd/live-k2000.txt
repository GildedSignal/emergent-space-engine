`Universe::start()` sizes the deadline-position cache `pos` for the bodies that exist
then; `add_body` after `start()` never extends it. The player's pull loop runs the fleet
battle, a member leaves, `resolve_handout` pushes body id 2028, and `apply` indexes
`pos[2028]` (len 29). Post-`start()` `add_body` is off-label: growth after start is
supported only through fleet hand-outs. In-situ flight at K > 0 is therefore not
measurable through the public API; per predictions.md P5 the live claim shrinks to K = 0
and the direct sweep carries the N-dependence.

The restart control (Run A's third digest) also showed a second `Universe::start()` is
NOT digest-neutral (the fleet re-keys), so restarting after adding bodies was never a
valid alternative either.
