I ran the named geometry: it holds (1.022/0.991), so nothing was hidden — but by the letter, the author never ran the case (s)he pre-registered. Same ledger class 3ebf's checker flagged.
- **The claim sentence overstates per-ship resolution.** "The active-ship set within 0.003–0.04 absolute" is true only on the x/w scenes; the a-scenes are 0.05–0.13 off and my a=1.5/e=0.4 runs 0.11–0.14 off; per-active load is ~30% off at low λ (1.43 vs 1.98). Totals and ranking are the robust quantities; identity resolution at low λ is Poisson-limited and should be scoped as such. The table is honest; the headline is not.
- **b2-300 is in-sample.** The co-rotating frame was chosen on it (disclosed as an instrument note, ratio 0.51 → 1.016), yet its 1.016 row sits in the same table as the out-of-sample scenes without a label.
- **The regime boundary is wider than caveat (ii).** Repeated crossings (grazing orbits: 40–50% of contacts outside one window) and unconcentrated fleets (w=2: 77% outside) break the single-window aggregate as measured; in-window accuracy holds, but the trigger needs a second condition — packet concentration / one crossing per window — and repeated crossings need one aggregate per conjunction epoch. μ alone does not encode this.
- **The O(n) hides a per-ship constant**: 256 segments × 144 grid cells ≈ 3.7×10⁴ cell iterations per ship (≈3.7×10¹⁰ at 10⁶ ships) — trivial on the M1 GPU, but the CPU cost and the grid-resolution knob should be stated for adoption.
