# Dossier — 20261010-0352-taleb-cb7c (lens: Taleb)

**Claim.** The plane's retained state is bounded by the light-cone lag window, not by history (H_live ≈ 0 after warm-up, RSS drift 0 kB over the last thirds of every run), determinism holds on every axis tested, and the first thing that breaks under load is the **unbudgeted view gather** — max 23.25 ms against a 16.67 ms frame at 428 bodies — not the budget-clipped engine (max 9.4 ms against its 5 ms budget).

**Bottleneck.** Board #4 (smoothness, the worst frame) and the HANDOFF risks "H_live not measured yet" and "the light cone keeps growing", plus the queued `b-readout-cost`. Expected gain: it redirects the readout work to the one term that crosses the deadline (the gather is 0.55–0.68 of frame time at every load), and it closes the memory-ruin question for the plane as built: nothing accumulates, so 10⁹-in-8 GB is decided by populations (B18b, shipmem), not by the flying plane's history. It also shows the smoothness headline (p99 2.45 ms, no hitch) is a 76-body number: the deadline is first crossed at ~400 bodies in the one battle cell, and the scene cannot be load-tested past 428 (`--members` clamps there).

**Predictions** (`predictions.md`, written before the decisive runs): #1 determinism held as predicted; #2 held in its main clause (saturated, growth not proportional to game time) but my < 10 MB / < 1 kB-per-game-s bound was violated at rtf ≤ 100 by the warm-up window itself; #3 held (flat in time); #4 half-held — frame_ms did NOT stay bounded (worst 17.5 ms crosses the deadline, because the *view*, not the engine, is unbudgeted) and overflow grew superlinearly but noisily; #5 failed in an informative direction: the tail is seed-stable (1.66), the median is not (3.15).

**What would make it wrong.** The cliff location (≈400 bodies) is a cloud-box, single-seed number with a load-1→4 machine: the M1 re-measure can move it by the ~30% thermal swing or more; the *attribution* (gather > engine at every load) and the *saturation* (0 kB drift) are robust to that. RSS is an upper bound on live state (allocator retention included); an owned-bytes counter could shrink the plateau but not create a slope. If a future scene with farther emitters (deeper cones) or member recycling keeps tubes alive past the cone, the flat-in-time claim must be re-measured, not assumed.

**Cost of adoption, fit with PRINCIPLES.** Adoption is a work order, not code: the readout (`b-readout-cost`) needs the same skin the engine has — a budgeted, deferrable gather — before any fleet scene grows past a few hundred bodies per cell; and load rungs need ≥ 8 seeds (the median moves 3×, single-seed sweeps mislead; the N=124 point proves it). This is #12 ("the algorithm that allocates the work to prevent those heavy tails, it's the most important one") and #34's guard logic applied to the view: the gather is today the unbounded synchronous claim that "Bounded work per presented frame" (plane.md §7, "nothing takes an unbounded synchronous claim on the frame, whether a cycle compile, a rebinding burst, a decompression or **a gather**") names — the promise is written, the shipped plane does not yet keep it. Via negativa: no engine work is warranted by these numbers; the engine budget holds.

**The lens.** Taleb prizes ruin and made me spend the run proving what does *not* break (determinism, history growth) — real but expected results that cap the grade at silver. It nearly missed the constructive reading: the stable tail across seeds with a fat-tailed *median* is good news (the budget clips extremes; the allocator problem is average-load placement, exactly Julien's p95/99 concern inverted), and the gather fix is an add, not a delete, which the lens is biased against. It also says nothing about *how* to bound the gather; the right representation (screen-space, cone-skinned readout) is another lens's work.

**Self-grade: silver.** Reproduced controls, one named experiment from gold: the same probe on the M1 (thermal state recorded) plus a many-seed load ladder at 300–500 bodies, fixing the cliff's location and the gather share well enough for the integrator to order the readout skin.
