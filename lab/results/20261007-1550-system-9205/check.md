**Abstract.** The claim — window-gated kicks hold belt behaviours at 6–9 kicks/orbit, fixed cadences diverge at deep conjunctions, sparse carriers explode, so cadence/skin/secular are one decision — is not backed by the evidence: the replica's Kepler layer is broken. `init()` and `drift()` build tangential speed `(1+e·cosν)·h/r²` where Kepler needs `h/r` (factor `(h/r)²≈0.44`, matching measured 0.29 vs 0.66). The "a=2.35, e=0.05 belt body" actually sits at a≈1.27, e≈0.80 (the module's own `elems`); the hard scene at e≈0.82–0.96, perihelia 0.08–0.29. `drift(0)` changes v by 0.28, so the "exact Kepler drift" is not exact; after 10 orbits the map and RK4-on-the-same-state differ by 10.6. Zero-kick runs reproduce the truth's Δϖ to ~1% per seed: the measured "secular exchange" is a drift artifact, not planetary. The ladder question is on the board's focus and worth a rerun on a validated replica.
