# Dossier — inversion-7655: the k=4 train certificate is not packing-safe; the watch width must be the tube count (a cell-local number), and concentrated fleets route to contact compute

## Claim (one sentence)
The adopted gold's phase-neighbour certificate (`20261006-0346-taleb-09b5`: "every real encounter sits within distance-rank 4 … a phase-neighbour certificate at O(n_c·k) replaces the quadratic tier") is blind to packing — the number of ships inside one encounter tube — and breaks as soon as packing exceeds what its evidence covered: at packing Π = 2 ships per ρ of arc the k=4 watch already misses 0.3% of real encounters, at Π = 4 it misses 2.1–2.8%, at Π = 8 it misses 13% (r_max 18), while the board's own target scene (a 10⁶-ship fleet in a 1° arc) sits at Π ≈ 6,000, where k=4 sees 4 of ~6,000 concurrent tube contacts and no pairwise watch at k(Π)≈2Π survives either — so the certificate needs its watch width set by the cell's tube count (the same dimensionless number is the regime trigger to contact compute).

## Bottleneck
The board's top scaling risk (candidate work must track real encounters; "Scale: encounters at N — the co-orbital certificate", HANDOFF §Headed-next 5) and the "Risks to attack first" line: "co-orbital concentration (fleets) makes the pairwise tier miss contacts". The gold replaced the quadratic tier for concentrated workloads; this run shows the replacement's own soundness precondition was never stated or measured, and the game's flagship fleet scene violates it by three orders of magnitude. Gain: not a speedup — a correctness hole closed before the certificate is built into the encounters crate (it is deleted-candidate code today, `encounters` used by nothing, so adoption is a spec change, not a migration).

2. **k=4 dies between Π = 0.8 and Π = 2** (first misses), and the miss fraction is a function of Π alone: stable across seeds (2.8% vs 2.1%) and across N at fixed Π (2.8% at N=240 and N=480; r_p99 identical at 6). r_max is not a small-N artifact.
3. **The corrective law is measured**: r_max ≈ 2Π + 4 (4, 8, 10–12, 18 at Π = 0.8, 2, 4, 8), r_p99 ≈ 1.5Π (2, 3, 6, 12), and r_max ≤ multiplicity − 1 held at every scene (r_max 18 < mult_max 30 at Π=8; r_max 4 < mult 10 at Π=0.8): the partner's rank is bounded by the count of ships inside the encounter tube. At Π ≳ 2 the close encounter is no longer "one exact pair event" — the pair-event layer needs a many-body/contact regime, triggered by the same tube count.
6. **Fermi for the flagship scene** (arithmetic, no run): 10⁶ ships in a 1° arc at 1 AU = 173ρ of arc → Π ≈ 5,800 ships per tube. k=4 watches ≤ 4 of them (≤ 0.07% of concurrent tube contacts); k(Π) ≈ 2Π needs ~1.2×10¹⁰ watch entries (≈ 12,000/ship — linear but useless at game scale). Above Π ~ O(10) no pairwise representation survives: concentrated fleets are contact compute, which AGENTS already reserves as "a separate, on-demand structure".

## Predictions
`predictions.md` was written before any run. Held: P1, P2 (gold reproduces at its packing), P3 (first misses at Π=2, 0.33% ∈ [0.1%, 5%]), P6 (e-spread harmless — against the gold's own stated worry), P7 (r_max ≈ 2Π + 4; multiplicity grows with Π; pair premise dissolves). Failed, both in the same direction (I overestimated miss fractions): P4 predicted ≥ 10% at Π=4, measured 2.8%; P5 predicted ≥ 40% at Π=8, measured 13.2%. Cause: rank inflation needs crowding *fluctuations* beyond the mean tube count; the median partner stays at rank ≈ 1.5Π. The certificate kill does not rest on these fractions — it rests on r_max > 4 at all (a certificate with misses is dead by the gold's own zero-miss standard), which held everywhere predicted.

## What would make it wrong
My kill scenes are co-planar near-circular arcs — the worst case for along-track crowding, and exactly the "fleet on one orbit" the gold targets; radial/e-spread at the same packing lowers ranks (P6), so Π overestimates risk for thick formations — the precondition should use the 3D tube count, not the 1D packing (my data supports this: r_max ≤ mult − 1). The tube count itself fluctuates; a static k set from the mean count can miss on tail clumping (r_max 18 vs mean-tube ~Π=8 → margin ~2× + rebuild-on-fluctuation is needed; untested). r_max ≈ 2Π + 4 is a fit over Π ∈ [0.8, 8] at one a-spread and arc shape; the +4 tail term was not decomposed (likely binomial clumping, ~log N). The route-to-contact-compute threshold (Π ~ O(10)) is asserted from the multiplicity curve, not measured against a contact representation — I did not build one. The trigger-blindness claim rests on reading the gold's dossier, not executing their trigger code.

## Cost of adoption, fit with PRINCIPLES
One spec change in the encounters design before it is built: (i) the train certificate carries a stated precondition — watch width = the cell's ρ-tube count (one integer, cheap, dimensionless), not a universal k=4; (ii) the same count is the second regime trigger: tube count O(1) → train; O(10⁺) → contact compute, never a pairwise watch. No code on main depends on it (`encounters` is a deletion candidate). Fit: "regimes are chosen by dimensionless numbers" (#4) — the gold chose one number (element clump) where the regime needs two; "A fix signals the wrong representation" (#19) — k=4 was the wrong constant because the representation's invariant was unstated; "An ant cares about a raindrop…" (#33) — the tube count is the scale's own resolution of what matters; "Compute only surprises" (#3) — a watch that misses 13% of surprises at lab packing is not a certificate. Inversion (Munger): the gold asked how the quadratic tier fails; asking how the *replacement* fails found the hole in one run.
