# Predictions (written before the decisive experiment)

The lever: thread the prior tube's found impact through `rebind` into `next_impact` as a
certified guess (`inside(g) && !inside(g-1)` accepts in two readouts; otherwise the guess
narrows the bracket exactly as one bisection step would). Env-gated `ES_IMPACT_GUESS=1`,
default off = shipped. The 20261014-0450-quant-1845 run priced a warm-start bracket at
−45%/kick, −10%/hand-out on the battle arm m4000/s3 without building it.

Cases not tuned on (the arms are quant-1845's; the held-out seeds and shapes are mine):

1. **Identity (load-bearing).** Canonical digest at the cut and kick-bench replay digests are
   bit-identical on/off at every arm, including held-out seed 7 and the flying pace m400/rtf100.
   The guess is certified by the same predicate on the same monotone branch, so any digest that
   moves means the change is wrong: kill.
2. **Kick cost at the battle arm (m4000/s3, seed 3, rtf 1).** `kick_us` median falls 30–50%
   (quant-1845's price −45%); leaver kicks (hand-out ships' own later kicks) fall by the same
   order. If the median moves <10%, the built lever fails its own price: kill.
3. **Hit rate.** ≥60% of `next_impact` calls that carry a prior settle by the two-readout
   certificate at m4000/s3 (counter `impact guess`). If most calls fall back to the bisection,
   the price was an artifact of their probe's bracket, not of the real crossing drift.
4. **Held-out seed 7, m1500/s3.** Kick median falls 25–50%, digests identical.
5. **At 48 members (the default scene).** Few dipping ships: the made kicks (the player's own
   tube does not dip) barely move; the search counter is dominated by leavers. Small or no
   signal is expected and is not a failure of the lever.
6. **Frame level.** The bench's engine p50/p99 at m4000/s8/rtf1 moves −5…−15% at most: the
   bisection is 45% of the *kick*, the kick is a fraction of D, and the burst is bind-dominated.
   Hand-out items themselves (newborn ships have no prior) do not improve; their ships' later
   kicks do.
7. **Suite.** `cargo test --release -p emergent-universe` green with `ES_IMPACT_GUESS=1` forced
   on (the identity claim under the tests' own scenes).

Kill rule: any digest that moves, or <10% kick-median movement at m4000/s3, kills the built
lever (the configuration, not the method: the prior run's price stands as a price).
