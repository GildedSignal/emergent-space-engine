## Claim

The scan pin's fix ("answer-or-expire", named by acf2 hours before this run) is a
trigger-and-expiry change, not a cost change: composed from measured constants,
answering every mint at arrival costs 0.34% of the frame budget at the fix's own
10⁶ target, expiry-on-supersede bounds the pin load-independently at ~2 entries
(426–448 MB, 18× under the wall), the shipped 2048-count cap is the spike
generator and dies for one byte counter, and at 10⁹ the pin's multiplier is the
doll's D (≲1.8×10⁷), never the queue policy.

## Bottleneck

Board #1 (bodies: 28 → 10⁹). The ladder's standing #1 term: acf2 measured the
held-scan pin as the wall that "stands ALONE between the capture walk (~6×10⁴)
and everything else", 8 GB at 687 game-s at the fix's own 10⁶ target (~7 wall-s
at rtf 100), ~1 game-s at 10⁹.

- Expected gain: the pin deletes as a wall — from unbounded (t_8GB = 8e9/(λ·224·(K+38)))
  to ~2 entries steady: ≥ 3 decades more game time at 10⁶, and the memory term
  becomes load-independent (b8e2's µ/λ knob can no longer grow it).
- The p99 spike deletes: one forced latch answer = 19–44 ms = 158–364% of the
  12-ms frame → 51.5 µs amortized in the margin (369×).
- What it deletes: the 2048-count cap, the latch's forced-answer mode, and the
  pin as an N-ladder term.

## What would make it wrong

- The compare-form's 0.5 ns is acf2's native bench, not the shipped answer path;
  if it does not survive there, the trigger half needs the store-grounded form
  (0.44% — still viable) or idle-window answers (P17).
- The 2-entry bound assumes supersede (replacement); if head-count flips (9% of
  mints) stack distinct questions in battle, it is a design target, not a law —
  lesson 5's gate exists to falsify exactly this.
- If the player's view contract needs an answer per minted question rather than
  per live question, expiry costs view freshness; the census's 91% t-trigger is
  the evidence the re-ask covers it, but no view-page bar was measured here.
- All arithmetic is Linux/x86 at rtf 100; the integrator re-measures on the M1.

## Cost of adopting

Small: one byte counter at capture (b77d-1fcf already specifies it), the latch's

own triggers. No law change — b8e2 measured the digest independent of when scans
are answered. Two flights are owed before freeze (the 2-entry arm; the loaded
crossover).
