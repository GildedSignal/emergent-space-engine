## Bottleneck
The board's adopted encounters design (6 October gold). The gold replaced the quadratic pairwise tier with a λ-ring train: "sort by longitude, watch k≈4 neighbours, rebuild on overtakes — a Diophantine event like the pair's", triggered by the Binet-clump number. The gold's deepest probe (σ_λ = 0.01, n_c = 200, spacing ≈ 1.25ρ, max rank 4) stopped at the boundary of (i); neither invariant is measured by the trigger.

## What would make it wrong
The maintenance kill rests on overtakes counted at 4096 samples/orbit (Kendall distance, exact given the samples; relative λ-motion per sample ≪ spacing in these scenes) and on the reading that "rebuild on overtakes" is the specified maintenance — if a redesign keeps a frozen ring (mean longitude) with a proven pad instead, the Θ(n²) applies only to the pad: k must then cover wiggle/spacing, which is Θ(n_c) in the same regimes (wiggle 4e-3 vs spacing 1.25e-4 in the gold's own control scene), so the cost re-enters as watch width; that alternative is argued, not measured. The rank survival is single-seed per scene, one orbit, a-spread exactly 0 (generous: no drift), n ≤ 512 — it bounds my attack, not the claim.
