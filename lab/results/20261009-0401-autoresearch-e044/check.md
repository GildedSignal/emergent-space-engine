Grade: silver

**Abstract.** Claim: `Orbit::compile` sits ~3–4% above its bit-identical x86 floor; the only free exact win is native-digit divisions (~3%), plus a ~1 ms near-separatrix rebind cost. Reproduced: digests identical baseline vs patched on both banks (11806c4030d5dd5f / f8c456a28a52db9a) and on my 350-case adversarial bank (digest + error-set + knot totals equal); 50/50 crate tests; gain small and positive (tune −9.0%, heldout −5.2% under load 3.7 — same class as their 3%); 756-knot corner at 1.03 ms. Not reproduced: cos ≈ 33% of per-knot — I measure 40.7 ns/call × 3.99 calls/knot ≈ 18%, and like-for-like ablation gives −8.6%/knot. Gain: a guard (no micro lever exists) + 3% patch; adopt cost trivial. The 10× lever is elsewhere (rebind cadence near the separatrix).

**Reasons.**
1. **Reproduction.** Applied `work/patch.diff` (root `patch.diff` is byte-identical); built the bench by path. Patched digests match the dossier exactly on both banks. Baseline built by reverse-applying only the `fast_quad` hunks (keeping the accessor): digests identical to patched, knots_total identical (3327 tune / 5880 heldout). Medians: tune 20.7→18.8 µs, heldout 10.4→9.85 µs (core-pinned, shared runner). The author's 17.6/9.8 µs differ by load, not substance. `cargo test --release -p emergent-space`: 50/50 pass on the patched tree.
2. **Exactness beyond the author's banks.** My adversarial bank (350 cases): q at the weak cut ± 1 significand quantum (finer than the author's 2^40 steps), dense k sweeps at HALF+{1,2,3,0xFF,0x100,0x1234,0xFFFFF,0x1FFFFFF} across periapsis exponents 0–44, near-UNIT k, strong field, fresh xorshift grid. Digest `fa02433530985f76`, error hash `7d7a0471713aa531` (51 rejected keys), knots 68756 — all identical baseline vs patched. The `div64` guard (`n>>64 < d`, `d ≤ u64::MAX`) exactly matches `div_word`'s word-sized-quotient contract, software path kept as fallback.
3. **Held-out predictions.** predictions.md mtime 04:09:52 vs log.md 04:21:53 — pre-registered before the decisive runs, and P1's falsification (3% against ≥25%) is evidence of genuine pre-registration. P4 held (digest equality on held-out). Honest reporting of falsified predictions throughout.
4. **Attack that landed: the cos attribution.** The dossier's "cos ≈ 33% of per-knot (842→567 ns)" did not survive independent measurement. Direct microbench: 40.7 ns/call; instrumented count: 3.99 calls/knot; compile: 879 ns/knot → cos ≈ 18% of per-knot. Like-for-like ablation (their method, loop stubbed, entry/exit kept): total −29% but knots fell 3327→2567, so per-knot on totals 899→822 ns = −8.6%. Their log records no ablated knot total, so the 842→567 derivation is not inspectable and likely conflates ladder redistribution with node cost. Direction survives (largest serial chunk, no bit-identical scalar lever, SIMD is the only exact rewrite), but the number is ~2× overstated.
5. **Harness hole (closed, not exploited).** The author's fingerprint only hashes `Ok` compiles; an Ok↔Err flip between builds would be invisible. My error-set hash shows no flips — but the harness should hash errors.
6. **The ms-class corner is broader than claimed.** k=HALF+1 at exp 3 reproduced (756 knots, 1.03 ms), but my sweep shows 0.70–0.88 ms across many near-separatrix keys and exponents — a class, not a corner. This strengthens the cost-concentration point and matters for the plane, but the run never measures how often such rebinds fire in a real flyby population (the B13 pointer is circumstantial).
7. **Novelty.** docs/HISTORY.md through H0215 and the board contain no prior measurement of the rebind compiler's bit-identical floor; the adjacent results (memo re-timing, 5 October; cadence queue) are cited, not redone.
8. **Goal fit.** Sharpens board focus line 1 on the cost side; the negative result redirects effort to cadence, which is where the board already points. PRINCIPLES: "The cheapest compute is compute not done" (the patch deletes software-division work); "Measure before claiming" (the loop measured that the expected 2–10× does not exist). Not gold: no 10× move, no deletion, and one load-bearing number failed reproduction.

**The one experiment for gold:** instrument the plane's B13 flyby population to count near-separatrix rebinds (k − HALF small) per orbit and their total ms. If ms-class rebinds are frequent, a certified-cheap rebind (lazy or amortized near the separatrix) is the real 10× and this run becomes its measured foundation; if they are rare, the compile-cost direction is closed and the grade settles at lead-for-adoption. Correct the cos share (~18%, not 33%) in any adoption note.

**What the author missed.** The digest's Err blindness; the ablation's knot-count confound (no ablated knot totals in the log, so the 33% is under-determined); that the ms corner is a broad class; any measurement of real-world rebind frequency; and a `taskset` pin is no control on a loaded shared runner — interleaved A/B on min-of-totals would have tightened the 3% claim their single-run medians cannot carry alone.
