## What the author missed

- **The margin floor**: SCAN_MARGIN_NS = 750 µs means nothing is ever answered once room < 1.5 ms, at any S (my b = 1 ms arm: answers = 0). The stated law "S > b/2" holds only for b ≳ 1.5 ms.
- **The latch holds the worst answer, not the mean**: the pilot's flush answers 115 scans in 0.242 s (steady S ≈ 2.1 ms) while the 4.2 ms cold first answer is what latched `scan_ns`. N_freeze computed from mean s understates the freeze side; the criterion should use max-S over the flight.
- **S is run-fuzzy at fixed N+seed** (2.97/4.20/3.35 across three runs): "seed-fuzzy" understates it; any single-run band carries ≈ ±1.5×.
- **"B = 0, every scan answered in its own frame"**: serving arms transiently hold 1–4 (a frame's mint before its drain); 0 at frame boundaries after service.
