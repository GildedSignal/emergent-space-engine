# Predictions (written before the staggered arms; baselines are measurements, not tuning)

Component: the locals' storm — the frame's binding N-independent term on the nladder page
(open direction 1: "the same one-step first-kick spread (secondary dolls' clocks) flattens
them; one law, one lever").

Premise from the record (917c, this branch's family): pre-wave max 334 events/frame at every
N flown; N=0 without locals peaks at 51. My reading of shipped code adds a mechanism the
conjecture did not have: doll!=0 ships step on Aarseth–Makino block steps, a GLOBAL dyadic
grid anchored at tick 0 (`step::aligned`: every power-of-two interval starts on a multiple of
its own length, "every pair shares boundaries"). The belt's cure (ed3a) staggered the first
clock begin of ext-clock bodies, whose next kick is planned at its own tick + half (no grid);
the locals' kicks snap back to the shared grid by construction.

## Arms (budget-free, rtf 1000, coast, seed 1)

- OFF (shipped, gate unset): must equal pristine main digest at canonical cuts (gate-neutral
  control).
- BEGIN (`ES_STAGGER_LOC=1`): the letter of the conjecture — stagger each doll!=0 ship's
  first plan begin by hash(id) mod 4096 at genesis, nothing else.
- GRID (`ES_STAGGER_LOC=2`): the grid-translate law — each doll!=0 ship's block-step grid is
  translated by a per-body offset o = hash(id) mod step (dyadic, deterministic in (id, step);
  re-phased on rebind, spread uniform across the population).

## Predictions

- P0 (baseline, from the record): locals arms (extra 600 = 300 locals; extra 10^4/10^5)
  show a recurring storm of ~300-380 events/frame, N-independent, at a fixed period; the
  pure-plane N=0 peak is far lower (~50).
- P1 (BEGIN): the FIRST storm flattens, but the shared grid re-locks the population within
  one step period: the standing train returns, max events/frame recovering to >= 60% of the
  OFF arm's within two step periods. The belt's cure does NOT transplant by the letter; the
  lock is the grid, not the begin.
- P2 (GRID): the standing train flattens: pre-wave max events/frame <= the N=0-class
  baseline plus the stream (<= ~120 at these N), i.e. >= 2.5x cut, with total kicks/events
  within ~2% of OFF (same cadence, phase-spread only) and no refusal/dead-body change.
- P3: OFF digest identical to a pristine-main build at the same canonical cuts (the patch is
  inert unset); BEGIN and GRID digests differ (a schedule law change at genesis, expected and
  stated, as with ed3a's stagger).

## Kill criteria

- No recurring >= 200-event storm in the OFF locals arms on this tree: premise fails, VOID,
  diagnose what fires instead.
- BEGIN flattens the standing train: P1 wrong, the conjecture holds as written (report held).
- GRID leaves max > 150: the storm is not grid phase-lock; diagnose the firing class before
  any adoption claim.
