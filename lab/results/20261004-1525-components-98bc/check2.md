The tests that actually defend the claim — cross-column joint χ², serial tests, conjunction-pair counts — were absent. - The lattice negative control undersells its own failure (perfect p↔ω correlation from the duplicated generator, zero conjunction pairs); a future adversary could have found these and used them to question the battery's sensitivity.
