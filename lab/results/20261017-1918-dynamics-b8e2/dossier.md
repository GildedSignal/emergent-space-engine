**Claim.** The plane's FLOW growth is a single-server queue past its stability point — arrivals λ ≈ 0.053–0.064 held scans/game-s are game-lawful (member- and seed-independent), service exists only when the frame's wall budget leaves the drain's 1.5 ms margin, so any of {load, the player's own pace (rtf), a smaller engine-ms} flips ρ past 1 and the queue drifts linearly at λ·pin(m) — and the `SCAN_BACKLOG` count-latch, never before observed engaged, is a genuine bounded attractor: the queue pins at the cap, answers continue inside the frame budget, live memory plateaus (never ruins), and the game pays in presented time; the cap's memory price is `cap × pin(m)` with pin(m) ≈ 87/214/313 kB at m = 100/200/400 (≈ m^0.9), so the shipped 2048-cap plateaus at ≈ 0.64 GB at 400 members — the fence must be a byte budget, not a count.

**Bottleneck.** `memory-h-live`'s standing conflict (inversion-4b8c "wall-owned cleanup, pin not ∝ N" vs library-7bd9 "held-scans accumulator, 171 kB/scan, latch not safe at scale"), and the page's own gap: "the cap was never observed engaged". Both mechanisms are one queue; this run measures its law end to end and resolves the conflict.
