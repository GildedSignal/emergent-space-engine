Reproduced bit-for-bit: all four singles, `scenep`/`scener`, and the 192-body mixed (rulePA 192/192 at 1.03x, rulePB 0.65x vs robust fixed 8). ## What the author missed

- The mixed scene's kill criterion was decided by construction: from its own ladder (fixed4 37.5%, fixed8 100%), even an oracle per-body rule could cut at most ~1.28x. The 1.03x headline measures the scene (e-only spread creates almost no need spread), not only the rules; the real kill evidence is the singles' transfer failures and over-spend. The dossier even says "the 2–4x a per-body rule could have won" — in that scene the winnable margin was ~1.3x.
- "Cuts nothing" is uniform-a scoping. In the radially spread member of the same defined family the best rule transfers and buys 1.67x (still <2x). The correct summary is "under 2x everywhere measured, up to 1.67x in spread belts", which is what q-cadence needs to know.
- Robust minima are horizon-specific (8 -> 4 at 200 orbits on h2); the evaluation warning should include horizon sampling, or a plane will mistake a transient pass for a robust one in the other direction too.
- P8 is a failed prediction not named as failed (see above).
