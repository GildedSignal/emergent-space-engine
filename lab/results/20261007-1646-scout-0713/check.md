## What the author missed

- **Horizon dependence:** the robust fixed minimum moves with run length (h2: 8 at 48 orbits, 4 at 96). "Sample several N" should read "several N *and several horizons*"; a sweep at one T can mis-rank rules.
- **The harness's own boundary was unmeasured:** at e=0.9 the RK4 truth's varpi slope is off 39% (2048 vs 8192 steps/orbit). Convergence was checked on cal only; each scene's own truth convergence should be reported alongside its verdicts.
- **The calibration claim overstates precision** ("largest threshold where both rules hold" — actually the largest tested; true boundary ≤ 2x higher).
- **Prior-art citation gap:** the board's own Calvo–Sanz-Serna lead is the closest recorded neighbour and goes uncited.
- **The e=0 budget is loose** (dq/de only; varpi undefined), so the "30x" end of the over-spend range is measured against the weakest budget. The kill does not rest on it — the mixed scene carries the full budget and gives 1.03x.
- The claim that no orbit-local magnitude bound can see the budget is proven for three families plus structure (non-monotonicity); a bound keyed on the perturbation's Fourier content rather than its magnitude is conceivable — which is precisely the dossier's own redirection, but the sentence could be read as stronger than what was measured.
