**Abstract.** Claim: the scar layer charges ~130 bits/body for common-mode secular drift, so programs-plus-scars cannot hold 10⁹ belts and the drift must be derived. Reproduction is bit-exact (all 29 evidence lines; encounter rates 0.00198/0.0203 per body-orbit exact; physics anchor correct), but the decisive number is an accounting artifact: `bytes()` read after `compact()` counts the cleared-but-unshrunk forwarding map (1,032,192 B = 126.0 of the 130.06 bits/body; it appears with zero drift). After the crate's own `tidy()` — the pattern its own examples use — costs are +1.0 (band-common, 1 h), +4.0 (2.2-s cliff), +21.2 bits/body (differential, 1000 h), below the author's own kill threshold (P3: ≥60 at ≤1000 h). The kill does not stand; nothing to adopt.

**Reasons.**
1. **Reproduced, exactly.** `cargo run --release` in `work/hlive`: every number in evidence.md matches bit for bit (17.9 s, <60 MB). Encounters `steady 1000 1e-4|1e-3 1 10 50`: hits 99/1015, rates 0.00198/0.0203 exact. Anchor verified: `crates/universe/src/scenario.rs:120` (Giant, 5.2 AU, mass_log2 −10); LL formula is the standard first-order result (Δϖ/orbit = (π/2)μα·b_{3/2}^{(1)}), GR 6.8×10⁻⁸ ✓.
2. **The flat 130 is the forwarding map, not the scar layer.** Probe (independent copy, `/tmp/a122-check`): update-all-bodies → `compact()` → breakdown shows `forward=1,032,192` B of the +1,065,472 B; with **zero** drift the same +126.0 bits/body appears. After `tidy()` (`shrink` + arena reclaim — the idle-time call the crate's own `examples/measure.rs:264-265` and `ladder.rs:337-339` use after every compact): band-common 1 h **+1.01** bits/body; the 2.29-wall-second cliff (every band ≥1 quantum) **+3.97** bits/body vs zero-drift; per-body ±5% rate jitter at 1000 h **+21.2** bits/body. The author's flatness across five decades of horizon and the "repack → 126.0" rows (scar layer empty, 126 remains) were the smoking gun, left unexamined; the lens paragraph guessed "row codec lacks a constant-column form" — wrong mechanism.
3. **The author's own criteria, honestly measured, pass.** P2 predicted ≤5 bits/body for band-common drift: measured +1.0 — P2 held. The pre-registered kill (P3 ≥60 bits at ≤1000 h) is not met at +21.2. 21 bits × 10⁹ ≈ 2.6 GB plus encounters ~1 GB fits 8 GB once the bijection replaces the 120-bit base (components-98bc). "Cannot hold 10⁹" is falsified at the measured horizons.
4. **Secondary errors.** Ratetable `cells` are negative (advance(2.0)−advance(3.5) sign flip) and `table_bits` collapses to `cells` (log2 clamp at 2) — direction unaffected (the bug understates the table's cost). Bits/scar is likewise inflated: 655 updates leave ≥5,895 B of map residue ≈ 72 of the 163.4 bits/scar (arithmetic from the measured breakdown; true ~85–90, same order). P5's "≤0.3 GB" holds only at ρ=1e-4; at ρ=1e-3, 0.0203 × 2.5 orbits × 163 bits ≈ 1.0 GB at 10⁹.
5. **Novelty.** The H_live measurement is new (no HISTORY entry measures scar growth under tide), but it builds on H0187 ("program plus scars", Opus) and gold components-98bc; the surviving recommendation — derive predictable precession from program state — already is the law's design (`docs/law.md:14,21`: the cycle memo carries ∂/∂E, ∂/∂L; positions derived, precession per cycle). It restates existing design rather than moving a bottleneck.
6. **Legitimate residue.** One real risk survives: during a seal pass in which every body passed through `update()`, the forwarding map transiently holds ~9–11 GB at 10⁹ — but that is a working set bounded by compaction cadence and region size (streamed per region), not a permanent per-body storage charge, and it argues for cadence, not for deleting the scar layer.

**What the author missed.** The crate's own `tidy()` (and its examples' compact→tidy pattern) was never called; `bytes()` counts working-map capacity, and the constant 126-bit offset — visible in the zero-event control had one been run post-compact — was the artifact. The "saturation at ~127 bits" pilot conclusion inherited the same flaw. The honest experiment is one line away: `sh.tidy()` before `sh.bytes()`, plus a zero-drift control per variant. Also missed: the transient working-set framing (§6 above), the ρ-qualification on P5, and the sign bug in the ratetable cells.
