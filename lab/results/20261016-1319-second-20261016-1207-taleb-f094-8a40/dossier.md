# The exodus holds under burn: the cap, the wave and the price survive, and the burn's own cadence becomes the opening's dearer owner on the same lever

## Claim

The second checker's named experiment — the one unflown composition, **the opening under burn**
(a player decision landing inside the drain, competing with the hand-outs for the budget while the
scan latch freezes) — is flown at the page's own arms (fleet 48 and 400, N = 0, seed 1, rtf 100,
12 ms; the served 5 ms at 400; levels 4 and 8; plus one decision landing exactly at the coast's
crest tick), with pre-registered magnitudes (`predictions.md`, written before the grid). **The
claim holds:** the presented-lag cap (never above one frame's advance: 1.433-1.448 game-s at 48,
1.666-1.667 at 400, both budgets), the wave's ownership (leaver totals **identical to coast** —
43/43 at 48, 361/361 at 400; opens = leavers) and the per-leaver hand-out price (555-637 us, the
measured band) all survive the burn. And the composition adds the boundary the record lacked: a
held level-8 burn makes **the player's own thrust cadence the opening's dominant cost at served
fleets** — 126.6 ms over f0-100 at fleet 48, **4.8x the entire 48-member hand-out bill** (26.4 ms),
the same order as the 400-member bill (130 ms vs 207 ms) — because every flush pays the same
`bind_surface` fit a hand-out pays (prof: ~195 us/flush, 499 extra fits at 48), and it freezes the
scan latch through the drain (backlog plateau ~145 scans at the served budget; encounters delayed,
order-preserving, never lost, far under the 2048 cap). The lever is unchanged: the promotion price
(`bind_surface`) that b29a/e0f9/760f own — the burn composes on it, it does not need a new one.

## Bottleneck

Board line 1's "which term breaks first as the plane grows": this closes birth-stagger's open
residue ("the opening under burn ... unmeasured", the checker2-named gold condition) and completes
the exodus attribution in its own domain. No smoothness term appears at 1e6 that the burn adds:
the exodus stays N-flat, one-time, budget-capped under burn; what the burn adds (its own cadence)
is player-owned, level-proportional (26.7 ms at L4, invisible against 26.1 ms of hand-outs) and on
the already-owned lever.

**The cap (prediction 1, held; kill criterion never fired).** Worst presented lag is one frame's
advance in every arm, burn or coast, 12 ms or 5 ms; at 48 members the burn's lag (1.433) is
marginally *below* coast (1.448) — the player's cadence interleaves with the drain, it does not
stack on it.

**The wave is the fleet's own (prediction 2, held exactly).** Leaver totals identical to the
same-binary coast at every fleet and budget (43/43; 361/361); at 400 one leaver resolves after
f100 under burn (356 vs 357 — resolution order, not a crossing), and the 5-ms arms still drain at
f120 (347 coast, 343 burn). Opens = leavers everywhere; the crest arm's 44th open is the player's
own tube re-opened by the flush (dec = 1). Mechanism: a level-8 burn moves the player's rapidity
by ~5e-4 over the opening — no member's tube is touched.

**The price is untouched (prediction 3, held).** Fleet-class us ÷ leavers on leaver frames:
555-637 us in every arm, inside the original's 485-686 band; the burn adds cost in the player
class only.

**The burn's own bill (prediction 4, held on the band).** At L8 the player's opening bill is
126.6 ms at 48 (4.8x the hand-out bill), 130.0 ms at 400 (0.63x of 207 ms) — the opening's owner
under burn at served fleets is the thrust cadence, not the hand-outs. Prof (shares; the prof
binary carries ~12% timer overhead): at 48, `bind_surface` fits rise 250 -> 542 calls (25.6 ->
122.2 ms) — the ~499 extra fits are the flushes, ~195 us each, the same stage the hand-out pays
(43 binds, ~430 us each); held-scan answers rise 24 -> 608 calls (4.7 -> 38.4 ms). At 400 the
scan answers are the dearer half: 577 calls / 380 ms (~660 us each — the denser Home doll), above
the player's fit bill (~171 ms). At L4 the player bill is 26.7 ms — just under the hand-out bill
at 48, invisible: the term is proportional to the throttle level.

**The latch (prediction 5, magnitude missed, kill criterion not fired).** At 12 ms the backlog
engages only inside the drain (54 peak at 400, 91 frames) and fully recovers (tail 0-2). At the
served 5 ms it plateaus at ~139-157 through the drain (peak 157, above my registered <100 band —
a miss), bounded ~10 KB a scan, never near the 2048 cap that would block the player's next item;
encounters are delayed, not lost (order-preserving; the log reads as-if-answered once empty —
69f3's one-way latch, here measured inside the opening composition).

**The crest decision (prediction 6: the flush held, the drain length missed).** A decision
landing at the coast's crest tick resolves inside the drain: the crest frame is unchanged (11.5
ms, lag 1.448), the teeth totals unchanged. My "drain +≤3 frames" missed by construction: the
*held key* starts the thrust cadence (drain 40 vs 3 — the burn arm's own 30 plus the crest), one
decision is not one flush. The flush itself is one ~0.5 ms rebind.

**The served budget (prediction 7, held).** 5 ms at 400 under burn: worst frame 5.1 ms (one item
over, no drop), drain frames 81 -> 115 of 120, lag capped — a wider, deeper rtf dip, still a dip.

## Predictions

`predictions.md` precedes the grid (one disclosed 12-frame smoke at L8/48 for the instrument
only). Held: the cap (1), the wave (2, exactly), the price (3), the burn bill band (4: 126.6 ms
inside 100-400, 4.8x inside 3-10x; L4 < 30 ms), the served budget (7). Missed: the latch's peak
band (<100; measured 157 at 5 ms — the plateau, not the peak, was the miss), and the crest arm's
drain length (+37, not +≤3: a held key is a cadence, my conflation). The kill criteria (lag >
1.667, leaver totals moved, price outside 300-800 us, backlog ≥ 2048 or growing at f150) never
fired. **The original run's record, completed as check2 required:** its prediction 1's
parenthetical leaver counts (~70 at 200, ~140 at 400) were 2.4x low (168/357 measured; the
proportionality anchored on its own 43@48 predicts 179/358 — the anchor was right, the
parentheticals wrong), and its "presented lag ≥ 5 game-s at 400" was wrong (the cap holds at
1.667); both are now named, not framed as held.

## What would make it wrong

- The arms are N = 0 and one seed at each fleet: a belt-N or held-out-seed burn arm could move
  the latch plateau (the scan answer is doll-density-dependent — 69f3/cdf0's regime question).
- Levels above 8 are the burst job's (gate T1, off this page): the cadence bill's growth with
  level is measured only at {0, 4, 8} (α-proportional between them, as the mechanism says).
- The player's own frame crossing is not composed (a burn strong enough to leave Home's doll
  inside the opening would add hand-off items): at L8 over 100 frames the rapidity moves ~5e-4,
  so this arm does not exist below L15-class burns.
- The M1's absolute rates; and the player's *decisions* during the drain are one crest landing —
  a keys-*changing* player (many decisions inside the drain) adds one flush a change, unmeasured.
