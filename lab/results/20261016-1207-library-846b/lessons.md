# Five lessons for the N ladder, from Julien's library (mined verbatim 2026-10-16)

1. **Question the requirement, then delete; the bar is quantitative.** "Step one should be to
   question the requirements… Make them less wrong and dumb… And then delete, delete, delete." /
   "You may have to add them back later. In fact, if you do not end up adding back at least 10% of
   them, then you didn't delete enough." — Isaacson, *Elon Musk*. Mechanism: the comb's requirement
   is law.md's own invariant (shared boundaries via the absolute anchor); deletion (step 5's secular
   kicks: zero events from that population) beats a paid spread (de-phase: +11–20% forever, stub
   mints). Change: fly the deletion first. Test: 10³-locals coast 6,000 game-s — zero events from
   that population AND apsidal precession inside B14 (r9 proved this can fail); fail → the de-phase.
2. **A different toolbox, not more force in the old one.** "I got a great reputation for doing
   integrals, only because my box of tools was different from everybody else's…" — Feynman,
   *Surely You're Joking*. Mechanism: every patch inside the absolute anchor is re-absorbed (the
   one-step spread, in one period — 4c22); the clock law, which chains from the previous event,
   kept its cure. Change: chain intervals from the body's own previous event (one law, retires the
   comb class at every scale) instead of ES_LOCAL_PHASE in the old anchor. Test: N=600/10³, 6,000
   game-s — max events/frame < 150 (comb re-forms → kill), events/game-s ≤ base +20%.
3. **Serve the view from the program: zero marginal cost of replication.** "Fortunes require
   leverage. … products with no marginal cost of replication (code and media)." — *Almanack of
   Naval*; "…assimilate large amounts of information into a cluster bound together by certain
   patterns…" — Waitzkin, *The Art of Learning*. Mechanism: the compiled orbit is the chunk (3dc8:
   113 B vs 18.8–25.3 kB of record); the record stack (1.0–1.3 TB at 10⁹) stands on one reader (the
   view's retired-row gather). Change: resolve retired eras by program readout (scout-1652's ~10×
   cut). Test: one 10⁵ scene, gather→program, served views digest-identical; kill on any visible
   difference or if the readout walks cost more than the reads they delete.
4. **Size by the process, never by an average; size hurts at stress.** "…size hurts you at times of
   stress; it is not a good idea to be large during difficult times." — Taleb, *Antifragile*.
   Mechanism: the burn's working set is the demotion process (0619: WS = demotions, integer-exact);
   a fixed cap degrades 1.24→8.50× with N and concentration moves the knee 30× (b172). Change:
   admission ∝ demotions per wave period, c frozen per scene. Test: burn 10⁵, process-sized vs
   fixed 16,384 at identical digest; kill if RSS or re-mints exceed 2× the fixed arm.
5. **Automation last: no new seam machinery before the requirement is questioned.** "Always wait
   until the end of designing a process—after you have questioned all requirements and deleted
   unnecessary parts—before you introduce automation." — Isaacson, *Elon Musk*. Mechanism: adopted
   seam machinery manages re-mints that un-questioned requirements mint (the anchor mints the comb;
   the view's reads mint the record). Change: freeze NEW seam machinery until 1–3 land; count what
   the representation change deletes. Test: ≥3 of {ES_LOCAL_STAGGER, ES_LOCAL_PHASE, the belt
   stagger's adoption arm, the retention re-mint path} deleted; zero kills the rule.
