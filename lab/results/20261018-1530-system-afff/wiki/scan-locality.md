## Refuted

- **"The scan is a no-surprise machine (≥ 90% empty answers), so an empty-answer
  certificate deletes the walk"** (this run's own P1): 46.9% empty at the shipped
  and ladder scenes, 12.6% at the crowd — the walk carries real surprise; only its
  *locality* is certifiable (20261018-1530-system-afff).
- **"The ladder's belt inflates the player's scan"**: the belt is doll 0 and never
  a candidate; the inflation is the locals (Home-doll, a quarter of the ladder)
  — the walk couples to the doll population, the answer does not (same run).
