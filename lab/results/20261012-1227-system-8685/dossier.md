# Dossier — run 20261012-1227-system-8685 (system lens)

## Claim

The plane's kick cadence (uniform 1/64 of the body's own period), the SyMBA shell
engagement and the encounter predictor are three expressions of one question — *when
does this pair interact?* — but they unify only **piecewise**: the interaction's clock
is regime-dependent (conjunction clock in impulsive regimes, orbit-averaged secular
term in smooth regimes, resonant-angle clock near commensurability), and a measured
float replica shows each regime punishes the wrong clock: conjunction windows hold
behaviour at ~1.5–1.6× fewer kicks where interactions are impulsive and beat
phase-blind sparsity by ~3.6×, but break smooth secular precession by up to 147% and
mis-time resonant locking entirely; an event-scheduled variable cadence does **not**
walk (Jacobi stays at the uniform law's own floor), while any between-window forcing
quantized at a fixed time-fraction of the orbit **aliases into artificial resonances**
(ė error 165 ensemble MADs, Jacobi 100× the law's floor).

## Bottleneck

Board bullet 1 ("fewer kicks per orbit at the same behaviour: kicks dominate the
engine's cost") and the top scaling risk. Expected gain: not a new 10× lever — the
slack skin (12.6×, gold components-4643) already owns the window machinery; this run
answers whether the *cadence* and the *skin* are the same lever (partially: yes, in
impulsive regimes) and names the two clocks that must stay separate. What it lets us
delete: the uniform 64-kicks-per-orbit floor for encounter-dominated bodies (the
fleet, flybys), and the per-kick window setup (92% of per-kick evals) — the window is
computed once per window, not once per kick.

## Evidence

Replica: 2D test particle + one circular planet, static star (μ=1); DKD
Wisdom–Holman split, exact Kepler drift, kicks differ only in schedule; SyMBA-style
proximity refinement (3 Hill radii, resolve d/v_rel by 8) for **all** variants alike,
so `u64` = the plane's cadence + its shells and `wW` = the window clock. 16 seeds per
cell, behaviours as ensemble medians (ϖ̇, ė linear fits over 500–1000 P_planet),
ejections, and per-seed Jacobi-constant drift. Truth = the same split at u2048;
convergence checked at u4096 (belt: 0.66 MADs; encounter: 0.59 MADs ϖ̇, ė 2.43 MADs —
seed noise, the ė signal sits at its own MAD there).

## Predictions

`predictions.md` was written before the decisive runs. Scored:

- **P1 (encounter): half holds.** w0.5 keeps ė 3.6× better than count-matched u8 and
  matches u16 at 64% of its count — but the honest factor is ~1.5–1.6×, not ≥8× vs
  u64 (u64's own deviation is 0; nothing matches 0).
- **P2 (resonant): FAILS.** Conjunction windows mis-time the resonant angle; near-truth
  needs W≈1.5–2.5 rad ≈ uniform. Held-out miss, reported as held.
- **P3 (smooth belt): the degradation half holds** (windows alone break ϖ̇ up to 147%),
  **the recovery half fails**: my seg8 stand-in for the averaged kick aliases (165
  MADs). The favourable-direction suspicion was also resolved: the W=0.25 pass is a
  cancellation accident, demonstrated by the sweep.
- **P4 holds:** no Jacobi walk under event-scheduled cadence.
- **Kill criterion:** not triggered (u8 ≠ w0.5 on the encounter config), so the clock
  matters — but the strong unification (one clock, all regimes) is dead.

## What would make it wrong

The replica is not the engine: a static star (no indirect term), a prescribed planet
(no reflex), float64 (not the integer law), one perturber (the many-source shrinkage —
4 planets × ~13 window kicks ≈ 52 vs 64 — is Fermi-only in `predictions.md`), and
window detection evaluated per step rather than predicted from elements (the real
cost the skin owns). Behaviour judged on 16 seeds × 3 regimes × rate fits: a law whose
error appears in higher moments than medians (libration spectra, tail ejections at
longer T) could reorder the impulsive-regime win. The truth's ē at the encounter
config is itself only 2.4 MADs converged (4-seed u4096 check) — margins smaller than
that are noise.

## Cost of adoption, and fit with PRINCIPLES

Adoption is **conditional, not wholesale**: key the kick cadence to the skin's
conjunction windows only for impulsive-regime bodies (the co-orbital certificate's
formations, flybys), keep the orbit-averaged term for stable orbits **orbit-anchored**
(never time-quantized), and leave resonant pairs to the rotating doll's own
representation (`docs/law.md`). Cost: the scheduler must consume window predictions
(the `crates/encounters` machinery) instead of a fixed H — the same dependency the
skin already creates, so no new component. Deletions: the uniform 64/orbit floor for
encounter-dominated bodies; the per-kick window setup.

Principle 4, "Everything is emergent. No central control and no categories: regimes
are chosen by dimensionless numbers" — the clocks are exactly that: a regime number
(roughly m-meso-motion's U/√(μ_p/b), the interaction's impulsive-ness) chooses the
conjunction clock, the secular term or the resonant angle. Principle 19, "a fix
signals the wrong representation" — the uniform cadence is a fix for not asking when
the interaction happens; but the piecewise outcome is itself the finding: there is no
single interaction clock, and pretending otherwise (my strong form) is a bandaid.
Principle 14, "no work for no reason, so no ticks" — the aliasing kill is that
principle observed numerically: a time-quantized average is a tick, and it
manufactures a resonance that does not exist.

## The lens

The whole-system lens looked for mechanisms that are secretly one and found three
that are only *partly* one; what it may have missed by staring at clocks: the
readout/jet side of the per-kick cluster (the memo and rebind, b-readout-cost's
territory), and whether the integer law's sigma-delta carries change the aliasing
conclusion (integer kicks quantized per orbit-fraction could alias differently than
floats). The unification's hard special case was named in advance (the smooth secular
regime) and it did break; the second hard case (resonance) broke beyond the named
one. One lens-shaped confirmation worth keeping: the e/ϖ split (conjunction-local
versus averaged) maps onto the engine's existing division — `crates/encounters` owns
conjunctions, the secular layer owns precession — suggesting the two components were
never one mechanism to merge, but two clocks the dimensionless number must choose
between.
