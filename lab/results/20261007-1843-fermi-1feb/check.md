**What the author missed.**
- The slice conflation (3c): `events()` mixes per-frame battle slices with per-body orbital events; the hot bracket at 10⁹ is ~2× overstated. The author's lens section names burstiness but not this scaling error.
- The hot rate is a window average over a decaying battle (1500–2000 game-s). Decomposing m400 minus m0, the per-member kick rate inside the battle is only ~0.005/game-s — at 10⁹ the compute is set by the orbital belt rate times the hot fraction, which *strengthens* the compute-first ranking; the presented number is a window mean, not a battle rate.
- One seed per configuration: seed scatter (1.4–1.7×) is the larger uncertainty, and only members were varied.
- `bytes_per_body` varies 4809–6317 across scenes/runs (wall-end live bodies); the dossier quotes one value (5067). Order-fine, but it is a moving field, not a constant.
- The rtf pacing-quality numbers (lowest 1 % 58, 21 overflows) are shared-box load artifacts, not engine properties; a quiet rerun gives ≈100 and 5 with an identical digest.
