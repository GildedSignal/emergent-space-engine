## What would make it wrong

- One fire in 14 draws is a fat-tail point estimate; the page's own protocol warns incidence
  lives in bands and windows. More draws (windows, rates, longer horizons — my 4.5 s covers
  present 3.5k–10.8k chronons at rate 2400, not the rate-600 long haul) could dissolve or
  multiply it. The honest statement is "reachable, race-decided", not a rate.
- `speed_margin 0.5` is not the shipped default (2.0); at 2.0 nothing fired (0/6, and all
  gas conflicts absorbed). If the engine keeps 2.0 forever, the game-shaped exposure is
  latent, not live — but 2.0 buys finality lead with 2× the promise growth, taxing exactly
  the ahead-publishing the plane needs; the tests themselves run at 0.0.
- The scene is `worlds::galaxy`, a fleet generator, not the plane's flown battle; the claim
  transfers to the game by shape (converging fleets with fling), not by identity.
