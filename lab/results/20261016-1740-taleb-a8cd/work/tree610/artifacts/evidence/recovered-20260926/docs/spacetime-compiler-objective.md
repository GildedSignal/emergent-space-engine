# The objective: compile a living spacetime

This briefing consolidates the user's September 12 direction for the GLM architecture investigation. It is an interpretation to check against the supplied discussions, not a newly accepted physical law or a replacement for frozen qualification gates. ## What success means

Build a living, causally consistent universe with roughly one billion individually addressable ships and ten billion simulated years on the user's M1, with a smooth observatory. Matter, light, clocks, interactions and history must belong to one coherent spacetime computation. The intended scale comes from exploiting shared structure and reusing completed physical work. It cannot be established by a frozen inertial population alone. Logical population, independent activity, elapsed time and retained history are separate dimensions that eventually need a combined workload.

Gravity is intrinsic to this design. Sources and their history determine a shared geometric description; matter and light propagate through it; clocks measure proper time from it; changed matter feeds back into later geometry. Computation should specialize and reuse this relationship wherever its assumptions remain valid. Merely putting separate force, clock and light formulas in one struct does not establish physical consistency or computational reuse.

The intended central chain is:

**Laws + seed + interventions → source/field history → shared spacetime geometry → reusable propagation/region responses → worldlines, light, clocks and interactions → certified consequences → causal repair and recompression.**

Feedback connects later events in this chain. A change to A may affect a later B and then a still later A. Causal ordering is useful computational structure; it does not imply feedback vanishes, contracts, or costs nothing. Initial and incoming fields, source conservation and boundary constraints must be represented by the selected law.

## How the existing ideas fit together

| Idea from the discussions | Role in the same computation | Obligation before claiming success |
|---|---|---|
| Worldlines and indexed families | Store shared generators while preserving every identity | Interventions, contacts and decoding preserve canonical outcomes |
| Finite causal envelopes and compile islands | Bound possible dependence over a finite horizon; schedule and group work | Include pre-existing fields and incoming/exterior influence; geometric overlap alone does not prove independence |
| Hyperedges / causal intermediate representation | Express many-source geometry construction and many-consumer propagation without expanding every pair | An operation must eliminate or share actual work; one range opcode is only an initial instance |
| Partial evaluation and shared geometry | Specialize propagation to source configurations and reuse across distinct consumers | Derive consistent observables within the admitted law and charge compilation, application and verification |
| Regional boundary responses | Replace an interior calculation with sufficient incoming-to-outgoing behavior | Preserve future-relevant state, field/history, interior events and reconstruction obligations; boundaries need not be small |
| Certificates and causal repair | Keep valid work; refine uncertainty and update affected dependencies | Bounds need independent checks and must retain accumulated effects, not merely current visible event labels |
| Regions at different resolved times | Resolve a dependency before a consequence needs it, without a universal tick frontier | No missing incoming cause; observation changes scheduling, never truth |
| History sealing and demand queries | Reuse completed consequences and reconstruct detail when needed | No omitted future influence; real storage/reconstruction costs included |

These are intended roles, not a demand to implement every technique simultaneously. One causal representation can support several mathematical operations. Min-plus can fit arrival/value problems; ordinary field arithmetic, nonlinear evolution and other constraints need their own valid algebra. FMM, Schur elimination, analytic propagators and piecewise geometry are candidates, not universally applicable solutions.

## What the foundation does and does not settle

The supplied research report already states the central architecture in its executive summary. The continued conversation develops finite causal horizons, gravity changes, hypergraphs, a spacetime region interface, partial evaluation and a slow reference interpreter. The problem is not an absence of vision in those documents.

They leave substantive questions open: the admitted self-consistent gravity law; what data makes a region response sufficient; how to bound errors through feedback; which source or trajectory structure is reusable; and when that representation saves more than it costs. The unification is an architectural hypothesis to make concrete and falsifiable, not a proof of billion-ship feasibility. Earlier assistant enthusiasm and broad independence/compression claims must be examined rather than inherited as facts.

The current research patch and inertial compiler are useful evidence and instruments. Neither must become the permanent architecture merely because it exists. In particular, exact preservation of a rounded laboratory map is required when claiming an optimization of that map; it is not a reason to declare that map the desired final physics. If it blocks the intended architecture, identify the conflict and propose a separately versioned alternative with explicit requirements and tests. Do not silently replace its law.

## The next decision

First explain, with source references, which links of this chain exist, which are only named, and which are absent. Trace one concrete example all the way through: two mutually influencing masses, several distinct ships, a light signal and a clock, followed by one controlled intervention. State what was compiled, what different consumers share, what changes, what remains reusable, and what future state must survive. Include the ship fleet's aggregate source contribution where admitted; do not make observation or object type decide whether mass exists.

Then select one cheap discriminating experiment for the most consequential missing link. A useful result combines physical consistency, semantics-preserving reuse and measured eliminated work. Separate identical-input memoization, shared geometry across distinct consumers, and skipping physical substeps over time. Full costs include construction, certification, application, invalidation, history, memory and fallbacks. A measured negative result that exposes a precise architectural conflict is useful; another broad abstraction or passing helper fixture alone is not the requested outcome.

G2 stays a final physical acceptance constraint. Its failure must remain visible, but the next red subtest must not substitute for the objective above. Preserve current instruments and frozen evidence. Do not claim production gravity, integrated scale or a speed win without matching evidence.
