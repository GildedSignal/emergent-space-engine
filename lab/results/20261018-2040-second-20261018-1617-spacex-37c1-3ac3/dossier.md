## What would make it wrong

1. **The oracle shares the fix's data**: for the 4 contaminated fires (τ* < −1, mid-batch
   velocity change) both sides back-project linearly with the current w; the physical
   truth is a kinked path the gate's integers do not contain. The 63/63 bit-identity
   there is a same-data comparison. Untouched fires (τ* ∈ (−1, 0)) have no such caveat —
   the hit predicate guarantees their crossing.
2. **The 2⁻²⁰ absorption is measured, not proven**: a proof would bound the exact
   rational targets' distance from the floor/ceil rounding boundaries. 8,489 fired pairs
   without one flip is strong evidence, not a theorem; a pathological near-boundary pair
   could still round differently.
3. **The firing rule's claim "Δ ≥ 0 ⟺ came within reach"** holds exactly for untouched
   pairs (the hit predicate implies it); for contaminated pairs Δ ≥ 0 is the linear
   back-projection's verdict, which may fire where the kinked path never touched. The
   measured contamination rate is the residue (4/63 at 400 members, 0 at 48).
4. The flight arms are 30 s of game on one seed each; longer horizons and more seeds are
   unbodied. The player-side contact path (doll-level, logged not resolved) remains
   unmeasured — where a real game would first meet a fast crosser.
