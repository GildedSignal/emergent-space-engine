## What would make it wrong

The grid is one seed, one scene, one nose (the unfixed retrograde nose on main), forward holds,
48 members, brackets at 0.2%: a crossing that lands at E−1 < 10⁻¹², a level-5 crash on another
seed, or a refusal at the handoff on other geometry would all cut it. The clock oracle is the
gate's own f64 readout formula — if that formula misreads the near-parabolic regime, so does C1.

## The lens

survives the family flip exactly, while the game loses the ship silently on both approach (surface
