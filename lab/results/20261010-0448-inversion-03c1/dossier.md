## What would make it wrong
The store arithmetic extrapolates the 600-ship admitted fraction and taleb-2e81's 72 B/section figure rather than measuring a 10⁶ fleet; if the engine stores Cross sections implicitly (the design says "candidates stay implicit: bodies sorted by U, filtered by the cone" — the rung's own ruin arithmetic assumed materialised sections), the residue is cheaper than 72 B/pair but still Θ(n²) to enumerate. The truth adjudicator shares `truth.rs` with the method's construction path (mitigated: train sweeps and truth use different propagators, and the build's tests cover `window_spans` against brute force). Soundness counts are small (≤5 encounters/scene). Whether real fleets sit at σ_e 10⁻⁴ (convoy, saved) or 10⁻² (post-combat, ruined) is a game-design fact I cannot measure.

## The lens
Inversion looked for what the adopted fix silently assumes and found the assumption is *time*: a fleet is a strict formation only until something disperses it, and the certificate's value decays with exactly the quantity (dispersion) the game's own content (combat, debris, burns) produces. What the lens may have missed: it prices the store but not the allocator's p99 under tier switching (efc9 flagged the same gap), it did not test the watch above e = 0.6 (declined there — untestable without forcing ζ), and a speed lens would have measured the 10⁶ case directly instead of extrapolating the admitted fraction.
