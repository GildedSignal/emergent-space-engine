## Attack (three adversarial cases, one beyond the author's range)

1. **K = 10⁵ (the next decade, unflown by the author):** the cost law extends *exactly* — in-band walks 42, cands = 99,715 = N−313, ns_cap p50 21.36 ms ⇒ 214 ns a body, in-band capture above the 12 ms budget, so at 10⁵ the seam owns over frames precisely as the dossier's scoping extrapolated ("crossed at ~6×10⁴"). But the memory story does not extend: the 1.5 GB guard fired at frame ≈ 21k, mid-hull (before the walk zone finished), with a pre/early-zone slope ≈ 5.8 MB per held scan (444→819 MB over held 3→68) and ~22× faster per frame than K=10⁴ — the "K-independent MB/held slope" is a 10⁴–3×10⁴ fact, and at 10⁵ memory breaks before the seam does. The dossier hedges this ("could be an artefact of both arms dying at the same guard"); the wiki page stated it more firmly and I have scoped it there.
2. **Coast control, whole flight** (the author's control was only the pre-burn frames of a burn flight): 290 scans, zero walks, player never leaves doll 2. The seam is the burn's, not the census's.
3. **Mechanism in code:** `radial.rs` confirms the story — registration is the hull of radial ranges in 16-bins-an-octave, retained history widened; the bail is `hit + unbounded > bodies/4 + 64` (a global threshold against summed bin-entries), so a window touching the hull's bins fires it. Two nits: the belt's generator caps apoapsis at 3·(1+0.4) = **4.2 AU**, not the dossier's "3/(1−0.4) = 5.0" (peri/apo slipped), so the zone's outer ~1 AU (4.2→5.17) is window reach, not registration — "the registration's width, not the window's, sets the zone" is right for the inner 3 AU, overstated for the last AU; and "~10–20 bins each" overstates the typical belt body (median e ≈ 0.07 ⇒ ~3–4 bins; only the re-drawn e → 0.4 tail spans ~19) — the bail needs no per-body bin count, the summed entries suffice.
