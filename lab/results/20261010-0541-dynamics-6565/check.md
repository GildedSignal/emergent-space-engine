## What the author missed

1. No positive control: an instrument calibrated only on Poisson cannot distinguish "no cascade" from "blind to cascades" — theirs was blind (truncation at short windows, baseline drowning at high density).
2. The τ=2 mislabel and the unnamed cap-bound τ=2 run — a pre-registered criterion was silently skipped rather than reported as VOID.
3. The quenching mechanism story ("restitution cools the participants") was the physical reading of an arithmetic offset; the corrected data shows a small *positive* partner excess, consistent with the bounce trains they had already measured at ≤1%.
4. Small overstatements: "by t=150" for the cap-truncated hot start (T=128.7); "60.6 Ω" and "T=150 orbits" are time units (an orbit is 2π tu), while post.py's λ column converts correctly — two time units in one dossier.
5. The unarchived canary number (1.097) — after October's lesson, every number the dossier quotes must be regenerable by a shipped command.
