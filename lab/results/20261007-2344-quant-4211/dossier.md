# Dossier — 20261007-2344-quant-4211 (quant lens)

**Claim.** The plane's deadline calendar is O(N) per frame in three places (rekey-all, the drain, defer-requeue) and caps the engine at ~3×10⁴ bodies at 60 FPS/rtf 100; a dyadic wheel on event time (exact, player-independent — the law's steps are already powers of two) plus a triangle-inequality skin on deadlines deletes the drain and the rekey sqrt, cutting the queue's frame cost 7–20× at 10⁶–3×10⁶ bodies with bit-identical canonical states.

**Bottleneck.** Board item "scale: encounters/candidate work at N" and bottleneck 4 (smoothness and the allocator): the queue is the frame's O(N) floor. `Universe::advance` calls `rekey` every frame (crates/universe/src/universe.rs:493: every entity's deadline recomputed — an f64 sqrt — and re-set on the 4-ary heap), then its drain peeks every entity, removes (set None) all with `t > target`, and requeues them after the loop (another sqrt + insert). Measured at 10⁶: 2.2 heap sets + 1.2 peeks + 2 sqrts per entity per frame. The heap, not the sqrt, dominates (~85% of rekey: von Neumann, not arithmetic — prediction 2 held). The drain is structural, not an oversight: a deadline-keyed heap cannot find due events without visiting them, and the canonical cut (`settle`) needs every due event — my settle shortcut lost events and the checksum caught it. Gain: 7.4× (β=0.1), 13.2× (β=1), 19.5× (a 10⁶ fleet ring — the co-orbital concentration case), 11× at 3×10⁶; V0's extrapolated ~2.4 s/frame at 10⁷ vs V4's measured 207 ms under load 14. What it deletes: the per-frame sqrt over all bodies and ~2.2N heap mutations — the whole O(N) frame floor except one ~5–10 ns/entity stream.
