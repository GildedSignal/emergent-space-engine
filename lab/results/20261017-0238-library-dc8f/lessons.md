# Five lessons for the ladder's open decisions (the lens deliverable)

Top board bottleneck: 28 live bodies vs 10^9 (nladder compose/gate/belt choices; promotion's
burn read stream). All quotes verbatim, verified by grep against this run's cached export.

1. **Delete maximally; pre-budget the add-back at 10%.**
   "Delete any part or process you can. You may have to add them back later. In fact, if you
   do not end up adding back at least 10% of them, then you didn’t delete enough." — Musk (Isaacson).
   Mechanism: a pre-widened exclusion prices an unmeasured risk (the belt's +/-2% band spares
   434/712; deleting them: -0.7% ledger, 1a46). Change: adopt RES; the sparse re-kick IS the
   10% add-back, bound to the ~10^7-gs fence horizon, k per population (2^8.5 belt, ~2^16 doll).

2. **Only physics is law; every other frozen constant is a recommendation.**
   "The only rules are the ones dictated by the laws of physics. Everything else is a
   recommendation." — Musk (Isaacson). Mechanism: constants transferred outside their derivation
   regime become silent tuning (tau_0 satellite->belt assumed; Moon's line re-corrected).
   Change: constants carry their derivation regime in the name; out-of-regime use needs its probe.
   Test: the B14-class belt probe under Cinder+Giant tide (nladder dir 2); <3x margin widens.

3. **Read the micro before designing the macro store.**
   "The learning principle is to plunge into the detailed mystery of the micro in order to
   understand what makes the macro tick." — Waitzkin, The Art of Learning. Mechanism: the dwell
   dial fails (71-75% miss at every dwell <=80 gs) because residency is global while reads
   arrive in bursts of tens of gs; the chunk is the burst. Change: per-body last-gap residency,
   demote one burst-gap after the last read. Test: print-instrument the burn read stream; kill
   if the cliff (80-100 gs) misses the burst-gap upper quantile.

4. **Keep promotion convex: mint on realized touch only.**
   "The more uncertainty, the more role for optionality to kick in, and the more you will
   outperform." — Taleb, Incerto. Mechanism: a compact body is an unexercised option; pre-minting
   (translate class) pays mint+memory on both error sides — concave. Change: among the arms that
   saturate the same memory wall, reactive beats predictive on principle (parsimony tie, 1051).
   Test: a prefetch arm must beat reactive's standing population at equal miss, or be deleted.

5. **A band that has not crossed yet is a trend: freeze slopes, not envelopes.**
   "The first principle is that you must not fool yourself—and you are the easiest person to
   fool." — Feynman. Mechanism: OFF's drift median grows ~x10 per x2 horizon, crossed the cure
   by 6,700 gs; an envelope at a flown horizon freezes where the discriminator has not acted.
   Change: the doll-born gate freezes the drift SLOPE at >=2x the crossing horizon (>=13,400 gs).
   Test: one 18,000-gs arm; slope predicts OFF median >=1e-4, band law predicts flat.
