## The five lessons (each: verbatim quote, mechanism, change, cheapest test)

1. **Delete maximally; pre-budget the add-back at 10%.**
   "Delete any part or process you can. You may have to add them back later. In fact, if
   you do not end up adding back at least 10% of them, then you didn’t delete enough."
   — Elon Musk (Walter Isaacson).
   Mechanism: a pre-widened exclusion prices an unmeasured risk; deletion reveals what
   the part actually did. The belt's +/-2% resonance band spares 434/712 while deleting
   them moves the ledger only -0.7% (1a46).
   Change: adopt RES (max delete); the sparse secular re-kick IS the 10%-budgeted
   add-back, bound to ~10^7 game-s, derived per population (arithmetic above).
   Test: the compose rung as one arm — RES + horizon-bound re-kick, fence |dA| under one
   quantum at the checker's worst horizon; add-back measured <=1.6%.

2. **Only physics is law; every other frozen constant is a recommendation.**
   "The only rules are the ones dictated by the laws of physics. Everything else is a
   recommendation." — Elon Musk (Walter Isaacson).
   Mechanism: a constant transferred outside its derivation regime becomes silent tuning
   (tau_0 from satellite tides to the heliocentric belt — assumed, not measured; the
   Moon's line corrected from 116,333 ru inward).
   Change: every gate/route constant carries its derivation regime in its name; use
   outside the regime requires its own probe.
   Test: nladder direction 2, the B14-class belt probe under Cinder+Giant tide: margin
   >= the corrected 5.5-6.4x holds it; below ~3x the exclusion widens.

3. **Read the micro before designing the macro store.**
   "The learning principle is to plunge into the detailed mystery of the micro in order
   to understand what makes the macro tick." — Josh Waitzkin, The Art of Learning.
   Mechanism: the dwell dial fails (71-75% miss at every dwell <=80 gs) because residency
   is a global per-body constant while the read stream arrives in bursts of tens of gs —
   the unit that chunks (Waitzkin: one file, accessed as one piece) is the burst.
   Change: promotion's new home — per-body last-gap residency (demote one burst-gap after
   the last read), not a dial constant; measure the burst-gap histogram first.
   Test: print-instrument the shipped burn read stream at 10^3; kill if the cliff band
   (80-100 gs) does not coincide with the burst-gap upper quantile — then chunking is the
   dial renamed.

4. **Keep promotion convex: mint on realized touch only.**
   "The more uncertainty, the more role for optionality to kick in, and the more you
   will outperform." — Nassim Taleb (Incerto; full highlight in cache).
   Mechanism: a compact body is an unexercised option — bounded holding cost, bounded
   exercise cost, upside under burst uncertainty; pre-minting (the translate class) pays
   mint+memory on both error sides — concave, and every equal-letter arm choice that
   pre-mints is dominated.
   Change: among the arms that saturate the same memory wall (all of them, 1051), the
   reactive ones (delete+chain) beat the predictive ones on principle — agreeing with the
   parsimony tie already measured.
   Test: any prefetch arm must beat the reactive arm's standing population at equal miss
   rate on the shipped burn, or be deleted.

5. **A band that has not crossed yet is a trend: freeze slopes, not envelopes.**
   "The first principle is that you must not fool yourself—and you are the easiest
   person to fool." — Richard Feynman, "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!".
   Mechanism: OFF's drift median grows ~x10 per x2 horizon and crossed the cure by
   6,700 gs (1637 check); an envelope frozen at a flown horizon freezes where the
   discriminator has not yet acted — a control that cannot fail.
   Change: the doll-born gate freezes the drift SLOPE per decade of horizon, at >=2x the
   measured crossing horizon (>=13,400 gs), and reports crossing time as primary output.
   Test: one 18,000-gs arm; predicted OFF median >=1e-4 (slope law) vs flat (band law).
