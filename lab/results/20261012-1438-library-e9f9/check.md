3. **Adversarial (my three+ cases).**
   - *Commensurability tautology:* the observable (per-orbit mean) is A-independent **by construction** — a sinusoid with exactly P ticks per period averages to zero over every orbit, so the A=20 arm is statistically the same test as A=1. "0% false alarms at 20× amplitude" is a property of the model, not a measured robustness.
   - *Detuned (precessing) bounded error* (`e = A·sin(2πt/(P(1+ε)))`, frozen (k,h)): A=20, ε=10⁻⁴ → 0/200; A=20, ε=10⁻³ → **200/200 false alarms**, median orbit 9; A=5, ε=10⁻² → 200/200. Breaking condition ≈ A·ε ≳ k. GR apsidal precession and integer-tick period rounding make ε ≠ 0 the norm for integrated orbits (the plane), even where generator-fed dolls are commensurate. The frozen gate would fail acceptable bounded errors exactly when the dossier's headline matters most (large bounded amplitude, small minimum leak).
   - *Sign-alternating leak* (±0.02/orbit): 0/200 — the author-disclosed hole, confirmed.
   - *Slow diffusive walks* (per-orbit step 3×10⁻³ and 10⁻³, i.e. at the noise floor, not the author's fast end whose steps are 5× h): caught 200/200 (median 32) and 186/200 (median 154). This strengthens the method beyond what the author measured.

**What the author missed.**
- The amplitude-invariance claim is the model's construction, not a finding; the physically-native adversarial case (precession detuning — the engine's core GR behaviour) breaks it at the author's own stress amplitude.
- The competitors were stand-ins (decile RMS ratio ≈ the recurrence ratio; running-max log-log slope ≈ the Jacobi exponent), not the shipped statistics — "both live candidates fail their own positive control" is measured on embodiments, though the mechanism (amplitude statistics blind to a small monotone drift under large bounded oscillation) is sound and the leak-side blindness at c/2, c/4 is convincingly demonstrated.
- "The doll knows its period" is true for generator-fed dolls but not for the plane's integrated orbits; a gate scene needs a period estimator or a beat-robust observable (e.g. CUSUM on residuals after removing the best-fit slow sinusoid).
- The walk regime was only run at its trivial fast end (per-orbit steps 5× h, alarm at orbit 0); the noise-floor end — which I ran — is the regime probe 1 actually killed, and it holds.
