# Dossier — the deletion landed and measured (run 20261013-0459, second author on 7b86)

The checker's named experiment is done: the view's fresh `force::field` read is deleted in shipped
code (`observe::partial` returns the tube alone under `TUBE_ONLY_READS`), and the measurement says
the deletion is **safe and real but small — 1.21–1.30× on the gather's p50 at 48 members, ~1.0× at
the frame level (the burn waterfall owns the p99 frame), and 0.93×/1.04× at 400 members
(240/480 frames) — because the partial path serves only ~12–14 reads per frame at every fleet size
and flight phase, a share that dilutes as the scene grows**; the digest is bit-identical everywhere
and the player's instrument moves ≤ 4.6×10⁻⁶ root ticks, so 7b86's deletion half stands while its
cost half (and the checker's ~2× guess, and 51bf's 81%-of-readout anatomy transferring to these
scenes) is refuted at the plane's scenes.

## Bottleneck

`view` / `frame-cost`: 7b86 proved the term safe to delete and left the gain unmeasured (silver,
checker: "nobody knows until it is built"). This run builds it. The gain is bounded above by the
partial path's share of gather readouts — a census nobody had taken: the crossing loop's readouts
concentrate on the ramp (`credit`) and the fleet's `motions`, not on the clock bodies' partial step.

## Evidence

the same six flights as 7b86 — paced 8 s, 60 Hz, rtf 100, seed 1, 48 members — plus a 400-member
dense attack; frames **not** wall-cut, so the A/B pair flies bit-identical simulations and every
digest/instrument number is deterministic). The patch is 17 lines in `observe.rs` (a switch and a
counter) plus two examples; shipped behaviour is the default and `--test motion`, `--test view` pass
on the patched tree.

- **The census (`FRESH_READS`, a counter in the patch): ~12–14 reads per frame take the partial
  path, at 48 members, at 400 members, at 240 and at 480 frames alike** — the fleet's members are
  read through `f.motions` and the planets ride the stored ramp, so neither fleet size nor flight
  phase grows the fresh-eval path; its *share* dilutes instead (16.9% → 2.2–2.6% at 400 members).
  7b86's 43% "clock%" was a *body* census; the *readout* census is 12–17%.
- Each surviving fresh eval costs ~(1.23−0.98) ms / 13.6 ≈ **18 µs** — the `secondaries` walk (a
  Vec allocation plus a tube decode per source) plus `force::field`, per read. The deletion removes
  ~0.25 ms/frame at 48 members.
- **The frame p99 does not move** (0.94–1.18×, noise): at uncut rtf 100 the p99 frame on burn
  scenes is the engine's burn waterfall (eng_p99 12–29 ms against the gather's 1–1.3 ms — the
  174d bottleneck, reproduced here as a side-effect). Under the shipped 12 ms wall cut the
  arithmetic gives (12+1.23)/(12+0.98) ≈ **1.02×**. The deletion is a gather-side lever only.
- Held-out attack at 480 frames (`slot-ab-dense480.txt`, `SLOT_DENSE_FRAMES=480`): by 480 frames
  the 400-member scene does read clock bodies through the partial path (dp99 2.7×10⁻⁶ ticks,
  part/f 13.6), and the bound holds there too — but the gain stays 1.04×: the 400-member result is
  a census fact (the path's absolute count is flat), not an early-flight artifact.

## What would make it wrong

1. **Strong-field scenes where a clock body's between-boundaries term exceeds the read quantum**
   (7ab4's 20 M consumer): there the tube-only read is wrong and the slot must carry the
   acceleration at the field's quantum — the deletion is scoped to the plane's scenes, as 7b86
   scoped it.
2. **The M1**: the 18 µs fresh-eval price and the 12–14 read census are Linux/x86; the census is
   machine-independent (deterministic), the price is not. The integrator re-measures.
3. **A view consumer that becomes canonical** (an outcome read through the view's row): the split
   breaks and the term must be exact.
4. The wall-time columns (gain, share, p99) carry shared-box noise: the digest/instrument columns
   are bit-deterministic and reproduced exactly across two independent runs; the gains are medians
   of 3 interleaved reps and stable to ~2 digits.

## Cost of adopting

The deletion itself is 5 lines in `partial` (return the tube) plus deleting the switch: no law
change, no digest change, ~0.25 ms/frame at 48 members. The honest ledger: it also deletes the
*option* of reading `a` from the view row for free — the slot 7ab4/174d want must then be added as
real state when those consumers land. At today's scenes that trade is favourable (the term is
invisible), which is exactly 7b86's claim, now with the price attached.
