# Predictions (written before the decisive experiment)

Question: on a contiguous belt at 10⁹, does a quantum gate (an encounter enters the
exact tier only if its impulse moves the target's key by ≥ 1 quantum) delete the
~10³ of pair work? Anchor constants from the record: c7eb's m_crit ≈ 1.3×10⁻¹⁷ M☉
(~1.35-km rock) measured as "never crosses a period tick" (0/1000 50-m rocks);
62f7's per-ship flux (0.87–1.04 of truth) as the accumulator for the deleted tail;
1738's contiguous-averaged belt at 1.65×10³–8.2×10³ cores as the ungated wall.

Held-out cases (not tuned on):

1. **Worst-case-over-phases group certificate (the r8-v2 lead's naive form).** For
   any two cells of crossing orbits with random phases, min-over-member-phases miss
   distance at a crossing longitude → 0 as the phase sample grows. Prediction: the
   cell-pair worst case is exactly 0 for every crossing cell pair (no phase-free
   group certificate can skip anything). This kills direction 2's first candidate
   on random-phase belts and redirects it to phase-carrying or mass/quantum gates.
2. **Impulse-vs-quantum distribution (the gate's yield).** For a Dohnanyi-style
   mass spectrum (dN/dD ∝ D^-3.5, D ∈ [50 m, 300 km], belt mass ~10⁻³ of the
   star) at 1-AU-class orbits: the fraction of conjunction pairs whose impulse is
   ≥ 1 key quantum for at least one member is ≤ 10⁻²·(candidate fraction). I
   predict deletable fraction ≥ 99% at these parameters (c7eb's 0/1000 anchors the
   small end; the prediction is about the whole spectrum integral, which c7eb did
   not compute).
3. **Spectrum sensitivity (the content dial).** If instead every body is ≥ 10 km
   (all-large belt), deletable fraction falls below 50% — the lever is a content
   policy, not free physics. This is the honest negative control; if it does NOT
   fall, my gate model is wrong.
4. **The composed ledger.** Gated exact-tier work at 10⁹ contiguous-averaged:
   ≤ 4 cores iff deletable fraction ≥ 99.8%. I predict the Dohnanyi belt lands
   under 8 cores but NOT under 4 (i.e. the gate alone buys ~10³ but the last 2×
   needs the flux/observation split or islands topology).

What would make it wrong: if the impulse formula (deflection Δv = 2μ/(b·v_rel),
c7eb-validated form) mispredicts RK4 flybys by > 2× median in my own replica; if
the quantum anchor (c7eb's m_crit) does not scale as m^(2/3) with Hill-scaled b;
if the deletable fraction is dominated by mid-spectrum bodies (1–10 km) rather
than the small tail.
