# Dossier — the quantum gate: which encounters never need a pair test

## Claim

On a contiguous 10⁹ belt, a gate that charges each candidate pair's strongest
conjunction against the law's own key quantum (one Q48 quantum of E, `crates/space`
`Key`) deletes 99.96% of exact-tier pair work at asteroid-like mass spectra — the

from 1738's 1.65×10³–8.2×10³ averaged anchors) — and the phase-free group certificate
(the r8-v2 lead's naive form) is vacuous on random-phase belts, so this gate, not
grouping, is the candidate-count lever.

## What would make it wrong

- **The quantum anchor.** I gate on one Q48 quantum of E (E≈1 for the belt's bound
  orbits; `Key { energy: Words }` is Q48). If the wired kick trigger is coarser than
  one E-quantum, deletion only grows; if the key's effective granularity is finer
  (exponent-normalized E), survivors rise as the square of the ratio.
- **The conjunction model.** b_min = b_max·U/N_c (min of N_c quasi-random misses).
  A resonant pair's quasi-periodic miss sequence can revisit near-zero more often
  than uniform-random (regular CF structure) — under-counting survivors for the
  resonant minority; the law's resonance gate is the natural complement there.
- **Domain.** Planar, prograde, one belt band (σ_e = 0.05, a∈[0.95,1.05]), horizon
  10³ orbits, 2 crossing longitudes; float replica (orders of magnitude only; the
  integrator re-measures on the M1). Not tested: 3-D inclination (crossings per pair
  rise), the GR doll, kick-perturbed elements inside the horizon, and the
  accumulator's feedback (the deleted tail must still drive secular stirring —
  43d5/6875's no-steady-state finding says the aggregate channel stays loaded).
- **The view seam.** The gate deletes event work, not observable state: positions
  derive from generators regardless, and a rendered close passage costs readout, not
  certification. But a player interacting with a sub-quantum rock (landing on it)
  enters its light cone and re-opens exact treatment for it — the gate is per-pair
  and per-light-cone, never global.
