**Reasons.**

1. **Reproduction (all from shipped code, patch applied by me).** All four plane flights regenerate their stored digests exactly; the four v2 JSONs match the stored first-run digests the dossier cites. The decisive belt run is byte-identical except the machine timestamp. Belt wall 13.2 s ≤ 60 s; memory trivial (123 bodies). `--kick-bench` runs again (median 35 µs/kick).
2. **Adversarial cases (mine, beyond the author's).** (a) Belt at 8–12 AU — beyond Giant, orbital periods ~60× the inner belt's: cadence 3.31e-5 (−7.5%), debt 177.8, cause 0. (b) Belt at 0.15–0.25 AU — inside Cinder: cadence 3.547e-5, debt 174.9; the slow-EMA "reabsorption" makes debt worse (192.7), as on the plane. Radius-invariance of cadence now measured over a ~200× orbital-period range; debt stable within 2%; the kick's applied dv rounds to zero in 100% of bindings in every scene. (c) Source check: ext.rs:26 and `ext::constants` confirm the clock is doll-wide (shortest secondary's period, power-of-two grid, `step_log2 = 7`), so the mechanism claim is real, not just a correlation.
3. **The claim's target is live.** law.md line 13 really reads "The allocator reads H_live … as its memory budget: about +62 bits per kick and −60 per reabsorption", and HANDOFF still lists H_live as "not measured yet". The run fills exactly that: the +62 is ~3× low for the record the plane writes, and the −60 has no mechanism — in a per-binding refit the base moves every kick, so nothing can return to it (white-jitter innovations, measured ≥ raw in three of four scenes).
4. **Predictions — this is what blocks gold.** File times: predictions.md's mtime postdates every decisive belt run (the disclosed append explains one write; unverifiable either way, run.log empty). Decisive: `belt-smoke.json` (mtime 11 min before the decisive runs, disclosed as pre-predictions) already contains debt 175.1 and cadence 3.56e-5 — both headline numbers, at 1/11 the duration. The disclosure admits only the cadence was "available and ignored"; the debt bracket [120, 260] was equally available. The verdict rule (debt ≥ 50 ⇒ ledger dead) was therefore determinable before the predictions were written. The genuinely held-out axes — seed, radius, density, duration, rtf — all held within 1%, and the failed cadence bracket (18×) and the dead fire-fraction instrument were reported honestly; but "the held-out predictions held" is true only of the variants, not of the load-bearing numbers.
5. **Novelty.** No HISTORY entry measures live H_live anywhere; components-98bc's 0.126 bits/body is the static birth program, a different question. This is the first measurement on the plane's real record and the first on a belt.

**The one experiment that would make it gold.** Build the cause-journal twin: journal only the kick quanta (~2 bits/binding) and recompute the per-binding refit on read, then verify bit-identical records on the belt. That converts "the ledger is dead" into "the replacement is measured at ~2 bits/binding with exact replay", which is the adoptable form of the finding.

**What the author missed.** (a) The disclosure names the smoke's cadence but not its debt, though both were on disk before the brackets were written. (b) The grid arithmetic is loose: measured 1/cadence = 27.96 ks against "P_D/256 ≈ 29.7 ks" (my independent Cinder estimate gives ~30.7 ks; `ds` is P_D/128/f'(c) floored to a power of two, so "/256" is approximate, ~6–8% off). (c) The sweep's w=0 fire fraction (21.7% on the belt) contradicts dv ≡ 0 — an artifact of the replay carry starting at zero while the engine's carry swings inside its band; the author flagged w ≥ 8 as artifact but not this. (d) "≥ 98% not absorbable by any mean-style refit" rests on one EMA gain (1/64); the innovations support the white-jitter diagnosis but a gain sweep would close it. (e) The compute twin (~3.6e4 binding-events per game-second at 10⁹, the allocator's real load) is named in the lens section and left unmeasured.
