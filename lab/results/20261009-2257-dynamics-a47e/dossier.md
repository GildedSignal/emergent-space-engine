# Dossier — 20261009-2257-dynamics-a47e (dynamics lens)

**Claim.** B12's growth exponent inverts because on chaotic ships every error series saturates on the state attractor, so the exponent measures saturation timing, not boundedness — and a threshold-free exact-integer Allan-variance rule (`leak ⇔ AVAR(T/4) > AVAR(T/32)`) separates bounded from leaking error series with every miss at a measured horizon, giving the B12 redesign the detectable leak rate and window condition it asks for.

**Bottleneck.** The instrument: "B12 cannot yet tell bounded from compounding error on chaotic ships; until it can, the motion law's stability is judged by probes, not a gate" (HANDOFF risks; law.md's miscalibration note; probe P2: bounded scored 0.493, compound 0.305). Gain: a correct gate statistic unlocks the frozen B12 (handoffs judged by the Jacobi constant) and every later stability judgement; without it the motion law v2 stays probe-judged. Not a 10× engine-cost move — an instrument unblock, the lens's "a gate we lack."

**Evidence** (all from `python3 exp.py controls|inversion|wh` in this folder; deterministic, one LCG, ≤ 60 s each; `wh` ≈ 2–5 min):

1. **The inversion reproduces without the plane** (`exp.py inversion`): 16-seed ensembles, chaotic-saturated error series (e^{λt} growth to the attractor, λ set to saturate at 10% of the window, leak = walk with total drift 1.5 attractor amplitudes). Median growth exponent: bounded 49.2–56.7 vs slow-leak 0.32–0.48, **inversion 3/3 draws** (predicted ≥ 2). The leak's exponent ≈ 0.3–0.5 matches P2's 0.305; the bounded family's is larger still. Conclusion: the inversion is a property of the statistic on any saturated series, not of the plane's law — B12's number never tested the law.
2. **The rule classifies all 25 planted integer series correctly** (`exp.py controls`): quasiperiodic, chaos-saturated (OU), slow sinusoids P ≤ T/4 → bounded; pure walks at every rate tried (total drift 0.1A–10A) → leak; walk+quasiperiodic mixtures at drift 1.5A, 3A → leak. Exact rational arithmetic (Fractions on integer series); no threshold anywhere.
3. **The horizon, measured, not asserted** (post-prediction, disclosed): with a quasiperiodic floor of amplitude A, leaks are caught from σ√T/A ≈ 0.2–0.3 (0.05/0.1/0.2 missed, 0.3/0.5/1/1.5/3 caught) — ~5× better than the √2 I derived before the run (the floor averages out at τ = T/4; the honest statement is AVAR-level: *caught ⇔ σ²·(T/4) > AVAR_bounded(T/4)*). A pure walk with no bounded floor is caught at every rate tried (0.1A–10A total drift).
4. **The window condition (alias)** (post-prediction, disclosed): a bounded oscillation slower than the longest averaging time misclassifies: P = N/2 → leak at 4/5 phases (phase 0 dodges by exact cancellation — half-period block sums of cos ≈ 1, not 2/π·N; my pre-registered arithmetic was wrong there), P = N/2.5 → leak, P = N/3 and P ≤ T/4 → bounded. **Window must satisfy T ≥ 4·P_max** (τ₂ = T/4 must cover the slowest bounded period: a libration, a secular oscillation). This is the same number the horizon trades against: the gate window is a budget, and both the slowest bounded mode and the slowest detectable leak are paid from it.
5. **On the law's cheap path** (`exp.py wh`: Wisdom–Holman split — exact classical-elements Kepler drift about the star, verified to 3×10⁻¹⁴ relative energy over 30k steps, plus planet kicks at fixed phase steps, CRTBP Jacobi sampled 2/orbit, integerized at 2⁴⁰, exact rule; 8 ships, μ = 10⁻³, 250 planet orbits): draw 0 leak-free symmetric DKD → **0/8 leak flags**, median growth exponent 0.049. A one-step-retarded planet (a simultaneity bug) → 0/8 flags: below the horizon, as expected for a perturbation weaker than the bounded wiggle — and note the ordering inversion again on the physical path: the leaky variant's exponent (0.021) is *lower* than the good one's (0.049). The 10-step retard and draw 1 land in `wh-output.txt` (this run's pre-screen re-seeds scenes that eject a ship or dip under 0.02 — deep encounters need SyMBA's changeover, out of this toy's scope).

**Predictions.** `predictions.md` written before the decisive runs, plus two dated addenda disclosing instrument fixes (the first Part C run's universal-variable Kepler drift lost 4% of two-body energy — measured, fixed, re-run; the first-order asymmetric "leak" turned out genuinely bounded, replaced by retarded-source kicks). **What would make it wrong.** (a) The rule's τ₂ = T/4 with only 3–4 non-overlapping blocks is statistically coarse: a bounded series with long memory (flicker-like, 1/f) sits near AVAR flat and could flip either way — untested here; the redesign should sample AVAR across a decade of τ and demand *consistency* (falling or level-then-falling), not one ratio. (b) A law whose bounded error contains a mode slower than T/4 (a Trojan-horseshoe libration, ~10²–10³ orbits) will be flagged: B12's window must be set by the scene's slowest physical mode, which for the crossing ships is the encounter-return timescale — that needs a scene-specific derivation the redesign must own. (c) Part C's toy has no changeover: deep-encounter ships were screened out, so "chaotic crossing ships" are represented by the model series (item 1) and draw 0's 28 Hill-crossing steps, not by a faithful B12 scene; the inversion's reproduction (item 1) is the evidence P2's number is the statistic's fault, but the rule's behaviour on true SyMBA-handled crossings is unproven here. (d) Everything float is exploration; only the integer series decide, per the lab rule.

**Cost of adopting.** The statistic is ~20 lines (integer block sums, Fraction comparison), one pass over a per-ship conserved-quantity series the engine already samples; no reference run, no trajectory identity, machine-independent (no `echo` convergence — P2's Linux failure mode disappears because nothing is compared across machines). It slots into `behave` B12's adapter: per ship, integerized J series over the gate window, rule at τ₂ = T/4 vs τ₁ = T/32, window chosen ≥ 4× the scene's slowest bounded mode. Fits PRINCIPLES: "types of errors and not just errors" (OC#42 — the rule classifies the error's type, bounded vs compounding, exactly the B5/B12 distinction); "exact means deterministic and reproducible" (integers decide, no floats, no thresholds fitted — law.md's "no threshold is fitted"); "i care about the p95/99 results" (CL 09-25) — a median exponent over 16 seeds crushed the tail information, the exact failure P2 caught.

**The lens.** Dynamics and attractors made me see the error series as the object (attractor vs random walk, Allan variance as the classifier) and frame the horizon and alias as two faces of one window budget; it also made me under-rate the phase-dependence of the alias (a return-map detail) and trust my √2 derivation over measurement — the run corrected both. What the lens may have missed: the multi-scale/flicker subtlety (a), and everything the engineers already know about why J alone might not be the right conserved quantity on ships that legitimately exchange energy (J is exact in CRTBP, but a real doll tree has no exact J — the redesign must pick the invariant per handoff class).

The strong-leak-caught prediction is therefore **not delivered**; the rule's leak detection on the physical path rests on the planted controls (items 2–3) and remains open for the checker or a follow-up run with a gentler planted leak (e.g. a small multiplicative kick bias), which is exactly one experiment away.
