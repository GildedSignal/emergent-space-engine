**What the author missed.**

The dossier's "`64` → any small bound" is wrong, and the residual unbudgeted term that re-emerges at small caps (most plausibly the fleet/handout resolutions ahead of the player's item, `universe.rs:733-741`) is unnamed and unmeasured.
