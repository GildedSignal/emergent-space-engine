**Cost of adoption.** Small for two of the three defects: `rebuild` should reset-then-max the caps over the retained bindings (it already iterates them), and the density rule should compare against the doll's own membership with the fallback iterating the doll's registered ids (`bins` ∪ `unbounded`) instead of `0..total`. The window poison's full fix is a representation change: register each body's bins widened by **its own** speed bound (`v_j·h`), not the doll-wide cap — slow bodies stay in 1–2 bins, hot bodies correctly spread.
