# Dossier: the multiply-add cycle clause measured — a drift certificate, a missing type rule

## Claim

representation.md L67 ("a cycle's derivatives are stored with it, so a kick updates the cycle by
a multiply-add") is sound at tidal amplitudes but incomplete as written: on the exact
Schwarzschild cycle map its certificate is not a magic length but a **drift rule — re-derive
when accumulated kick drift (relative, in L or in the binding energy 1−E) reaches ≈10%** —
and on the escape channel it is **dead by type, not size** (the φ error stays ≤10⁻⁵ of the
pericentre advance while the true cycle period diverges ×76 and then ceases to exist; the
multiply-add keeps returning a finite returning cycle to the end). No rung has any kick-chain
arm; nothing anywhere had measured this clause (binet-cells' open direction 4 named it
unguarded).

## Bottleneck

Moves the board's macro row ("the per-body step as a certificate, chosen by dimensionless
numbers per regime"): the certificate is now measured and it is one field-independent
dimensionless number (relative drift ≈ 0.1), not a regime table. It prices the shipped
engine's recompile-on-every-distinct-kick path (taste log, 5 Oct): mandatory at encounter
amplitudes (N* = 20–380 kicks at δ=10⁻², every cell incl. belt-like), ~10³–10⁶× wasteful at
tidal amplitudes (δ≤10⁻⁴: no size failure through 2×10⁵ kicks anywhere).

## Evidence

Instrument: exact (Δφ, Δt, Δτ) per radial cycle of the exact Schwarzschild geodesic by
quadrature (endpoint-singular substitution; `cycle.py`). Controls, green before the grid:
ϖ/(6π/p̄) → 1 with residual ∝ p̄⁻¹ (4.5×10⁻⁴ at p̄=10⁴ → 4.5×10⁻⁶ at 10⁶); Δt → Kepler
2πa^{3/2} (rel ∝ p̄⁻¹); an independent Binet-ODE integration matches Δφ to 4.0×10⁻¹⁵ / 1.6×10⁻¹³
at p̄ = 20 / 1000. Derivatives are central differences of the exact map (generous: the design
stores exact derivatives; nothing real does better). State (E,L) is exact at every kick; only
the description is linear (frozen derivatives). Frozen bar (predictions.md): |Δφ̂−Δφ| ≤ 10% of
the current exact advance ϖ.

Grid: 7 cells × δ ∈ {10⁻⁶,10⁻⁴,10⁻²} × 3 kick programs (`random` tidal walk; `coherent-dL`
drag/inspiral; `coherent-dE` toward escape), kicks scaled to the binding energy (disclosed
pre-run correction), cap 2×10⁵ kicks, geometric checkpoints (`grid.json`):

- **Drift law (the certificate).** Coherent arms cross at N*·δ = 0.066–0.20 across all seven
  cells (belt-like 1.96×10⁵/1 959/19.6 at δ = 10⁻⁶/10⁻⁴/10⁻²; whirl 6.6×10⁴/663/6.6; strong
  9.3×10⁴/929/9.3; growth b ≈ 1.85–2.33, r² ≥ 0.995). Random arms that cross (δ=10⁻²) do so at
  rms drift √N*·δ = 0.05–0.19 (belt-like 50, strong 45, whirl 23, r3-heartland 377). Field
  strength drops out: the certificate is the **relative drift, ~10%**, one number for every
  regime measured — the board's "dimensionless numbers per regime" collapses to one constant.
- **Heartland holds (positive control).** No size failure through the 2×10⁵ cap at δ ≤ 10⁻⁴
  random anywhere; extrapolated N* ~ 10⁶–10⁹ (noisy fits, orders only). At encounter amplitude
  (δ=10⁻²) the certificate collapses to 10¹–10² kicks **including at belt scale** — the shipped
  recompile-per-distinct-kick is mandatory there, not waste.
- **Escape is type-blind.** near-sep, coherent-dE δ=10⁻⁴ (escape at exactly n = 1/δ = 10⁴):
  at n = 5 000 / 9 500 the true period Δt = 4.1×10⁵ / 1.3×10⁷ (×76 from n = 10³) while the
  multiply-add says 2.5×10⁵ / 3.5×10⁵ (0.62×, then 0.027× of truth) and its φ error stays at
  4.4×10⁻⁶ / 1.6×10⁻⁵ of ϖ — **the φ bar never fires**; at n = 9 999 the description returns a
  complete returning cycle for an orbit that has no bound cycle at all. Plunge arms by contrast
  warn in time (whirl δ=10⁻²: bar breaks at kick 6.7, plunge at ≤16).
- The same 10% drift rule catches escape early: at n = 0.1/δ = 1 000 the description is still
  accurate (Δt ratio 0.98) — re-derivation there costs 1/10 of the kicks-to-escape.

Reproduce: `python3 cycle.py validate` (~1 s) and `python3 cycle.py grid` (~17 s), both
regenerated by `regen.sh` from this folder.

## What would make it wrong, adoption cost, fit

Wrong if: the shipped kick amplitudes are far from my δ grid (then N* moves as δ⁻¹·0.1 — the
drift rule itself is amplitude-free and survives); or the belt's real kick correlations are
pathological (neither changes the type-blindness, which is amplitude-free by construction: the
φ-channel is smooth through escape). Cost of adopting: one rule in the doc (L67) plus a
per-body drift accumulator (2 adds per kick, 0 bytes with the id-derived calendar's slot) and
one rung arm (below). It composes with the shipped path: recompile stays the fallback, the
accumulator only decides when. Fit with PRINCIPLES: it is the certificate Julien asked for
("steps are there to solve a problem of how much error we are ok with… We need to expand what
steps mean", 29 Sep); the type finding is his error-type-over-error-size (P2/P13: "divergence
is not a joke and it compounds" — here the compounding is avoided only if the rule exists);
the drift rule is one law at every scale (P19/P32: the representation makes the symmetry
explicit — the field cancels out of the certificate).
