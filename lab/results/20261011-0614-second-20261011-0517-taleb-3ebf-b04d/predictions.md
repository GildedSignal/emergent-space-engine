# Predictions (written before the fix runs; the parent record is the known side)

The second checker's named experiment, done from the run's own builders. Question restated: is
the union phase sort's zero-miss catch a property of **exact period equality** (eternal only when
fleets are period-quantized onto one shared integer period, the belt-`keys` construction), and
does the merge-join at a crossing epoch catch the different-period interception's cross partners
at small rank?

Known side (controls, regenerated by `battle`, not predictions): the 8 parent scenes, including
b1-cross-150 (a-spread 1e-4, 20 orbits): kmax_union_cross 14, kmed 2, caught_union 1.000 by k=16;
b2-intercept: cross partners at kmed 67/166/284, kmax 100/217/423 (~n/2, 0.7·n). My `a-1e-4-H20`
scene uses the parent's exact seed and must reproduce b1-cross-150's numbers exactly — if it does
not, the instrument is broken and nothing below counts.

Model behind the predictions: n = a^-3/2, so two bodies with |Δa| drift apart in mean longitude
at |Δn| ≈ 1.5·|Δa| rad per time unit ≈ TAU·1.5·|Δa| per orbit. Rank in the setup sort grows
≈ drift / spacing, spacing = packet width / n_total = 0.5/300. So
kmax ≈ c · TAU·1.5·da·H·(n_total/0.5) = c·9.42·da·H·600, with c ∈ [0.5, 1.5] (the parent's b1-150
point kmax=14 at da=1e-4, H=20 gives c ≈ 1.24). Saturated (fully scrambled) rank ≈ uniform on
[0, n_total]: kmed ≈ n/4…n/3, kmax ≈ 0.7·n (the parent's b2 values).

## A: period-quantization sweep (n = 150 per fleet, horizons 5/20/80/320)

1. **q (both fleets on one exact integer period):** relative mean longitude is constant by
   arithmetic (same period ⇒ M_i(t) − M_j(t) is exactly t-independent), so the sort is eternal:
   kmax_uc IDENTICAL at every horizon (flat), value = the setup-only rank, 2–10.
   caught_uc(8) = 1.000 at every horizon. This is the fix's foundation; if kmax grows with H
   here, the fix dies at its root.
2. **da=1e-4 (the parent's b1, extended):** kmax ≈ 14 at H=20 (control), ≈ 3–7 at H=5,
   35–60 at H=80, 140–210 (near or at saturation) at H=320. caught_uc(32): ≈ 1.0 at H≤20,
   0.95–1.0 at H=80, 0.4–0.85 at H=320. The validity horizon H*(kmax=32) ≈ 40–70 orbits.
3. **da=1e-3:** kmax ≈ 20–35 at H=5, ≈ 100–130 at H=20, saturated at H≥80. The sort is already
   broken at the parent's horizon.
4. **da=1e-2:** saturated at every horizon (kmed_uc ≈ n/2-ish, kmax_uc ≈ 0.6–0.75·n_total);
   AND the scene leaves the co-located regime: cone_cross < n_A·n_B (the Binet cone keeps only
   |Δa| ≲ 2e-3 pairs), so I predict cone_cross ≈ 0.3–0.5·n². Reported as a regime flag.
5. **k (knife-edge, both fleets quantized but on keys 0.15% apart, a=1 vs a=1.001):** drift is
   now fleet-to-fleet with zero internal spread: kmax ≈ 90–130 at H=20, saturated at H=80.
   Quantization alone does not save the union sort unless the two groups SHARE the key; adjacent
   keys need either the built code's reach-bounded refusal (k ∝ n·reach) or a cross-group tier.
6. **Fleet-local plateau (the silent-miss side):** caught_f_k plateaus at ≈ 1 − cross_frac at
   every spread and horizon (cross partners are structurally invisible to a per-clump sort).
7. **outside_cone = 0 in every scene** (instrument control; if not, the truth or matchup is
   broken and nothing counts).

## B: merge-join at the crossing epoch (b2 scenes, parent's seeds)

Mechanism: at the crossing epoch e, an encounter pair's key difference is
key_A(e) − key_B(e) ≈ −(n_A − n_B)(t_enc − e) ± ρ-terms, with |t_enc − e| bounded by the
crossing window (~0.1–0.3 time units) and |n_A − n_B| ≈ 0.095; B's spacing at the crossing
≈ 0.1 rad / n_B. So kmax ≈ 0.095·0.13/0.1·n_B ≈ 0.12·n_B — ∝ n, but ~40 at n_B = 300.

8. Keyed by **mean longitude at t_meet**: kmax_uc ≤ 90 at n=300/fleet (vs 423 at setup),
   kmed ≤ 12, caught_uc(32) ≥ 0.98 at every size. Keyed by **true longitude at t_meet**:
   the same within noise (A is near-circular, B is at perihelion where mean = true).
9. **Per-epoch keying** (epochs = B's perihelion passages, ~5 in the 6-orbit scene, nearest to
   each encounter): ≈ the single-epoch key, because the geometry gives one real alignment
   (t_meet) — I predict all cross encounters lie within ~0.1 orbits of an epoch, and ≥ 90% of
   them within ~0.15 orbits of t_meet itself. If encounters spread over several epochs and the
   single-epoch key inflates while per-epoch stays small, then per-epoch re-keying is REQUIRED,
   not optional.
10. If the t_meet-keyed sort still needs k > n/4 for zero miss, the merge-join does not catch
    the interception and the missing-tier design stays open (report as such).

## C: the honest number (arithmetic on the parent's b1 scenes)

11. Unique pairs within linear rank k of the setup union sort: ≈ n·k − k(k+1)/2. At b1-300
    (600 bodies): ≈ 18,672 pairs at k=32 (caught_union(32) = 1.000, the parent's record) vs
    179,701 window-tier sections on 179,700 cone pairs → **9.6× at zero miss**. The 19× headline
    needs k=16 (≈ 9,464 pairs, 19.0×) which misses 2.1% silently. And the catching k grows ∝ n
    (8/16/32 at 150/300/600 bodies), so the union sort is a constant-factor pair reduction at
    fixed packet width, not an asymptotic one — the constant is the packet's phase fraction.

## Kill criteria

- q's kmax growing with H kills the fix's foundation (exact shared periods do NOT freeze the
  sort) — the adoption line "fleets must be period-quantized" would be wrong, and I report the
  fix as dead in its happy case.
- caught_uc(32) < 0.98 under the t_meet key at every size kills the merge-join as the missing
  cross-group tier.
- outside_cone ≠ 0 anywhere, or a-1e-4-H20 ≠ b1-cross-150, voids the instrument.
- da=1e-4 at H=320 still caught_uc(32) ≥ 0.99 would falsify the drift model (positive surprise,
  report as such: the parent's spread is more benign than the model claims).
