## What the author missed

- The census instrument counts the whole scene; one `belt_first` read or one 10⁵ arm would have shown the belt is ~15% of it at 10⁴. The conclusion survives, the attribution did not.
- "Byte-identical" claimed for a fragment check; L1's unmarked splice is exactly what a quote-lens run must not carry silently.
- Belt bodies are `Kind::Ship`: the scan structure registers their radial ranges (static key, at events). Harmless for the mid-interval claim, but "readout callers" is not the whole reader story and the page should say so.
- One seed. The outcome classes are structural (surface/edge checks), so seed risk is low, but the lab's own bar is behaviours over seeds; the burn arm — the only stirring the shipped code offers — was available and unflown.
