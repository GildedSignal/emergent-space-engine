# The belt: birth programs, the secular field, conjunction calendars

## Current best

**The birth program stands, is wired into the law, and survives stirring only if it
carries phase.** A smooth belt (banded p, Rayleigh e, uniform angles) is a bijective
counter-mix through dyadic quantile tables plus `Key::darwin`: 16,456 bytes total,
0.126 bits/body at 2²⁰ and 1.3×10⁻⁴ at 10⁹, against 157.4 bits/body packed blocks
(19.7 GB); scars cost ~60–75 bits per scarred body at any churn
(20261004-1525-components-98bc, gold). It is shipped: `belt_birth`/`belt_restore` in
the `space` law take B18b from FAIL to PASS on all five frozen runs — 31 B of birth
for any n (32 B at 10⁹), 9.7 B per scar, 17 ns per regenerated body
(20261006-2240-b-belt-wire-a733, silver). But a *frozen* program dies under the belt's
own motion: self-stirring crosses 16 bits/body within 11–12 orbits at the behaviour
quantum and saturates ≈40 (20261006-1354-dynamics-04d7, silver); Jupiter-driven
memo-key shear re-keys every body each ~50–100 orbits
(20261008-0458-system-5a2c, silver); real Jupiter flybys are worse — chaotic for every
bounded event program (the 12-term basis recaptures 0.0 of 41.45 bits at q24;
100% of bodies scarred each 10-orbit epoch, min |Δz| = 43× the quantum), so the
event-scarred store prices at 4.8–5.2 GB/10⁹ (q24) or 3.0 GB (q16)
(20261007-0711-second-…-scout-6e10-ab9d, gold). The escape is measured: the churn is a
synodic sweep of ~459 cells/ship/P_p (exact 1/q scaling, ×16 per 4 bits), and a
phase-locked program — per-ship T_syn from a, one analytic line — absorbs it to
**0.55 key updates/ship/P_p, linear to 1200 P_p**; the mature footprint is 20.7–22.1
bits/ship at the 16-bit tube grid (20261011-0006-second-…-taleb-6baf-2b8e, gold).
Reband loops converge at 3.5–4.5 bits/body for the common-mode flow
(20261008-0615-second-…-system-5a2c-af34, silver); mutual self-stirring below a
crossover mass μ_b ≈ 1.7×10⁻¹⁸ (~1 km rock) creates zero distinctions on the law's
integer grid — a ship belt stays a 0.126-bit program forever, 0.3–3.2 GB per stirred
10⁹ above it (20261010-1816-components-6273, silver).

**The secular field does not carry the belt; real conjunctions do, and they are not
predictable in closed form.** A ring-averaged secular twin (same births, same
integrator) explains none of the belt's mean-element drift — residual 108.71 of
108.77 bits at 20 yr, the twin's E response 40–112× too small, held on a held-out
seed — and the staleness that remains is bounded machinery (median |Δa| saturates at
1.8×10⁻³ AU; clone divergence 7.8 bits), carried by the kick layer, not by a secular
program in the store (20261007-1106-second-…-taleb-ab03-0263, gold). Zero-kick
secular phasors (R2) are killed at two-planet belt scale: rings 14.5%, Laplace–Lagrange
0% against the 50% bar — the averaging binds, not the LL driver
(20261006-0737-q-secular-v2-5f69, silver, on a repaired truth; the first kill
20261005-2231-q-secular-008e ran on a truth with Saturn's kick erased). Secular
scarring saturates at ~6 bits/body (q = 10⁻⁶) within 2 orbits, then ~0 creation
(20261007-0933-scout-23c8, silver). The belt's own chaos is narrow: 5.75% chaotic
bodies at Jupiter μ = 9.54×10⁻⁴ (0.75% at μ = 10⁻⁶, 28.6% at 10⁻²), but only
**0.125% in a non-resonant sub-band** [0.50, 0.60], steady in time
(20261006-0916-redteam-e84d, silver). Conjunction calendars cannot be precomputed:
the miss-distance sequence has no fixed rotation number (next-closest index error
median 5.5, independent replication 19, against a kill threshold of 2), killing the
r8-v2 continued-fraction pay-once premise (20261008-1947-library-d583, silver); and
the distinction-rate closed form Λ = 0.85·Nρσ/W is dead — its K prefactor climbs
1.05 → 1.81 → 2.01 as W/σ goes 2 → 10, so measured rates are floors
(Λ(10⁻⁴) = 0.525; 20261010-0352-second-…-spacex-acb2-7b82, silver).

**What the belt costs the engine, measured.** The planar flux model reproduces the
measured element-write rate with no fitted constants (model/measured 1.05–1.47;
N^1.07, m^1.13): a planar 10⁹ belt writes 209 MB/game (31 writes/s), a 3-D belt
18 KB/game (20261007-0933-second-…-fermi-fc22-a7d0, gold). The per-body re-key rate
is broad, not concentrated: p99/mean ≈ 3.3, max/mean ≈ 5.7, no hot bodies — a
p99-uniform cadence wastes ~70%, and the exploitable structure is predictability
(librators), not concentration (20261009-1649-dynamics-ec3a, silver). At canonical
μ = 10⁻³ the (E,L) key process is diffusive below q ≈ element range (order μ), so
the exact memo cannot pay anywhere; compile load is uniform (top-5% ≈ 7% of keys),
and a separation-growth classifier ranks future work (ρ = 0.74) where
time-averaged resonance distance cannot (ρ = −0.05)
(20261011-2306-dynamics-0b04, silver).

**Domain.** 2-D test-particle belts (no belt–belt coupling), one star plus one Giant
(μ = 9.54×10⁻⁴) or Jupiter+Saturn analogs; μ swept 0–10⁻²; births to 2²³ bodies,
key-churn runs to 10⁶ ships and 1200 P_p; horizons 20–60 yr (secular twin), 23.7 kyr
(secular truth), ~2000 orbits (self-stirring). NOT tested: inclined/3-D belts, k > 1
stirrers, e₀ > 0.2 (0b04's instrument NaNs there), beyond 1200 P_p (approach to the
domain-uniform limit), collisional or player-driven repopulation, and conjunction-less
encounters (outside d583's channel). Store economics beyond the belt (promotion
census, floors, the shipped record) live on the [memory-h-live](memory-h-live.md)
page; kick forms on [kick-jet](kick-jet.md).

## Refuted

- Zero-kick secular phasors (R2) at two-planet belt scale: rings 14.5%, LL 0% against
  the 50% bar (20261006-0737-q-secular-v2-5f69, silver; the first kill's truth was
  broken — Saturn's kick erased — and was repaired first).
- The averaged secular field as the belt's driver: the twin explains none of the
  mean-element drift (20261007-1106-second-…-taleb-ab03-0263, gold).
- Bounded event programs for belt flyby kicks (12-term basis, synodic, Fourier): 0.0
  of 41.45 bits recaptured at q24 (20261007-0711-second-…-scout-6e10-ab9d, gold).
- Continued-fraction prediction of a pair's next close conjunction: no fixed rotation
  number (20261008-1947-library-d583, silver).
- The distinction-rate closed form Λ = 0.85·Nρσ/W: K is not constant in W/σ
  (20261010-0352-second-…-spacex-acb2-7b82, silver).
- The original scout-6e10 store line "≤ 0.6 GB + 37 MB/epoch": ≈ 8× optimistic; the
  real price is 4.8–5.2 GB/10⁹ at q24 (20261007-0711-second-…-scout-6e10-ab9d, gold).
- The original fc22 line "≤ 1 MB per game": holds only in 3-D; the planar belts the
  engine ships pay ~200× more (20261007-0933-second-…-fermi-fc22-a7d0, gold).
- Cadence by concentration (p99 clamp): the rate is broad, waste ~70%; rank by
  predictability instead (20261009-1649-dynamics-ec3a, silver).
- The exact element memo on belt (E,L) keys: diffusion below q ≈ range refills any
  cell (20261011-2306-dynamics-0b04, silver).

## Contradictions

None open. The store numbers spread (0.126 bits at birth; 2.4 self-stirred below the
crossover mass; ~21 at the tube grid under one stirrer; ≈40 saturated at belt-0
density; 4.8–5.2 GB/10⁹ under real flybys at q24) by drive and quantum, each measured
in its regime, not in conflict. 0b04's "the exact memo cannot pay" and 6baf's
phase-lock absorption are different objects: chaotic (E,L) diffusion below q ≈ range
vs the derived synodic sweep. af34's "≤ 0.6 GB via rebands" prices the common-mode
flow only; the scout gold's 4.8–5.2 GB is the chaotic-flyby residue at q24 — compose,
don't compare.
