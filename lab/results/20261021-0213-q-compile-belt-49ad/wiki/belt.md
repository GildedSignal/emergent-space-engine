## Current best

**The birth program stands, is wired into the law, and survives stirring only if it carries phase.** A smooth belt (banded p, Rayleigh e, uniform angles) is a bijective counter-mix through dyadic quantile tables plus `Key::darwin`: 16,456 bytes total, 0.126 bits/body at 2²⁰ (1.3×10⁻⁴ at 10⁹) against 157.4 bits/body packed blocks (19.7 GB); scars cost ~60–75 bits per scarred body at any churn (20261004-1525-components-98bc, gold). Shipped: `belt_birth`/`belt_restore` in the `space` law take B18b from FAIL to PASS on all five frozen runs — 31 B of birth for any n (32 B at 10⁹), 9.7 B per scar, 17 ns per regenerated body (20261006-2240-b-belt-wire-a733, silver).

**A frozen program dies under the belt's own motion; the measured escape is the phase-locked program.** Self-stirring crosses 16 bits/body within 11–12 orbits at the behaviour quantum and saturates ≈40 (20261006-1354-dynamics-04d7, silver); Jupiter-driven memo-key shear re-keys every body each ~50–100 orbits (20261008-0458-system-5a2c, silver), and reband loops converge at 3.5–4.5 bits/body for the common-mode flow (20261008-0615-second-20261008-0458-system-5a2c-af34, silver); real Jupiter flybys are worse — chaotic for every bounded event program, so the event-scarred store prices at 4.8–5.2 GB/10⁹ (q24) or 3.0 GB (q16) (20261007-0711-second-20261007-0621-scout-6e10-ab9d, gold).

The escape, measured: the churn is a synodic sweep of ~459 cells/ship/P_p (exact 1/q scaling, ×16 per 4 bits), and a phase-locked program — per-ship T_syn from a, one analytic line — absorbs it to **0.55 key updates/ship/P_p, linear to 1200 P_p**; the mature footprint is 20.7–22.1 bits/ship at the 16-bit tube grid (20261011-0006-second-20261010-2314-taleb-6baf-2b8e, gold). Below a crossover mass μ_b ≈ 1.7×10⁻¹⁸ (~1 km rock), mutual self-stirring creates zero distinctions on the law's integer grid — a ship belt stays a 0.126-bit program forever, 0.3–3.2 GB per stirred 10⁹ above it (20261010-1816-components-6273, silver).

**The secular field does not carry the belt; real conjunctions do, and they are not predictable in closed form.** The staleness that remains is bounded machinery — median |Δa| saturates at 1.8×10⁻³ AU, clone divergence 7.8 bits — carried by the kick layer, not by a secular program in the store (20261007-1106-second-20261007-1032-taleb-ab03-0263, gold). Secular scarring saturates at ~6 bits/body (q = 10⁻⁶) within 2 orbits, then ~0 creation (20261007-0933-scout-23c8, silver). The belt's own chaos is narrow: 5.75% chaotic bodies at Jupiter μ = 9.54×10⁻⁴ (0.75% at μ = 10⁻⁶, 28.6% at 10⁻²), but **0.125% in a non-resonant sub-band** [0.50, 0.60], steady in time (20261006-0916-redteam-e84d, silver).

**What the belt costs the engine, measured.** The planar flux model reproduces the measured element-write rate with no fitted constants (model/measured 1.05–1.47; N^1.07, m^1.13): a planar 10⁹ belt writes 209 MB/game (31 writes/s), a 3-D belt 18 KB/game (20261007-0933-second-20261007-0842-fermi-fc22-a7d0, gold). The per-body re-key rate is broad, not concentrated: p99/mean ≈ 3.3, max/mean ≈ 5.7, no hot bodies — a p99-uniform cadence wastes ~70%, and the exploitable structure is predictability (librators), not concentration (20261009-1649-dynamics-ec3a, silver). At canonical μ = 10⁻³ the (E,L) key process is diffusive below q ≈ element range (order μ), so the exact memo cannot pay anywhere; compile load is uniform (top-5% ≈ 7% of keys), and a separation-growth classifier ranks future work (ρ = 0.74) where time-averaged resonance distance cannot (ρ = −0.05) (20261011-2306-dynamics-0b04, silver).

**Store economics in one line:** 0.126 bits/body at birth (98bc); 2.4 self-stirred below the crossover mass (6273); ~21 at the tube grid under one stirrer (6baf); ≈40 saturated at belt-0 density (04d7); 4.8–5.2 GB/10⁹ under real flybys at q24 (ab9d) — spread by drive and quantum, each measured in its regime, not in conflict (details in Contradictions).

- Bounded event programs for belt flyby kicks (12-term basis, synodic, Fourier): 0.0 of 41.45 bits recaptured at q24; 100% of bodies scarred each 10-orbit epoch, min |Δz| = 43× the quantum (20261007-0711-second-20261007-0621-scout-6e10-ab9d, gold).
- Continued-fraction prediction of a pair's next close conjunction: the miss-distance sequence has no fixed rotation number — next-closest index error median 5.5, independent replication 19, against a kill threshold of 2 — killing the r8-v2 pay-once premise (20261008-1947-library-d583, silver).
- The distinction-rate closed form Λ = 0.85·Nρσ/W: its K prefactor climbs 1.05 → 1.81 → 2.01 as W/σ goes 2 → 10 (Λ(10⁻⁴) = 0.525), so measured rates are floors (20261010-0352-second-20261010-0306-spacex-acb2-7b82, silver).
- The original scout-6e10 store line "≤ 0.6 GB + 37 MB/epoch": ≈ 8× optimistic; the real price is 4.8–5.2 GB/10⁹ at q24 (20261007-0711-second-20261007-0621-scout-6e10-ab9d, gold).
- The original fc22 line "≤ 1 MB per game": holds only in 3-D; the planar belts the engine ships pay ~200× more (20261007-0933-second-20261007-0842-fermi-fc22-a7d0, gold).
- Cadence by concentration (p99 clamp): the re-key rate is broad, waste ~70%; rank by predictability instead (20261009-1649-dynamics-ec3a, silver).
- The exact element memo on belt (E,L) keys: diffusion below q ≈ element range refills any cell (20261011-2306-dynamics-0b04, silver).
- The frozen birth program as a store-and-forget object: superseded by the phase-locked program (20261011-0006-second-20261010-2314-taleb-6baf-2b8e, gold); it dies by self-stirring alone within 11–12 orbits even with no planet (20261006-1354-dynamics-04d7, silver).
