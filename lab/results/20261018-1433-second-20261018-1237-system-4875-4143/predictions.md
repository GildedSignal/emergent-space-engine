   shipped control is 0.98 ms; the deletion must not regress the plane Julien flies).
6. **Seed 2 at 10⁴:** within load noise of seed 1 (±20% on p50).
7. **Engine identity:** the digest at the same cut is identical with and without `--rows`

   must repeat it).

   3×10⁴), landing in frame 1's gather max — the page's own first-frame cost, not the steady
   frame (2df1's finding; it must not hide in a warm-up here).

current form. A frame at 10–16 ms p50 would be a partial collapse: report as such, not gold.
