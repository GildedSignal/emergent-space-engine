# Dossier — 20261018-2155-quant-34be (quant lens)

**Claim.** The u64 `first_hit` port named "unpriced" in encounters od 1 is a measured trap:
the naive all-u64 port is **unsound at the belt's own magnitudes** (m ≈ 2³⁷; the compose
product m·y reaches ~2⁷⁴·⁴ and wraps — 166–215 wrong dues per 100,000 calls), the sound
form (u64 recursion, 128-bit compose) is exact on all 400,000 shipped-class tuples but wins
only **1.42–1.60×** (192→120, 108→76 ns/call), moving e87d's composed 416 ns/pair/kick row
by ~69/105 cores — a dead lever against the row's 10³ gap, killed by its own pre-registered
< 2× rule.

**Bottleneck.** Encounter work at 10⁹ (the board's #2 gap, ~10³–10⁴ cores → a few). od 1's
composed cached-section proposal prices at 416 ns/pair/kick (~105 cores); its named next
step was "the u64 `first_hit` port (all section values < 2⁶³), unpriced". Pricing it either
moves the row or closes the direction so the next run looks elsewhere.

**Evidence** (Linux/x86 i7-4790 @3.6 GHz, shared box, interleaved 12-batch medians; orders
of magnitude per lab rules; raw output in `run-output.txt`, regenerated by `regen.sh`,
~3 min):

- **The population is the shipped tier's own:** `make_belt` (n=2,000, σ_e=0.07, ρ=B=3.34e-6 AU,
  a∈3±w/2 for w ∈ {1e-4, 1e-5} AU, keys=0) × seeds 20261018/777; tuples (c0,d,pi,w) built
  by the exact lines of `tier::arm_pair` (tier.rs:550–571); 50,000 pairs → 100,000 tuples
  per arm, **sec/pair = 2.000** (the Cross two-arc structure), max pi = 1.402×10¹¹ ≈ 2³⁷,
  max w ≈ 8.9×10⁴ ticks.
- **Soundness census:** 0/400,000 tuples have any value ≥ 2⁶³ (od 1's parenthetical is
  true — and insufficient). Naive u64 port vs shipped u128: **166/180/215/206 mismatches
  per 100,000** (arm/seed order as above), identical recursion depths elsewhere. The
  mechanism is exact: `min_k` returns `(l + m·y + a − 1)/a` with y < a < m, so the product
  m·y < m² ≈ 2⁷⁴·⁴ — it fits u128, wraps in u64 whenever y ≳ 2⁶⁴/m ≈ 1.3×10¹⁰.
- **The sound port** (recursion state u64, the one compose multiply/divide kept 128-bit):
  **0 mismatches and identical step counts on all 400,000 tuples** (positive control), plus
  300/300 vs brute force on small random tuples; the shipped `dioph` tests pass (regen).
- **Price** (p50 ns/call, shipped u128 → sound u64): 192→120 and 193→122 (w=1e-4 arms;
  **1.58–1.60×**), 108→76 and 110→78 (w=1e-5 arms; **1.42×**); p99 within 2% of p50 in
  every arm (the tail is the recursion depth, nothing else).
- **Depth law (measured, and not what I predicted):** steps p50 4 / p99 8 / max 11 on the
  1e-4 arms; p50 2 / p99 5 / max 7 on the 1e-5 arms — the depth tracks the a-window (the
  period-spread structure of d), not the window w, and both are far below the ~log_φ(pi) ≈ 55
  a full Euclid run would take: the recursion usually exits at depth 1–2 because a hit needs
  only *some* remainder below w, not the full continued fraction [mechanism inference; the
  depth numbers are measured].
- **Row arithmetic:** e87d's composed proposal 416 ns/pair/kick ≈ 2×192 (first_hit, my
  shipped p50, sec/pair = 2) + ~30 ns maintenance — the two instruments agree. The sound
  port saves 2×(192−120) ≈ 144 ns → ~272 ns/pair/kick → **~69 cores** (105 × 272/416);
  the (unsound) naive port would have given ~64. Both are noise against the row's 10³ gap.

P3 falsified — win 1.42–1.60× vs 3–8× predicted. P4 near-miss — ~69 cores vs 30–70

**What would make it wrong.** On the M1 the u128/u64 gap could differ (the u128 division is
a `__udivti3` library call there too, so a similar factor is expected; the integrator
re-measures). If the deployed tuple class ever reaches pi ≳ 2⁶³ the sound port's u128
compose stays exact while the recursion itself would need widening — not the belt's class
(pi ≈ 2³⁷ at 3 AU; the gap to 2⁶³ is ~10⁷ in a). The depth law was measured on two a-arms;
a population with structured (keyed) periods could deepen it, which would *raise* the u128
cost and the win proportionally — bounded by max depth 11 measured, still ≪ 2× on this
machine unless depth × u128/u64-level cost changes qualitatively.

**Cost of adopting / not adopting.** Adopting the sound port: a width change inside
`dioph.rs` only, zero law change (bit-identical outputs proven on 400k shipped-class tuples
+ brute); it buys ~1.5× on one term of a 416 ns/pair/kick row. Recommendation: **do not
adopt** — PRINCIPLES 1 ("the cheapest compute is compute not done") says spend the step on
a lever that moves the row's order of magnitude, not on 1.5× of a sub-term. The value
banked here is the trap: nobody should now wire the naive port from od 1's parenthetical
("all section values < 2⁶³"), which reads as a green light and is one.

**Fit with PRINCIPLES.** P26/"steal from skin in the game": the multiple-testing rule and

instruction anatomy — the mechanism (`__udivti3` vs native `div`) is inferred from the
binary shape, not measured cycle-by-cycle.
