(structure, composition, scale). The Opus taste memo (`lab/refs/opus-math/memo.md`)

## Claim (one sentence)

Three macro structures are adoptable now and measured here: **(A)** the mass field
of a doll as an integer multiresolution transform whose own-scale quadrupole
channel both accepts and bounds each node's far-field error (a dimensionless,
pure-integer criterion that beats Barnes–Hut's θ at matched error and is
order-independent in exact arithmetic); **(B)** the doll atlas as a discrete
principal bundle — handoff parameters derived from one per-frame table close
exactly, pairwise pure boosts leak the Wigner angle (~10⁻²·|s| per loop at β≈0.3,
a theorem not a rounding), and integer shear loops drift linearly (exponent 0.99)
unless each shear carries its sigma-delta remainder; **(C)** the cycle memo's
already-stored ∂/∂E, ∂/∂L make its own keys self-sizing: the bare quantized cell
is never behaviour-exact (sensitivity ≈ 3/(1−E²) ≈ 1.5p), but the first-order jet
is exact to ≤ 2⁻⁴⁶ relative across the whole bound plane.

## Bottleneck

(A) attacks board focus 1 (fewer, cheaper kick terms; B15 "gravity nearly free"):
measured 132–136 terms per query at ~10⁻⁴ error vs 4096 direct (30×), vs
Barnes–Hut θ=0.5's 166–171 at the same error, with all-integer tests and no
geometric θ. (B) attacks the plane's judged gate B12 (Jacobi exponent ≤ 0.2 at
≥ 20 doll crossings) and the 0.9c stream of B15. (C) sizes the strong-field
memo/jet that law.md line 35 commits to, before Astra's compiler pass (9 October).
Order-of-magnitude gains: (A) ~10–30× fewer kick source terms than per-body sums
at fixed error; (B) prevents a systematic ~1%·n drift at relativistic doll loops
(turns a lurking B12 failure into ≤ ~1 unit per 20 crossings); (C) deletes the
search for a safe fixed key width (there is none: the answer is jet + adaptive
width).

## The ranked catalogue

Ranked by gain × probability ÷ cost of adoption. Structures marked ✗ were
considered and killed without a probe, with the reason.

1. **Integer multiresolution moments (Haar/Alpert-style lifting on the dyadic
   doll tree) — probe P1, run.** The doll's mass field as (M, D, Q) integer
   moments per node; the *detail* at each level is the children's moment defect;
   a node's far field may be read as its monopole at its exact integer COM once
   its own-scale quadrupole satisfies Q·S² ≤ ε·M·d² (all integers; ε dyadic).
   Sub-quantum structure is not discarded — the parent's monopole includes every
   unit of mass exactly, so ε trades field accuracy, never conservation; that is
   principle 6 ("influences below one quantum accumulate") made structural, and
   the same transform is the forest bookkeeping that keeps nested kicks from
   double counting (Connes–Kreimer's rooted-tree algebra is this bookkeeping in
   the abstract; the linear transform is its cheap realization). Skin in the
   game: wavelet-multipole BEM (Alpert 1993 bases; Beylkin–Coifman–Rokhlin),
   JPEG2000's integer lifting. Exact in integers: yes (P1's determinism control).
   What it deletes/replaces: the per-cell moment storage and any per-neighbour
   kick loop ("kick sources are a function, not a trait" — HANDOFF's open item —
   becomes a homomorphism: sources compose by integer addition at each level);
   block floating point fits because P1 measures ≤ 6.1 bits of detail spread per
   level (bottleneck 0's "level as shared exponent" holds). Probe: P1 below.
   Cheapest kill: erratic error vs ε, or no sparsity on smooth scenes.

2. **The doll atlas as a discrete principal bundle (transition maps + connection)
   — probe P2, run.** Handoffs are transition functions between doll frames; a
   consistent atlas (all pairwise maps derived as exact products of per-frame
   maps) closes every loop exactly; an atlas of independently quantized pairwise
   pure boosts cannot close, by the Wigner-rotation theorem — the leak is
   physical size (β²), not quantization. The integer shear realization (R3)
   composes loops with a linear floor drift (measured exponent 0.989) that a
   per-shear sigma-delta remainder removes (66× at 10³ loops); the residual drift
   (0.055 units/loop) is exactly the pair-parameter rounding, negligible for
   B12's 20 crossings (~1 unit). This is the load-bearing residue of sheaf theory
   for us: not vocabulary — one measurable defect and its ledger. Skin in the
   game: Wigner/Thomas rotation (every HEP frame-analysis pipeline); Čech
   cocycles/connections are textbook GR (holonomy = precession). Exact in
   integers: yes, with the carry. What it deletes: any temptation to quantize
   per-pair handoffs; prescribes the atlas table (per doll, one quantized
   Lorentz matrix) + the carry. Probe: P2 below.

3. **Self-sizing memo keys from the cycle's own stored derivatives (the
   ultrametric error algebra, applied) — probe P3, run.** Sharing one compiled
   cycle across a (E, L) cell is exact only within one output quantum; the
   sensitivity field of the Schwarzschild cycle map says the bare cell never
   qualifies at useful widths (period sensitivity to E is 3/(1−E²) ≈ 1.5p), but
   the first-order jet (offset + the ∂/∂E, ∂/∂L already stored per law.md line
   14) is exact to ≤ 2⁻⁴⁶ relative everywhere measured — so the derivatives,
   read at compile time, ARE the key-width certificate (a dyadic adaptive key).
   This is the macro error law: every operation 1-Lipschitz in a dyadic
   valuation ("errors never compound" as a per-operation checkable property),
   and the memo-cell question answered where it lives. Skin in the game:
   proper-element catalogues (Milani; millions of asteroids reuse mean elements
   + corrections), non-Archimedean/idempotent analysis (Maslov, cited by
   representation.md already). Probe: P3 below.

4. **Max-plus spectral certificate for the frame budget (no probe; adopt with
   R4's log).** The plane's per-frame work is a dependency graph; its worst-case
   steady frame is the max-plus eigenvalue (critical circuit) of that graph —
   the same object VLSI static timing analysis and railway timetabling
   (Cohen–Gaubert–Quadrat; SNCF/Nederlandse Spoorwegen in production) use for
   clock period and cycle time. Gain: turns "p99 is a hope" into a certificate
   computed from the same event log Opus's R4 probe already prescribes; zero new
   runtime cost. Cost: a pass over the log. Not probed here because the claim is
   a theorem; the falsifiable part (is the plane's critical circuit short enough
   to matter?) needs the plane's log, which only the builder has.

5. **Low-discrepancy idle-time prefetch (van der Corput/bit-reversal) — no
   probe.** Deferrable work (cycle compiles ahead of need) is known ahead from
   the orbits themselves; ordering its idle-time execution by bit-reversal
   spreads coincident compile bursts deterministically with zero coordination.
   Skin: quasi-Monte Carlo (finance), Morton-order layouts (already in
   representation.md for space; this is the time-side twin). Outcome-dependent
   on a real arrival trace; a synthetic demo here would be a strawman, so it
   stays a catalogue lead for the plane's log.

6. **rANS (asymmetric numeral systems) for the scar layer — no probe.** The
   belt gold (components-98bc) is 0.126 bits/body for the program; scars are
   the open cost. rANS is exact-integer, stream-optimal and incrementally
   updatable, with the strongest possible skin (Zstd, LZFSE — shipped on
   billions of devices). Expected 1.2–2× over Elias–Fano on skewed scar
   streams; below the 10× gold bar, so it waits for B18b's churn distribution
   to exist. Not measured here because a synthetic churn would beg the question.

7. **Answering Julien's set-theory question at the macro level (no).** The one
   macro-shaped piece, well-founded trees, is already the doll tree (Mostowski
   collapse is its name); bisimulation-minimal descriptions add nothing over
   "identity is provenance". This confirms the Opus memo's verdict from the
   other end.

8. ✗ **Category theory as a framework (operads, wiring diagrams, polynomial
   functors, sheaf cohomology beyond item 2's cocycle).** Agrees with the Opus
   memo: adds text, deletes no compute. The two places it earns its keep are
   already extracted: commuting squares as tests (Opus's habit — P1's
   determinism check and P2's identity controls are exactly such squares), and
   item 2's transition-map consistency. Functors-between-scales language is
   descriptive; the physics answer at each scale (Binet cone, rings, Fermi
   frames) already found its own water.

9. ✗ **Carry-save / redundant number systems for order-independence.** ℤ
   addition is already associative and commutative exactly; the tie rule exists
   because events *interact*, not because sums are inexact. Solves a non-problem.

10. ✗ **RG that averages.** Any coarse-graining that replaces individuals with
    moments of a distribution is banned by the goal ("NO Elite style"); item 1
    is the only admissible renormalization: exact integer aggregation where
    every body stays real and the sub-quantum remainder is carried, never lost.

|---|---|---|---|---|

- C3 = accept iff (not containing the query) and qn·S² ≤ ε·M·d², all int64 with
  per-level normalization (Q stored as Q≫2·level so every quantity fits 32-bit
  packets). At matched error (~1e-4) C3 uses 1.2–1.3× fewer terms than
  Barnes–Hut θ=0.5 with no float criterion; error is ε-controlled (belt:
  1.0e-4 → 2.5e-5 → 8.0e-6 → 5.0e-7 as ε falls 4× each step).
- Negative controls behaved as such: C1 (the dipole channel, the naive "detail")
  accepts almost nothing on structured scenes — the dipole is already cancelled
  by evaluating at the COM; the *quadrupole* channel is the correct own-scale
  detail. C2 (concentration alone, no distance) needs ε ≥ 1/12 even for a line
  mass and still reads ~4000 terms: distance must be in the criterion.
- Determinism: the integer criterion and its node tally are bit-identical under
  randomized child orders (3/3 scenes); the same float sums in shuffled order
  differ (3/3). The order-independent check must itself be a commutative monoid
  sum — a rolling sequence hash fails its own test (first implementation did).
- Per-level detail spread ≤ 6.1 bits (p1–p99): 32-bit level-indexed packets hold.

### P2 — handoff loops of three dolls (β_AB=0.3, β_AC≈0.32 at 51°; shears at n=24)

- Controls: out-and-back exact 200/200 (3-stage pairwise and 4-stage derived),
  reproducing R3 — **but only with the inverse that subtracts the same floored
  amounts**; negated coefficients are NOT the exact inverse of a floored shear
  (floor(−x) ≠ −floor(x)). This subtlety belongs in the plane's handoff code.
- D1 (atlas consistency, exact rationals, no floors): independently quantized
  pairwise **pure boosts** leak 10,376 units (≈0.7% of |s|) after ONE loop —
  the Wigner rotation of the loop, a theorem: no choice of pairwise pure-boost
  parameters closes a non-collinear loop. Pair maps derived as exact products
  of per-frame quantized matrices close the loop **exactly** (bit-exact identity
  in ℚ; decomposition reconstruction 2.2e-16 in float).
- D2 (floor holonomy, derived atlas): without carry, defect grows 3 → 3852 over
  1024 loops, exponent 0.989 (linear). With a per-shear sigma-delta remainder:
  3 → 58; the residual slope (0.055 units/loop) matches the pair-parameter
  rounding alone — floors contribute nothing once carried. For B12's ≥20
  crossings the total drift is ~1 unit.
- Also measured: rotations near π must be split into an exact negation plus a
  residual (tan(θ/2) amplification blew one intermediate state to 10¹⁶ units
  before the split; Opus's R3 note already warned this — here it is measured).

### P3 — sensitivity of the Schwarzschild cycle map (882/900 grid points, p=7…1000 M, e=0.01…0.99)

- At a strict one-ulp(32) output quantum, the safe cell width is 2⁻³⁷…2⁻⁴⁴
  (median 2⁻⁴⁰·⁹): **the bare quantized cell is never law-exact at practical
  widths** — dominated by the period's E-sensitivity 3/(1−E²) (≈1.5p in the
  weakly-bound limit), not by a separatrix boundary layer (my prediction 2 was
  wrong about the shape: the whole weakly-bound plane is the hard region).
- At a behaviour quantum of 2⁻¹⁰ (gate B2's 0.1%), the safe width is ≈ 2⁻¹⁹±² —
  so a ~20-bit key is behaviour-safe in the bulk.
- **The jet is exact everywhere measured**: at w = w_safe the first-order
  residual is ≤ 2⁻⁴⁶ relative (median; 100% of points below 2⁻³²). The stored
  ∂/∂E, ∂/∂L turn a ~20-bit key into an exact shared cycle; the bare cell
  re-rounds the orbit (the 5 October memo finding), the jet does not.
- Convergence control: GL-512 vs GL-2048 agree to 1.4e-16 on Δφ at the worst
  point (p=1000, e=0.99). The sensitivity field is smooth in p (no fractal
  contours), so a dyadic width from the derivative's leading bits suffices.

## What would make it wrong

P1: the scenes are synthetic; a real Zipf-fleet trace with sharper filaments
could shrink the sparsity gain (the ε-control and determinism are structural
and would survive). The kernel is 2D log; the 3D/1r case changes constants, not
the criterion. P2: three dolls at β ≤ 0.9 with integer coordinates; highly
relativistic loops (β → 0.999) or many-doll chains could make the parameter
rounding the dominant term (measured slope would steepen; the carry still
removes floor drift). P3: float quadrature judging behaviour/statistics, not
bits; the jet-exactness claim is about the continuum map's curvature over a
cell, and integer fixed-point roundings inside the jet evaluation itself were
not modelled (the crate's own tables decide that).

## What this lens may have made me miss

A structure lens sees structures everywhere, and the two most expensive blind
spots here are the ones it is worst at: first, **dynamics that have no group or
monoid at all** — resonance, chaos and the non-hierarchical three-body regime,
where the board already knows the answer is a rotating doll, not an algebra;
nothing in this catalogue helps there, and a lens-primed reader might over-trust
exact-composition arguments exactly where phase feedback rules (r4's kill).
Second, **the player**: every structure here is observer-free, but the game's
distinctive costs (the gather, perception thresholds, the minimap-as-knowledge)
are representation problems about *information held by an observer*, closer to
lenses/optics than to atlases, and I set them aside as "already adopted" on the
Opus memo's word. Also missed by construction: everything below the scale of my
scripts — the micro number forms, carry widths and table arithmetic the crate
already owns; and I probed no GPU/SIMD shape, so the packet-fit claim (≤ 6.1
bits spread) is a measurement of the scenes, not of a layout.
