## The five lessons (deliverable, ≤ 40 lines)

1. **Feynman — a different box of tools.** "So I got a great reputation for doing integrals, only because my box of tools was different from everybody else's…" (Surely You're Joking). *Mechanism:* pre-computer methods (CF/Diophantine) answer questions modern machinery doesn't ask. *Change:* tested as the CF conjunction calendar — **killed at belt scale this run**; keep CF for the O(1) recurrence hint only. *Test:* `python3 probe_cf.py metrics.json`.
2. **Bostrom — exploit structure, not enumeration.** "To overcome the combinatorial explosion, one needs algorithms that exploit structure in the target domain… flexible abstract representations" (Superintelligence). *Mechanism:* O(N²) dies only if the certificate rides a domain invariant. *Change:* commensurability class fails as the invariant (92% universal ⇒ selects nothing); radial-envelope disjointness works (29% group no). *Test:* `python3 census.py`.
3. **Waitzkin — gather and release intensity.** "…the better we are at recovering, the greater potential we have to endure and perform under stress" (The Art of Learning, his 5★). *Mechanism:* endurance is recovery-limited, not output-limited; bursts + recovery beat steady drip. *Change (q-cadence):* align kick bursts with the orbit's own surprise peak (periapsis) and precompute in the coast — p99, not mean. *Test:* the queued q-cadence rung.
4. **Taleb/Buffett — the high filter comes first.** "…establishing a high filter, then picking opportunities that pass such threshold… really successful people say no to almost everything" (The Logic of Risk Taking). *Mechanism:* bulk rejection before any per-item cost. *Change:* the encounter tier's first gate must be a group property (envelopes disjoint), never per-pair geometry — per-pair work only for the 5.3% skin. *Test:* `python3 census.py`.
5. **Asimov — long searches find anything.** "Anything could be found in figures if the search were long enough and hard enough and if the proper pieces of information were ignored or overlooked" (The Naked Sun). *Mechanism:* uncontrolled metrics dredge false structure. *Change:* every rung criterion states its random-baseline control (here: rank_rand ≈ 0.48; sharp-convergent baseline 92.5% ⇒ vacuous selector). *Test:* this run's pre-registered predictions.md + controls.
