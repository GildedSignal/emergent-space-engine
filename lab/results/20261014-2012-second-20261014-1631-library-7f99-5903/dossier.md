## What would make it wrong

The digest witnesses only *logged* encounters on the arms run; a miss that produces no encounter
within a flight is invisible (all arms here log encounters, 128–3,946). The horizon default is
calibrated to these scenes' coarsest cadence; another scene silently falls to walk-all (exact, slow;
the census's `horizon` counter watched it: 0 outside the control). The chatter arm runs 18d2's
branch (6 October, lead-graded) plus this run's 887-line probe; its numbers describe that
configuration, and 193-vs-210 is the case's own noise floor. All costs are Linux/shared-box orders
of magnitude; the M1 re-measures.
