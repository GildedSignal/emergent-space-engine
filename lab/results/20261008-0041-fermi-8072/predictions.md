# Predictions (written before the decisive runs)

Scene: Sun (G=1, M=1), Jupiter-like (m=9.54×10⁻⁴ M, a=5.2 AU, e=0.048) on an exact analytic Kepler orbit (restricted problem), belt of N=2,000 massless bodies, banded a ∈ [2.2, 3.4] AU, Rayleigh e (σ=0.05), uniform angles — the B18b family. Test-particle Wisdom–Holman map: exact Kepler drift under the Sun, Jupiter impulse kicks, dt = P_J/64 ≈ 0.185 yr. Run length ~8×10³ Jupiter orbits (~95 kyr), snapshots every 128 orbits. All thresholds fixed now.

- **P1 (chaos marker).** By the end, ≥ 3% of bodies show |Δa|/a > 10⁻⁴ (Δa is the chaos marker: secular theory conserves a), concentrated within ~0.05 AU of the 3:1 (2.50 AU), 5:2 (2.82 AU) and 2:1 (3.27 AU) resonances.
- **P2 (static program scars everyone).** With the program frozen at birth, ≥ 80% of bodies leave the birth quantum cell in (e cos ϖ, e sin ϖ) within ~2×10³ Jupiter orbits (free-e precession period ~10–20 kyr); with a per-band nonparametric refit, the outside-quantum fraction stays below ~5% at all epochs (the ensemble drift is absorbed; the per-body phase is regenerable by the program's PRNG).
- **P3 (shuffle legality — the decisive one).** Take the true belt at T/2, build a synthetic belt from a fitted program (a: smoothed histogram; e: per-band empirical; angles: uniform redraw; labels: fresh ordinal draw), evolve BOTH to T. Predict: KS distance between synthetic-T and true-T in a and e within 3× the run-to-run noise (two independent true runs with different birth seeds), and scattered-body counts (a > 4 AU or e > 0.3) within 3σ. If this fails, the program family cannot hold the belt's fine structure and H_live grows with structure formation.
- **P4 (held out; the checker pushes here).** Gap depth near the 3:1 (fraction of bodies within 0.03 AU of 2.50 AU relative to start) grows monotonically past 2× its initial noise by T, and the synthetic run matches it within noise at T. I predict both hold at 95 kyr; at 10× longer (≈1 Myr) I expect P3 to get harder (resonant-island stickiness is real structure a smooth family cannot hold) — not tested here, stated as the boundary of the claim.
- **Kill criteria.** If P3 fails at these scales: the "shuffle makes H_live ≈ 0" path is dead for belts; the honest verdict is the no-shuffle formula f_chaos × 128 bits. If P1 shows f_chaos > 50% at 95 kyr, belts scar wholesale and the identity-preserving path fails too.
- **Controls.** Two independent true runs (different birth seeds) set the noise floor for every comparison; a control belt evolved with NO Jupiter must show |Δa|/a < 10⁻⁸ for all bodies at all times (pure drift; validates the Kepler drift's numerics).

All comparisons at orders of magnitude for time; fractions exact under float64 exploration (floats explore here, the verdict formula is what ships).
