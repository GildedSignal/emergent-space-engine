# Predictions (written before the decisive runs)

The experiment (the checker's named one): root-doll ship clocks on a **dyadic grid** — the step a
power of two of ticks (the clock's own step rounded **down**, so no interval is longer than the
law's own), every interval on its own grid (kicks at `k·H + H/2`), the kick's weight the exact
physical time of the interval it closes (`time_of(len)` — the drift ran it), so bodies of one
state class fire at one calendar key without any family bookkeeping. Pure law change in
`plan_clock`/`kick_clock`; no new per-body state (the floor's memory point).

Cases I have not tuned on, and what I expect there:

1. **Census, r = 1 AU, F = 16, arms δx = 10²–10⁶ (the b560 harness, unchanged):** per-arm mean
   distinct dues drops from 16.00 to **≤ 2** (one shared key per epoch; a fork transient only
   when a member's own step crosses a factor of two), `steps_eq` becomes true in every arm, and
   anchors equal after each member's first grid interval. Through 300 fires the co-fire index
   fraction per arm is ≥ 0.99. The six arms (different base phases, so different tide) may hold
   up to 6 keys among the 96 ships — the sharing class is the step, not the family.
2. **Fires per member** (the cost of rounding down): within **2×** of the shipped chain, and I
   expect the median ratio ≈ 1.2–1.5× (the grid step is the exact step's octave floor). If the
   ratio exceeds 2× on any arm, the floor-rounding choice is wrong and nearest-rounding is the
   candidate instead.
3. **The gates (`behave --law plane --gates B12,B13,B17`):** every verdict identical to the
   shipped plane's (PASS→PASS, FAIL→FAIL). B12's worst Jacobi error within one order of
   magnitude of shipped (the step is never longer than the law's own and the kick weight is the
   exact elapsed time, so the error type — a per-step shadow offset that cannot compound — is
   unchanged).
4. **Kill criterion:** any gate verdict flipping PASS→FAIL kills the grid as a plane-wide clock
   change (the configuration, not the method); the census sharing alone would then be a priced,
   ungated lead.

The unit I will not restate wrong: δx = 10² root = 148 km (1 root = 1.477 km), the b560 dossier's
"1.5 km" was off by 100×.
