## What would make it wrong

is one horizon; the long-lived-battle and multi-wave cases are unflown. The seed band uses
two seeds (one band width). The instrument judges the population at fixed N=1000, coast,
one scenario (`plane_extra`); the fleet's members and the belt are root-doll (untouched by
the cure by construction). If the checker finds the drift median band-crossing on a third
seed, the "tight band" premise dies and the median channel is noise-labeled instead.
