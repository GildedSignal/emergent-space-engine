## What the author missed

- The stated hot/cold bands silently drop two of their own six keys (e=0.01 hot ≈2.0, p=7 hot 5.0–5.2 and cold 8.2–8.7); the flat **delta** is the real invariant and needed no narrowing.

- The mix envelope is wider than shown (9.6–19.7 KB over N=10³–3×10⁴); one cheap wave arm each side of 3000/10000 would have closed it — I ran them, and they only widen the envelope.
- The data-only cold tax falls toward zero as knots grow (0.6 µs at 124, ≈0 at 230): the per-key table is ever more prefetch-friendly — worth one line, since it kills the layout lever harder than claimed.
- No cross-citation of memory-h-live's compute ≫ bandwidth ordering (the belt's persistence stream had already suggested the ordering at 10⁹; this run proves it for the event path with counted bytes — the two together close PRINCIPLES 17 for both memory halves).
- The code/statics/allocator attribution is by elimination; a perf-counter box would close it (acknowledged in the dossier's lens section).
