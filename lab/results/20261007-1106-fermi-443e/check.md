Grade: silver

**Abstract.** Measured on the plane (28 doll bodies, 3,600 game-s, new instrument `hlive`): H_live is not the binding term at 10⁹. Each binding change injects ~104 near-incompressible bits (zlib saves 4.5–5.5%), residuals reabsorb (late/early 1.09–1.20 per hour; my 10-hour rerun: 1.01), worst all-inner cadence 182 MB/s against 60 GB/s, fill time 44 s only if reabsorption breaks. The walls it exposes: stored representation 9,846 B/body = 1,231× over 8 GB, and inner-cadence kick compute. Decisive run reproduced bit-identically (`cmp` equal); `fermi.py` regenerates every table line. Caveats: the 0.014 events/body/s anchor mixes lattice kicks with doll binding events (overestimates, safe direction); one prediction miss is tallied as held; one scene class only. Adoption cost: none; re-ranks the HANDOFF risk.

**Reasons.**

1. **Reproduction: exact.** Applied `work/patch.diff` to a clean tree, built (`cargo build --release -p emergent-universe --example hlive`, 4.7 s warm), ran `3600 1 400`: every printed number matches the dossier (28 bodies, 9,846 B/body, 1,582 kicks, 615 bindings, 323/292, residual median 21, delta median 20, ratio 1.09, 13.00 B/event) and the delta stream is bit-identical to the author's `.bin` (`cmp`). Variants (1800/1, 3600/2, 3600/7) match the dossier ranges 0.010–0.018, 1.09–1.20. `python3 fermi.py` reproduces 182 MB/s, 1,231×, 508×, 44 s, 168/924 cores, 0.02 core·s/s. zlib on the four saved streams saves 4.5–5.5% (claim "~5%" confirmed).
2. **Pre-registration: real, tally flawed.** `predictions.md` mtime 11:09:43 precedes every output artifact (first `.bin` 11:12:00). But the dossier counts "residual bits (21 vs 40–100)" as held — 21 is outside 40–100; it is a third miss (tally should be 5 held / 3 missed, not 6/2). The number is stated openly in both files, so this is a miscount, not concealment, and it points in the safe direction (residuals smaller than modelled). The two big misses (bytes/body, compressibility) are disclosed honestly. The author's own kill criterion (bytes/body > 1 kB) fired; the reassignment of the fired part to the stored-representation term (shipmem/B18) is sound but is the author reinterpreting their own pre-registered criterion.
3. **Adversarial cases (three, all held).**
   - 10× horizon (36,000 game-s, seed 1): ratio tightens to 1.01 (76.7 vs 77.5), delta bits unchanged (median 20, 13.01 B/event). bytes/body grows to 45,239 — the "held today" term is run-length-dependent (cache grows linearly), supporting the author's "cache, not information" reading, but 1,231× is the 1-hour point, not a constant.
   - 10× sampling density (samples 400→4,000): bits/event unchanged (104.07) — not a sampling artifact; bindings saturate at 618.
   - 10× denser lattice fleet (members 24→240, my own `hlive_dense` variant): the binding stream stays bit-identical while `stats.kicks` scales 1,582→6,036. This proves `stats.kicks` counts lattice contact kicks, not doll binding changes: the "0.014 events/body/game-s" anchor attributes fleet kicks to 28 doll bodies. The doll binding-event rate is 615/28/3,600 ≈ 0.006/s, so the all-inner creation figure is ~2.3× high (≈79 MB/s at 10⁹) — an overestimate in the safe direction; the verdict (≥300× under bandwidth) is unaffected, and the two populations should not be mixed in the anchor.
4. **Novelty: first measurement of H_live.** HANDOFF carries the risk "not measured yet"; `docs/law.md` defines H_live for the allocator and `docs/plane.md` carries the outside review's premise ("This, not the body count, decides whether a billion bodies fit"). No HISTORY entry measures it; `residual_bits.rs` (7f81a9c) measured only static residual histograms — the delta (new info per event), reabsorption ratio and rate/budget layer are new. This is the queue's assigned `H_live` task, done.
5. **Goal fit.** Moves the board item "fitting 10⁹ in 8 GB … decided by distinctions created per game-second": the premise is corrected on this scene class — H_live is bounded and ~300× under bandwidth even at the worst cadence; what decides is the stored representation (1,231×) and inner cadence. Fits PRINCIPLES ("The memory floor is information-theoretic"; "Measure before claiming"); bits are exact integer counts, machine-independent. Not a 10× engine gain — a measurement that redirects the next one, as the dossier itself states.

**Why silver, not gold.** The premise correction is measured on one scene class (28 doll bodies, zero handoffs, no belt, no concentrated fleet — where the co-orbital gold says live scars concentrate), the rate anchor mixes two event populations (kicks vs binding changes), and the prediction tally misreports one miss as a hold — the lab's own rule says a headline the log contradicts costs the grade.

**The one experiment to gold.** Run `hlive` on a concentrated belt/fleet scene (B18b scale, 10³–10⁶ bodies, handoffs present — e.g. the `restricted` scene class), rate anchored per doll binding event with kicks-per-binding reported, predictions pre-registered. Bounded ratio there generalizes the premise kill; a clean tally on that run moots the miscount.
