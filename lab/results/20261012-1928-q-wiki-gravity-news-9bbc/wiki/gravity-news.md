## Refuted

- The frozen single-version ghost: structurally unstable — unbound after 5.05 orbits, |dE| ~310× (20261005-1540).
- The cursor's cost gain: ~8× only at sparse news; negative at dense news (99 vs 18 tests/kick against a cursor-seeded binary search); composed news-kick cost unmeasured (20261005-1540 checker).
- Plain per-cell accumulators: three distinct compounding leaks; the naive cell layer is dead (20261010-0920).
- "Near-stationary" deferral under the FMM-shell partition: a window artifact — past the light-cone fill the error walks ~t^0.5; and the cost is O(log N), not the analytic O(1) (20261010-1029).
- The toy's log₂R accumulator growth: on engine kicks impulse news is 1/d³, so O(1) saturation (20261008-1947).
- "≤3.4 source-cell light-crossings at every separation": max 68.6 source-cell; ≤3.02 larger-cell is the true unit (20261008-0148; 20261008-0234).
- The float event-time cell's published never-early: a shared-reference artifact — it is early by up to 5.3×10⁻² yr; the static integer lag table is the never-early mechanism (20261008-0234).
- The hierarchical gate's flat 3.9×10⁴ events/unit as a measured bound: it is the assumed one-news-per-source-orbit cap; uncapped it is 3.6×10⁷ higher ≈ pairwise (20261007-1918 checker).
- Dipole-only (ℓ=1) far-field grading: fails the energy rule at every rate, and seed 2 at 24L (2.5×10⁻³) (20261010-1544 checker).
- "The far gravity-news cone saturates, 0–2 events/orbit": degenerate metric (mean-subtracted rows sum to identically zero; 4.3×10⁻¹⁷ residue on a 1.16 force scale); honest variants invert it (~3×10⁴ events/orbit at ε 10⁻⁴) (20261009-0709).
