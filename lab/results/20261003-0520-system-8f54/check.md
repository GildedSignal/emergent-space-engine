Grade: silver

**Abstract.** Claim: the plane's kick layer re-fits and re-compiles a geodesic on 99.6% of firing kicks although 98.7% of rebinds stay in one memo cell; landing kicks on (E, L) deletes the fit+compile (~36% of the 29 µs kick). Reproduced exactly on a clean tree with the patch: rebinds 27721, fired 27694/27814, same-cell 27346, memo hits 24443 on / 0 off, 49596 items identical with memo on and off over 3×10¹⁰ ticks; an independent seed replicates all rates (98.6%, 87.9%). Gain is unpriced in wall time (load), and the wired memo saves only ~3% of compile here. Adoption cost: one owner rewires `kick`; the circle branch must be fixed first.

**Reasons.**
- Reproduction: every count bit-exact (see abstract); timings 3× under container load, but the fit+compile share (41% here, 36% author) is proportionally consistent. `cargo test -p emergent-universe` with the prototype: 18 passed, 0 failed, 1 ignored (author's "19" counts the ignored test).
- Predictions: `predictions.md` predates the dossier (file times; ranges, not fitted points). P1, P2 (the held-out core: 98.7% ≥ predicted 90%), P3 held. P4's only measurable claim (≥10% cost cut) was never measured and is honestly reported as such.
- Adversarial 1 (bit-identity hole): `Orbit::compile` has a symbolic-circle branch (k = UNIT, 1/6 ≤ q < 1/3) that `ShapeMemo::get` lacks; my test shows `get` returns `NoBoundOrbit` for p ∈ {3.5, 4, 5, 5.9} where `compile` succeeds, and `fit_key`'s own `circular(state)` path produces exactly k = 1<<48, so the divergence is reachable. "Bit-identical by construction" holds only on the generic branch (verified exact there). One-line fix, but the prototype is not adoptable as shipped.
- Adversarial 2 (the memo is not the win): compile avg 10.5 µs (memo on) vs 10.8 (off) — the quadrature `OrbitShape::bound` is a small part of compile; `from_shape`'s scalar algebra dominates. The 88% hit rate translates to ~1% of the kick on this box. The dossier's deletion value therefore lives entirely in skipping `from_state` per kick — which is its real claim, and it survives.
- Adversarial 3 (arithmetic): "~2^14× over-paid at the memo's own resolution" is wrong. `memo_key` keeps 12 significand bits (`MEMO_SHAPE_BITS = 12`), so a cell at k's exponent spans ~2^36 of the Q48 word; avg dk 2^34 gives ~4× per rebind, not 2^14×. The measured 98.7% stands; the derived exponent is inflated. Minor: `memo_key().ok() ==` counts both-Err as same-cell (passages), and the bench's 3000-iteration loop on one cloned record inflates the from_state counters (universe-only same-cell ≈ 98.6%, hits ≈ 86.7% — immaterial).
- Scene coverage: sparse 8-ship scenes only (two seeds). The dense-64 scene exceeded this container's budget too; untested.
- Novelty: no HISTORY entry measures the rebind share, same-cell rate or memo hit rate; the mechanism itself is the documented design (representation.md §3 L67, §6 L114, L66; `ShapeMemo` already in `crates/space`), and the dossier says so. This is a quantified premise probe of board focus 1's other half (cost per kick), in the spirit of principle 19: "if you find yourself fixing things that's oftentimes a signal that it is the wrong representation" — the fit+compile is that fix.
- Not gold: the expected gain is unmeasured in wall time anywhere (the author names this), and my circle finding shows the shipped prototype would break exactly-circular strong-field bodies. Real, reproduced, one experiment away.

**The experiment that would make it gold:** land the kick on the invariants (δE = v·δv, δL = x×δv, rebind scheduled at memo-cell overflow, circle branch routed through or keyed out of the memo) on one body class, and measure the resolve cost against this run's 29 µs baseline on a quiet machine or the M1, judged by B5/B12 — plus the same-cell rate on a dense scene.

**What the author missed.** The symbolic-circle divergence (above); that `from_shape`, not the quadrature, dominates compile, so the memo wiring alone buys ~1% and the A/B named for gold would price the wrong thing; the 2^14 over-payment arithmetic; the both-Err and bench-loop counter caveats; no dense or high-eccentricity scene.
