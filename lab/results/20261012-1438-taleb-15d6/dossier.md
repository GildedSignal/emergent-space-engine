# Dossier — taleb-15d6: the train certificate is density-blind and key-blind; at its own headline scale (10⁶ in one cell) it silently drops most contacts

## Claim (one sentence)
The adopted co-orbital "train" certificate (gold 20261006-0346-taleb-09b5, queued for build as b-coorbital-cert) is sound only where fleet local occupation (bodies within ρ) stays below the watch size k: measured at the gold's own 10⁶-ship headline the k=4 watch misses 88% of contacts (rank p50 17; 43% even with the gold's own e-generator), a mean-longitude sort key — the cheap one a builder would pick, and one the gold's dossier never pins down — misses 83–99.5% of contacts at any density because of the equation of center, and the Binet-clump trigger reads identically on both sides of the break, so the switch fires and the contacts are dropped silently.

The premise — "every real encounter lies within partner rank 4" — was measured only where pile-ups are geometrically impossible (occupation 0.01–0.16) while the ruin arithmetic that motivated it is computed where occupation is 3–31. This run is the pre-freeze red-team the lab owes every gold (lab README: "Before any freeze, one GLM red-team and the cheapest falsifying probe must have run"), aimed at the freeze about to happen (b-coorbital-cert, in lab/queue.txt and built on wt/cx-b-coorbital-cert).

The invariant, derivable and now measured: a k-watch catches a contact iff fewer than k bodies are closer than the partner at some watch instant; at closest approach d ≤ ρ, those bodies are themselves within ρ. So **misses ⟺ pile-ups of ≥ k+1 bodies within ~ρ**, with expected occupation μ ≈ (2ρ/s)·(ρ/h), s = local along-track spacing, h = radial breathing ≈ max(2e·a, ρ). The gold's evidence and its headline sit on opposite sides of μ ≈ k.

## What would make it wrong
The census ranks are measured at a random instant inside contact episodes, not at the exact closest approach (closest-approach rank is if anything lower, so miss fractions are understated, not overstated); a certificate implemented as "watch k nearest by distance, continuously" with k large enough for measured occupation, refreshed on overtakes, would restore soundness — my claim is about the certificate as specified (k ≈ 4, a longitude sort, a Binet trigger), not about trains in general. The trigger reading is my reconstruction of the gold's formula (their exact eps normalisation is unpublished); but its density-blindness is structural, not a calibration artefact. The contact model is the gold's own (conjunctions below ρ in a mutual-gravity-free Kepler fleet): finding conjunctions is geometric, so this measures the certificate, not the contact law. If the encounters design already keys the train on true longitude and bounds occupation, this dossier reduces to a confirmation — no such statement exists in the gold's dossier, law.md, or the board.
