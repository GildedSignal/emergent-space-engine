## What would make it wrong

- **Seed independence is weak.** All scenes share the A/B random streams (fixed seeds; scenes differ only through e_B), so the three zero-miss runs are not independent draws. The window is analytic (seed-free) and the necessity chain is proved, but a multi-seed rung is required before freeze — that is the integrator's r8-style next step, not this run's.
- Retrograde or mutually inclined crossings are untested (the crate is 2D prograde); the chain's azimuthal-separation step should carry, the incidence geometry of λ̇_B needs a 3D check.
- Element drift: the certificate re-derives crossing longitudes and re-sorts per epoch; components-4643's set-up-re-pay finding says the re-pay question must be asked at game cadence — unmeasured here.
- Near-apsis crossings (B tangent to A's circle at dr/dλ → 0) make tol_b → the quadratic-root scale; my closed form handles it exactly in principle, but only γ = 0.8° was run — the γ → 0 limit with λ̇-variation second-order terms needs its own probe.

**Cost of adopting:** one route in the encounters design: when two concentrated groups' orbits cross (|p/r_A − 1| < e_B against the annulus), rank per-body crossing times with the necessity window — all closed forms on top of `passage_tick`/`at_longitude`/`travel`, ~150 lines in this harness, plus a lane-geometry trigger computed once per epoch.
