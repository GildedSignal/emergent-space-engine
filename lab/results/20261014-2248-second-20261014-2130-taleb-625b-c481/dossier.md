## What would make it wrong

The standing counter is capacity-based (overcounts the untouched Vec tail — the same slack that makes RSS lag);
attribution carries ~25%. The mint cadence at 10⁵ is measured under a collapsing presented time (frames 30 ms, rtf
overflows), so per-game-second rates there are presented-time rates. The burn direction is fixed (angle
0x2000_0000); a retrograde burn could detune the outward drift the crossing arms showed (+5.5×10⁻⁶ AU/s at 1.03 AU).
The sweep's boundary (windowed→walk-all between 1.85 and 1.9 at 10⁴, below 1.898 at 10⁵) is located to ~0.05 AU,
not pinned. If a fix lands that keeps the window from degenerating, every number here moves — that is the point of
measuring them.
