# predictions.md (written before the decisive run)

Instrument: f64 mirror of the shipped kick formulas (`force.rs::pull`, `events::kicks::parent_tide` full differential, `origin_fall`; both cancel or compose exactly as in `force::acceleration`), root units (GM_star = 1 root, c = 1), the plane's real constants from `scenario.rs` (Home 2^-18 at a = 1.0133e8, e 1/50; Giant 2^-10 at a = 5.263e8, e 1/20; Cinder 2^-21 at 0.39 AU; Moon 2^-24, periapsis 260,000 root, e 1/20; fleet lattice radius 4,740 root; belt 2-3 AU, e ~ 0.07). Word = the w11 family word (a877): quantum q = 2^-11 * v_body, sigma-delta carry at 2^-16 q (step.rs::quantize's scheme). Cadence h = P_own/16 (the board's own-orbit floor, kick-cadence cbe6). Held-out: none of the three domains' fork counts below were fitted; the pre-estimates use only tidal arithmetic.

- **P1 (control, star-doll 1 AU family, a877's domain):** first fork at dx = 1e6 root lands at 1e3-1e6 kicks (a877 measured 14,030; different h/H unknowns allowed, order must match), and 0 forks at dx <= 1e5 within 2^17 kicks. If forks appear already at dx 1e4 the instrument disagrees with a877 and the run reports that.
- **P2 (belt domains):** H_eff rises vs control by ~(4.2/2.7)^3 ~ 3-4x (Giant closer), so first-fork kicks fall by the same factor: belt families share to dx ~ 5e4-1e5 for a 2^17-kick horizon; the 3:1 band (2.5 AU) is not special for the word (tides, not resonance, drive the differential; resonance drives the mean motion, which is shared).
- **P3 (child doll, r = 4,740 root, the plane's fleet lattice):** the Moon's tide (H ~ GM_M/d_M^3 = 3.4e-21) plus the star's (9.6e-25) gives Xi(dx=1e4) ~ 0.1-0.3 per kick, so first fork at dx 1e4 within 1e2-1e4 kicks; at dx 1e3, Xi ~ 0.01-0.03, first fork beyond 1e5 kicks. The child-doll certificate is therefore ~10-100x shorter-lived than a877's 1 AU star-doll domain at equal dx.
- **P4 (the lattice side, same domain):** the linear-tide residue grows ~ dx^2 and becomes O(1 quantum) only as dx approaches the Moon's distance scale (2.6e5 root): the Fermi lattice's certificate holds past dx ~ 1e5, i.e. further out than the family word. Crossover (od 4): the tube-family does NOT beat the Fermi lattice inside Home's doll at any dx where the family word still holds; the family only wins where the lattice is structurally impossible (dx > ~1e5, near/inside the Moon's shell).
- **P5 (cost arithmetic):** with fork rate r(dx) and refinement = one member fit (~4.4 us, c4d3) + one calendar key, family fires per body per interval = 1/F + r; the family beats per-member bindings (1.1 events/body/interval) for any r < 1 at F >= 2 — the binding-memory row (2.4 KB -> ~2 B) is where the family's 10x lives, not the fires row.

Kill criteria: if P3's Xi at dx 1e4 measures < 1e-3, child-doll families are as cheap as star-doll ones and od 4's crossover is set by the lattice's death alone; if the lattice residue at dx 1e4 already exceeds the quantum, the lattice is dead at the plane's own fleet span and the family wins everywhere inside the Moon.
