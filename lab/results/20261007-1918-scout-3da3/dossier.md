**The candidate (best fit from the search; fields ranked in `search.md` notes below).**
- *Mechanism.* A consumer never tracks a distant source individually: at distance D it consumes aggregates of size s = θD (Barnes–Hut opening angle = principle 33's "a raindrop is nothing for a lion"). An aggregate's multipole random-walks as its members are kicked; the consumer's field has a quantum q = ε·v²/r, and news is published only on a quantum crossing — dead-reckoning thresholds exactly as DIS (IEEE 1278) publishes entity state only when the receivers' dead-reckoned extrapolation deviates beyond a threshold [VERIFIED: DIS tutorial, Prepar3d DIS docs]. Below-threshold deltas accumulate in the aggregate's ledger and flush on crossing — AMR "refluxing" [UNVERIFIED this run: Berger–Oliger lineage]. The extrapolation itself is free: members' **tubes** are the dead-reckoning model (law.md), so between kicks an aggregate's predicted multipoles are exact; only kicks are surprises.
- *Memory.* The observer's past light cone fixes every snapshot's age (a body at distance d is always seen d/c ago — geometry, not history), so versions are reclaimable once the oldest reader's cone has passed them — MVCC in databases [UNVERIFIED this run] and fossil collection at GVT in Time-Warp PDES [VERIFIED: Young, *Optimistic Fossil Collection for Time Warp Simulation*]. Retained window = the galaxy's light-crossing time, independent of simulated time T.
- *Why this maps to our cells and certificates.* The doll tree is the aggregation hierarchy; the integer multipole ledger per (doll, direction band) is the accumulator (exact, principle 16); the flush event is a sparse-order calendar event with slack = (quantum − accumulated)/(drift rate) — the queue's own key. One quantity governs both memory and traffic: the rate of **distinctions above consumers' quanta** — H_live again, now doubly load-bearing (b-h-live's measure should be defined per quantum band, not per body).
