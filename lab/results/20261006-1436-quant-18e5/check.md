**Abstract.** Claim: the frame budget bounds the queue, not the item — one engine item can run 25–30 ms past the 5 ms budget — and the unbudgeted view is the majority spender. Reproduced bit-for-bit: my rerun of the tail config (m480/l15) gives worst 37.5 ms, engine max 31.2 ms, digest identical to the author's (same cut_tick); the 20 ms budget dial gives 51 vs their 50 hitches; sizes audit exact (Seen 152 B); unpaced p50 0.698 vs their 0.689 ms. Adversarial: seed 2 shows no giant item (engine max 10.5 ms) — magnitude is seed-specific, mechanism (items pass the budget unchecked, universe.rs:808) holds on every run. Not reproduced: the paced 2.7–5× factor — on a quieter box it is 1.06× (load artifact). Gain: redirects allocator work (ordering cannot fix the worst frame); adoption cost ≈ one instrumentation line. A 31.2 ms engine frame under a 5 ms budget therefore implies one item ≥ ~26 ms. Verified, not just asserted.
3. **Predictions.** `predictions.md` (14:41:45) predates the decisive e/e2b runs (14:51). **The one experiment for gold.** Add max-per-kind to `Stats::cost_ns` (the author's own one line), rerun the tail config on ≥5 seeds plus loaded vs quiet, and name the kind inside `resolve_entity` that costs 25–30 ms; with the item named, the finding becomes an adoptable fix or kill of a specific engine item rather than a location.

**What the author missed.**
- The paced 2.7–5× "warmth" factor is a contention artifact, not pacing: their unpaced number matches mine across boxes (0.689/0.698 ms) while their paced a-run (3.46 ms) does not (my 0.73 ms at load 2/8) — the factor measures their shared window (3 runs), and on a player-like quiet box it is ~1.06×. metrics.json records 5.0/2.7 unqualified; the actionable reading ("unpaced µs flatters played cost") is likely false for the player's machine.
- The bench's `machine_before/after` probe is macOS-only and returned "unavailable" for every field on this Linux runner, so predictions.md's "machine state is recorded by the bench itself" is false — no load/thermal record exists for any run (against AGENTS.md's "record the state so runs compare honestly").
- Seed-dependence of the headline magnitude was testable at the headline config (m480/l15) and wasn't run; they varied seeds only at 4800/l0.
- "Median item cost ~17 µs" is a mean (engine CPU ÷ events), not a median; engine share at l15 was 28.9% in my run, above the stated 22–26% "every paced run" range.
