# Dossier — one missing slot, three consumers (run 20261013-0350-system-7b86)

## Claim

The plane pays three times for one missing piece of representation — a body's perturbing **acceleration** is never stored — and the shipped code even contains the missing primitive three times over (`observe::credit` from kick history, `observe::partial` from the field, `thrust::owed` for the burn) while the gravity version payload carries none of it; on shipped code, the per-read `force::field` evaluation that makes the view the frame's constraint computes a term **≤ 10⁻⁵ of the view's own read quantum** (exactly zero on plain scenes), so the view's copy of the primitive is deletable outright, and the same slot is what the gravity ring (7ab4) and the burn waterfall (174d) each independently asked for.

## Bottleneck

`frame-cost` / `view`: the view is the frame's constraint (56–93% of every frame; 95–97% the crossing loop; 97% of that the ramped readout; 81% of readout time field evaluations re-read per emitter per Newton step — view page, `51bf`). This run shows the term those evaluations compute is invisible to the instrument they serve, on every shipped scene measured. Deleting it needs no new mechanism: `partial` already mirrors `credit`'s shape; it re-derives per read what the last kick committed (its `dv`) — and where the field is nonzero, the stored-kick bound holds to ≤ 9.8×10⁻⁶ root ticks (position) and ≤ 9.1×10⁻¹² c (velocity) per read.

One slot, asked for by name in three bottlenecks.

## What would make it wrong

1. **Scenes where the secondaries' field at read windows exceeds ~1 read quantum**: strong-field dolls (7ab4's 20 M consumer), resonant chains, dense same-doll fleets. Unmeasured here; the deletion claim for the view is scoped to the plane's shipped scenes.
2. **The cost ladder is Linux/x86 bench truth** (view page, `51bf`/`f22e`); the integrator re-measures on the M1. If the field-eval share collapses there, the deletion buys little — but the zero-term result (plain scenes) makes it free either way.
3. **Outcomes must not flow through the cheap read**: judges and encounters read canonical tubes / their own oracles; only the view (floats, never an outcome, law.md §Observation) reads the cheap form. If a future outcome consumer reads the view's row, this split breaks and the slot must be exact at the field's quantum.

## Cost of adopting

The full slot (acceleration at the field's quantum in the binding, ~1 word) additionally serves 7ab4's version payload and 174d's burn generator — those adoptions are theirs to build; this run prices the view side only.

## The lens (what it may have missed)

The system lens unified by reading three wiki pages against one code path; it may have missed that `credit` and `partial` serve *different laws* (ramp bodies are not motion-law-v2), so "one primitive" is a family, not one function. The hard special case that could break the unification: a strong-field consumer needing `a` accurately (rel_p99 = 1.00 shows the kick's dv quantum is not that) — there the slot is new state, not a deletion, and its quantum must be derived from the certificate it serves, not from the kick's.
