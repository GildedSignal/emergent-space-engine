- `quad` (152 µs/kick) exceeds `compile` (107) because quadrature also fires in tube readouts (orbit.rs:671/717) and `tail_quad` in passage reads, outside the wrapped compile — so "compile" actually understates the quadrature total; the panel-count story is even stronger than stated.

- The patch drops the memo JSON (`shapes.json()`) from kick-bench output in favor of phases — the two are not mutually exclusive, and the memo hit-rate is evidence the next runs will want.
