**Reasons.**

1. **Reproduction: exact.** `rustc -O replica.rs -o replica && ./replica ref && ./replica sweep` in a clean tree reproduce `ref.txt` and `sweep.txt` byte-identically. The patch is empty (all work in-folder), as expected for a question about the law rather than the code.
2. **Fidelity of the replica.** `crates/universe/src/step.rs` (doc comment, lines 5–11) specifies exactly what `replica.rs` implements: DKD with one kick of weight H at the interval midpoint, step = 1/64 of the radial period taken in the drift's own phase — including the "quasi-parabolic costs no more than a circle" claim the run measured (fact #5: e = 0.6 costs 33.5 vs 33.0 kicks/orbit). `docs/law.md` "Perturbed motion" matches.
3. **Held-out predictions: pre-registered and honestly scored.** `predictions.md` mtime 06:09:03 precedes `sweep.txt` 06:10:13; the sweep is a single deterministic command over a fixed grid, so nothing was tunable after the fact. The dossier reports its own misses (#1 and #5 refuted, #6 and #7 half) rather than hiding them.
4. **Solver control.** With Kepler-Newton iterations raised 8 → 16, all seven reference slopes are unchanged to the printed 7 digits (worst shift: D's ref–ref relative 5.496e-10 → 5.555e-10). The references are solver-converged, not a common-mode artifact — important at e = 0.6, where an under-converged Kepler solve would bias every scheme identically.
5. **Adversarial fine grid (my main correction).** The sweep's macro lists are powers of two, so "dkd min = 33.0 on A" is grid-quantized: dkd at macro 22 spends 22.8 kicks/orbit and passes every gate (prec_rel 0.011, e_rel 0.0015, jac 7.89e-5). y4's true minimum sits between 21.5 (jac 1.40e-4, fails) and 24.7. So the honest ratio on A is **~0.9–1.0, not 1.34×**: composition order buys nothing at these tolerances, and the same correction applies to B. This *strengthens* the headline (no 4×, decisively) but corrects a stated number in the direction that had favoured adoption. The dossier's grid-gap caveat covered C–G only.
6. **The near-edge cliff is real, silent, and now located.** At 0.45 R_H prograde the two references agree to 7.6e-6 (not chaotic), yet dkd@64 reports +0.488 vs −0.0448 (wrong sign, 11.9×) with e_rel 0.0009 and Jacobi 8.6e-6. My continuation runs show it heals only at ~107 kicks/orbit actually spent — dkd@96 (107.4) and y4@32 (107.4), the *same spend*, i.e. the mechanism is tide resolution, not composition order. Consequence sharpened: the plane's current 1/64 would fail B14's precession gate on judged G-like seeds today; the seed filter (or a local kick-rate rise near the edge) is not optional.
7. **F (deepest tide).** dkd's true all-gates minimum is 65.5 (macro 56, jac 9.72e-5), not 74.7 — the plane's margin there is ~1.1×, thinner than stated. y4 has **no** passing setting on F under the Jacobi gate (best: 55.3 spent, jac 1.27e-4); combined with the verified y4 escapes at coarse settings (F@8: e→9135; G@4: e→11659), fact #4 (negative sub-steps are fragile with phase stepping) is confirmed and is itself an adoption argument against correctors near the edge.
8. **Novelty.** No H entry measures composition order or the margin in the 1/64: H0190 K1 measured kick *rates* (32.5/orbit, averaging 114×) in a solar scene, not scheme comparison; the r9 kill was about averaging, not order. The run takes board focus item 1, queued as `q-correctors` (`lab/queue.txt` line 6). The cited mechanism is real: arXiv:1908.03468's abstract states symplectic correctors "do not improve the accuracy of secular frequencies" — though it is Rein et al. 2019, not "Wisdom et al." as the dossier says.
9. **Why not gold.** Nothing moves 10×, nothing is deleted, and nothing is adoptable as a one-step change: the answer is a negative plus facts about the existing law, and the frontier's binding gate (Jacobi ≤ 1e-4, exponent dropped) is the author's construction, not a frozen gate. Single trajectory per case, single mass ratio; B14 is 256 seeds. The author requested silver-to-lead; the work is better than its request.

That is one rung, and its outcome is adoptable verbatim.
