== ghost memory per scale: retention = influence radius / c =
   how long a source's pre-news path ('ghost') is still felt by someone ==
        Earth doll   (r_H ~ 0.01 AU):          5 s
        Jupiter doll (r_H ~ 0.35 AU):        175 s
         Sun doll     (R    ~ 50 AU):        6.9 h
   a star, individually (r_H ~ 1 pc):       3.3 yr
     galaxy cell, consumers at 1 kpc:      3260 yr
     galaxy cell, consumers at 8 kpc:     26080 yr

news-only history per source = 24 B (t,x,y for the cone test) x depth + 16 B tube (shared)
+ 4 B cursor per (body, source) pair; depth = news events inside the retention window.
  star system: thrust/contact/rebind news ~ 0 in hours => depth ~1 => ~40 B/source.
  every-step ring (a237 probe policy): 16 B x cadence x (light time in orbits) per source.
  galaxy: cell-cell news ~ 1/(5e8 yr); retention 3e3..2.6e4 yr => alive ghosts ~ 1e-5..1e-4/cell.

what the galaxy case requires: the ghost must be a GENERATOR — the cell's orbital program
in the smooth galactic field, advanced by addition (zero kicks). A stepped ghost would
cost a second body per news event for 1e4..1e5 yr. Ghost fields enter as aggregate
multipole differences (old program - new program), shrinking to zero as cones pass;
ghost-of-ghost branches are second order in the news size and pruned at retention.
