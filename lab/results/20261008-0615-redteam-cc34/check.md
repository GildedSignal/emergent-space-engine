## What the author missed

1. **The a₀=2W degeneracy:** their own "±2W closes the under-trigger" caveat was untestable in their setup (a ±2W gate never switches); from 4W, ±2W still fails (−27%) and ±3W fails worse (−42%). The damage is larger than the caveat implies.
2. **The handoff-phase channel:** non-monotone error in gate width means widening the gate is not a fix even in principle; r4 v4 should measure it explicitly (a per-case metric, not just the worst case).

4. **Capture:** zero captures at every rate tested here too; a rate sweep (or an adiabatic limit) would say whether capture is reachable at belt-realistic drag before r4 v4 leans on labels.
