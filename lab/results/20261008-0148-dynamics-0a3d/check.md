**What the author missed.**
- **Mechanism mislabel in the headline.** The cold-belt F(10)=2.25 is driven mostly by co-orbital chatter, not synodic locking: of 576 cold ≥3-event pairs, 353 (61%) have median gap ≈ 1 orbit ≪ T_syn (near-conjunction wiggle minima below ρ; their median T_syn ≈ 1542 orbits), only 180 (31%) are locked at ~1·T_syn. "Trains locked to integer multiples of the synodic period" and the "k·T_syn, k ∈ {1,2,3}" scheduling line hold for warm/hot belts; a cold belt's next repeat-pair event is ~1 orbit away inside the same conjunction — even easier to schedule, but a different certificate (per-conjunction multiplicity, not Diophantine next-conjunction).
- λ_max ∝ N and F(ρ)-dependence (both measured here) soften the "two constants" adoption; the wrongness section argued the first and missed the second.

- The closure is incoherent-only and one belt family (admitted); coherent/resonant stirring (H0203) is the regime where "no avalanche" could still fail.
