**What the author missed.**
- The gold's nc=800 evidence point was a **uniform**-λ fleet (a_hw 1e-3); its bunched evidence was only nc=200. So "one doubling past its evidence base" understates the inversion: in the bunched family the certificate already misses 8.6% at nc=800 — inside the gold's own nc range, one config the gold never ran bunched. The evidence base was smaller than the dossier says, and the failure starts earlier.
- The certificate's sort key could be mean longitude, not true longitude; true-longitude rank is the *conservative lower bound* (only in true longitude does contact guarantee ρ-arc proximity; epicycles interleave more bodies per arc in mean longitude). This asymmetry deserved a sentence — it says the measured miss fractions are floors.
- Rows mix time horizons (2 orbits at nc ≤ 800, 1 orbit above); occupancy's orbit-breathing (epicycle stirring of the bunch, σ_λ vs 2e) makes small-nc occupancy orbit-count-dependent. Harmless here — linearity at fixed orbit count is what the extrapolation uses, and my 6400 point confirms it — but the table's small-nc occupancy values are not comparable to the large-nc ones without that note.
- The 72 B/section figure is used without derivation (it is right: `Section` = f64 + u64 + 2×i64 + 4×f64 + tag).
