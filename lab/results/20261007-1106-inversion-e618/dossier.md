# Dossier — inversion-e618: the train certificate's k=4 is an artifact of nc ≤ 800; the watch width is the ρ-window occupancy, which grows linearly with fleet size

## Claim (one sentence)
The adopted phase-neighbour "train" certificate (gold 20261006-0346-taleb-09b5: "every real encounter lies within partner rank 4 along the orbit, so a phase-neighbour certificate at O(n_c·k) replaces the quadratic tier") fails by measurement at one doubling past its evidence base: at nc=3200 66% of real encounters have partner longitude-rank > 4 (p99=26) and at nc=12800 91% do (p50=34, max=114) under the gold's own fleet parameters — because the watch width k is not a constant but the ρ-arc occupancy (~nc·2ρ/λ-spread, linear in nc), so at the board's 10⁶-fleet scale k* ≈ 3–6×10³, making the train either a ~90+% miss (k=4) or a 300–450 GB section store (nc·k*·72 B) against 8 GB.

## Bottleneck
The board's top scaling risk ("candidate pairs per body grow with N; candidate work must track real encounters") and the risk list's first item ("co-orbital concentration makes the pairwise tier miss contacts and go quadratic; work must track real encounters"). The train certificate was the adopted answer for concentrated fleets. This run inverts the gold: it asked what the gold silently assumed, and the assumption — that the rank-4 evidence measured at nc ∈ {200, 800} says anything about the nc = 10⁶ regime the certificate was adopted for — is false by a linear-in-nc mechanism sitting in the gold's own data (their "spacing down to ~3ρ" traffic-jam case is the same occupancy, one doubling from failing).

1. **The nc cliff (headline).** At the gold's own fleet parameters the fraction of real encounters with partner longitude-rank > 4 goes 0 → 8.6% → 66% → 91% for nc = 200 → 800 → 3200 → 12800; occupancy (the certificate's true watch width, exact) goes 2.3 → 6.0 → 20.9 → 79.7, linear in nc as derived (k* = nc·2ρ/λ-spread). The gold's sweep stopped at nc=800, where k* ≈ 4–6 sits just under its k=4. **Even the gold's own metric fails at scale:** dist-rank max reaches 73 (55% of encounters > 4) at nc=12800 — the "234/234 ≤ 4" evidence does not extrapolate either.
2. **The instrument gap (the decoupling).** Sweeping σ_e at nc=3200: the angular interleavers' median radial offset grows 0.9ρ → 2.8ρ → 9.4ρ, dist-rank>4 collapses 0.211 → 0.032 → **0.000**, while lon-rank>4 stays 0.55–0.68. So in a dispersed fleet (any realistic e-spread ≫ ρ) the gold's distance-rank metric *recovers exactly when the certificate fails*: distance-rank is structurally blind to the failure mode. The gold measured the wrong rank for the mechanism it certified ("sort by longitude, watch k≈4 neighbours" — a longitude order, evidenced by a distance rank).
3. **Bunch width confirms the mechanism.** Doubling σ_λ halves occupancy (16.4 → 10.2) and lon-rank>4 (0.546 → 0.360), as 1/σ_λ predicts.
4. **Ruin arithmetic at the adopted scale (computed, not run).** nc = 10⁶, σ_λ uniform (the gold's fleet scenario): k* = nc·2.2ρ/TAU ≈ 35, sections = nc·k*·72 B ≈ 2.6 GB (fits, but only because the fleet is spread); σ_λ = 0.01: k* ≈ 6×10³ (occ 79.7 × 78), sections ≈ 450 GB against 8 GB — the gold's "n_c·4 sections ≈ 300 MB" is short by ~10³×. A k=4 train at that scale misses ~90%+ of contacts (measured 91% at nc=12800, and the miss fraction grows with nc).
5. **What survives.** The trigger idea (switch regime by a dimensionless number) and the O(n_c·k) form survive; what must change is that k is a *derived* quantity — the ρ-arc occupancy the trigger must compute — or the order must be 2D (longitude within radial bands; at 10⁶, uniform-λ fleets need k ≈ 35 in longitude × ~1 radial band ≈ 2.6 GB, bunched fleets are contact plasmas whose encounter rate itself is the cost). The certificate needs the occupancy as an invariant before the switch fires.

## Predictions

## What would make it wrong
The rank evidence is for λ-bunched fleets (encounters must be frequent to measure ranks); at uniform λ the gold's own 10⁶-fleet scenario has occ ≈ 35 and I claim rank > 4 there by the same mechanism, but I did not measure ranks in a uniform-λ fleet at nc ≥ 3200 (encounters too rare to sample cheaply). Contacts are unperturbed two-body Kepler events (the crate's standing assumption, shared with the gold — same harness, same solver; the grid-vs-brute control shares the solver too). The 10⁶ arithmetic extrapolates the measured linear occupancy ×78 from nc=12800, so it is arithmetic, not a measurement. σ_e ≥ 0.03 and inclination (3D) are unexplored; 3D can only add interleavers. And a train certificate that *re-derives its width* from occupancy, or sorts longitude within radial bands, would answer this run — the claim is about the certificate as adopted (constant k=4, 1D order).

## Cost of adoption, fit with PRINCIPLES
Adoption is a design change, not code: the encounters design's concentrated-fleet tier must (a) compute the ρ-arc occupancy as its watch width and budget its section store from it, or (b) use a 2D order; and its evidence bar must measure the *certificate's own order* (longitude), not a convenient proxy (distance). Cost: a redesign of one tier of the encounters spec plus its trigger; no crate on main implements the train yet, so nothing is deleted. Fit: "A fix signals the wrong representation" (#19) — a constant k over a 1D order is the wrong water for a 2D contact problem under concentration; "regimes are chosen by dimensionless numbers" (#4) — the occupancy *is* the dimensionless number (nc·ρ/λ-spread), and it was mistaken for a constant; "Measure before claiming" — rank-4 was measured at 200–800 and adopted for 10⁶; "no work for no reason" (#14) — at k* the 1D train's watch list is mostly radially-displaced non-contacts (measured: interleavers at 9ρ), i.e. work for no reason.

## The lens (what inversion made me miss)
Inversion found the hole but nearly inherited the gold's other blind spot: I initially reproduced *only* the distance-rank metric, and my first predictions treated it as sound ("dist-rank ≤ 4 everywhere"); the data broke that at nc=12800. A speed lens would have caught that my first rank pass was O(enc×nc) and timed out one config family (isotropic ϖ, extra seeds) — the sweep's tail is unmeasured, not absent. And by staying inside the gold's own scene family (tight a, tiny e), this run cannot say whether the *pairwise* tier's window bug (the 4/233 cone misses) is also occupancy-driven — a different lens should invert that tier next.
