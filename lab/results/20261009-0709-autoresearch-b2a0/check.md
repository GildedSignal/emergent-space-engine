Grade: lead

**Abstract.** Claim: exact 128/64 word divisions plus one algebraic identity make `Orbit::compile` ~5% cheaper with bit-identical outputs; per-node cost is at its structural floor under bit-identity, so the 10× for kicks is compiling less, not micro-speed. Everything reproduced: interleaved A/B gives tune −4.9% total / −7% median, held-out −5…−6% / −10…−11%, fingerprints `f4bd77fc76c8b072` and `9356db3f24802745` identical on both sides; 50/50 crate tests in release and debug. My adversarial cases (p_exp −3…40, e to 0.9999 and Q48-max, photon/null keys b=2.5…2⁴⁰) are also bit-identical, and `div_w64` is exact on 10M boundary cases. Gain 0.05×, not 10×: below the board's order-of-magnitude bar and same class as 1932f6c. Adoption cost ~30 lines, trivial, but M1 gain unmeasured.

**Reasons.**

1. **Reproduced, exactly.** Clean tree, `git apply work/patch.diff`, the dossier's commands: `ab.sh tune` A 1290778/1289747 → B 1228845/1238350 (−4.9% total; median 7075/7059 → 6559/6569, −7.1%); `ab.sh heldout` A 2376593/2467090 → B 2262044/2313351 (−5.0/−6.2%; median −9.7/−11.1%). Fingerprints identical A↔B on both populations, matching the author's numbers within shared-box noise. `cargo test -p emergent-space`: 50 passed, 0 failed in release **and** debug (debug fires `div_word`'s precondition asserts).
2. **Bit-identity attacked, not just reproduced.** (a) My own populations — p_exp ∈ {−3,−1,0,1,26,30,40} × e ∈ {0, 0.9, 0.99, 0.999, 0.9999} plus e = 2⁴⁸−1, and photon passages b ∈ {2.5…2⁴⁰} (null keys, the wrapped-`a` path; captures and flybys, duration/deflection/state readouts): fingerprints and refusal counts identical A↔B (`326ecca43a0d1207`, `81d2c7ec25166e0f`). (b) `div_w64` extracted verbatim and checked against `(n/d as u128) as u64` on 10,000,836 cases including the guard boundary (n≫64 = d−1, d, d+1), d ∈ {1 … 2⁶⁴−1}, n = 2¹²⁸−1, and q·d+r constructions: all equal. The patch is bit-identical by construction (guarded fallback is literally the original expression; `a = 4k+2x` is an integer identity, wrap-safe), and now also empirically.
3. **Pre-registration looks genuine.** predictions.md (33 m before log.md) contains the baseline fingerprints that the current bench still reproduces, and P1 *failed* honestly (predicted ≥25%, measured 5–9%) — a post-hoc author predicts 5%. P2/P3/P4 held. The mid-run broken-`isqrt` incident (−30% artifact, caught by the fingerprint) is disclosed in both log and dossier.
4. **Why lead, not silver.** The gold bar is 10×, a deletion, or a killed premise. This is 0.05×, deletes nothing, and its premise-kill (divisions do not dominate on this box) was already the board's state: commit 1932f6c (1 Oct, Sol) delivered the same class of bit-exact single-digit kick speedup (median 25.75→23.71 µs) with the same verification style, and the 6 Oct gold (components-4643) already put the lever on cadence/compiling-less. Silver requires one named experiment away from gold; no experiment on this path reaches it — P1's own failure bounds division-elimination (reciprocal division etc.) to a few more percent, and the author's instrumentation puts the rest of the node in `cos`/`isqrt`. The honest value of this run is a confirmed floor measurement plus an independently verified, cheap, bit-safe patch.
5. **Goal fit.** Real bottleneck (kicks dominate; H0210's 29 µs), real method (bit-identical integer law, "the cheapest arithmetic"), but exactly the named sin the author quotes: "polishing what may be replaced". Also x86-specific: the gain rests on `__udivti3` vs two hardware divisions on this i7; the M1's `udiv` economics differ and the lab reports orders of magnitude only — 5% is under that floor.

**What the author missed.**

- 1932f6c in `git log` (bit-exact kick speedup, same crate, 1 Oct) is the closest prior art and is not cited; the dossier's novelty case should have been argued against it (the lever differs — word divisions in `fast_quad` — but the class and the conclusion do not).
- The ~5 µs fixed `Scalar` cost in `Self::new`/`from_shape` is named as untouchable by node work but never probed: at the measured ~7 µs median it is most of the weak-key compile, and one profile there might have been the run's one non-marginal finding.
- The patch carries a whitespace-only hunk (blank line in num.rs) — trivial, but it is a line the integrator pays for.
