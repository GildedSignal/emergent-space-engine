# Dossier — taleb-09b5: concentration breaks the pairwise candidate system twice, and the train certificate is the guard

## Claim (one sentence)
Under workload concentration (a co-orbital formation — the board's "one 10⁶-ship fleet in one cell"), the pairwise candidate system breaks twice — the per-pair window tier misses ~2% of true encounters (4/233, all cone candidates, d_min down to 6× inside ρ), and setup/memory go quadratic with ~100× work per real encounter — while every measured encounter sits within distance-rank 4 of its partner, so a phase-neighbor "train" certificate at O(n_c·k) replaces the quadratic tier in exactly that regime.

## Bottleneck
The board's top scaling risk, answered "for fixed density" by q-candidate-degree (two-tier certificates, zero misses to 10⁶, work per real encounter constant). The Taleb question was the missing half: what does the *distribution not being fixed* do? Answer: the degenerate co-orbital regime is both a correctness hole and a ruin state for the pairwise representation, and it is detectable by a dimensionless number (clump Binet diameter vs eps_u — the all-Full condition), so the law can switch representations before paying.

## Evidence (all runs ≤ 26 s, Linux x86, 8 vCPU, < 60 MB; binary `crates/encounters/src/bin/taleb.rs`)
Reproduce: `cargo build --release -p emergent-encounters --bin taleb` then `taleb belts`, `taleb fleet`, `taleb ranks`, `taleb ranks2`, `taleb adj` (CARGO_TARGET_DIR is `/home/glmlab/lab-target-1` here). ρ = 1e-4, H = 20 orbits unless said.

1. **An a-clump does not concentrate candidate degree** (prediction 1 failed): N=2000, σ_e=0.05, uniform → degree p50 1282/1999 (the belt is already ~64% candidates; the Binet e-vector spread dominates Δa). With 25% of bodies in a-clumps of width 0.02 → 2e-5: clump degree p50 1615 vs sea 1396 (+16%). The fat tail inside the uniform belt is per-pair: setup evals p50 = p90 = 0, max 5926 (Wide numeric windows) — the mean lies; single pairs spike ~6k evals.
2. **A formation is the kill case** (`taleb fleet`: 400 ships, a half-width 1e-5, e~Rayleigh(0.001), ϖ within ±0.1, λ uniform, + 3600 sea): fleet-fleet = 79,800 = n_c²/2 candidate pairs; setup p50 **606 evals/pair** (sea pairs: 0 median, ~11 mean), 111–153 µs/pair; kinds 55% Wide / 35% Cross / 10% Full; over 20 orbits the sample of 2494 pairs produced **2 encounters** → (setup+run)/hit ≈ **5.0×10⁵ evals per real encounter** vs 3.7×10³ sea-sea (**135×**). Encounters per fleet body per orbit ≈ 0.008: the formation is dormant (ties to taleb-3ec1: setup work lands on dormant pairs).
3. **Ruin arithmetic** (computed from measured per-pair costs, not run): a 10⁶-ship formation stores ~5×10¹¹ sections × ~72 B ≈ **40 TB against 8 GB** and pays 5×10¹¹ × 606 evals × 0.18 µs ≈ **1.5 core-years** of setup — unrecoverable, while its true event rate is O(n_c) per orbit.
4. **The window tier misses real encounters in that regime** (`taleb adj`: 200 ships, σ_λ=0.01 phalanx, 1 orbit): brute+method disputes adjudicated by dense unbiased sampling (150k samples/pair, no binning): method found 229 pairs, **all 193 method-only pairs truly sub-ρ** (0 false), and **4 of 233 true encounters missed by the method — all inside the cone candidate set, d_min 1.6e-5…8.6e-5** (up to 6× inside ρ). So the Binet cone finds them; `sections`' Full/Wide window construction drops them. The zero-miss claim does not survive concentration. Suspect: `numeric_window`'s Lipschitz constant (bi.vmax·1.001) or `full_section`'s along-track pad is unsound for near-tangential relative motion with e-spread radial breathing ~1e-3 ≫ ρ.
5. **The train certificate held everywhere measured**: partner distance-rank at closest approach ≤ 4 for **234/234 adjudicated encounters** (max = 4, most rank 1), across uniform-λ, bunched σ_λ ∈ {0.05, 0.02, 0.01} (spacing down to ~3ρ — the traffic-jam case), a_hw ∈ {1e-5, 1e-3}, n_c ∈ {200, 800}. A k=4 nearest-neighbour watch would have caught every encounter at O(n_c·k) cost — n_c·4 sections vs n_c²/2: the 10⁶-fleet store becomes ~4×10⁶ sections (~300 MB) and setup falls 10⁵×.
6. **Instrument lesson (checker-relevant)**: my first brute force binned bodies by *mean* longitude with ±2ρ window; it missed 193/233 encounters because co-orbital proximity aligns *true* longitude and the equation of center (2(e_i+e_j)·r ≈ 5×10⁻³ rad) dwarfs ρ. Two instruments agreeing means nothing when they share a blind axis; adjudication by a third, unbiased sampler settled it.

## Predictions
`predictions.md` was written before any run. Held: setup convexity (via per-pair cost, not degree), unbounded work-per-encounter under concentration (dormancy), rank ≤ 4 with no visible growth, memory arithmetic. Failed: degree concentration in a mixed belt (cone already saturated — my mechanism was wrong), fleet majority-Full (it is majority Wide), and "zero misses holds in the degenerate regime" (it does not; this is the headline).

## What would make it wrong
The rank evidence is 234 encounters on one seed family around one a; a formation crossing a resonance or with e-spread ≫ ρ could break phase-order = distance-order (rank inflation). The 4 window-tier misses are reproducible on seed 27 but the *rate* (1.7%) is one scenario; the mechanism (which window construction fails) is diagnosed only by elimination (cone:true, so it is the second tier). The train certificate as specified watches k nearest by distance — it needs a proof that co-orbital sub-ρ contact implies rank ≤ k under stated invariants (radial spread ≲ ρ·c), which I did not write.

## Cost of adoption, fit with PRINCIPLES
The switch is representation-local: when a cell's clump number (max pairwise Binet distance / eps_u) ≤ 1, replace pairwise sections with the phase-neighbor train (sort by longitude, watch k≈4 neighbours, rebuild on overtakes — a Diophantine event like the pair's). Cost: one more regime in the encounters crate, the dimensionless trigger, and fixing the window-tier miss (a soundness bug regardless of regime). Fit: "A fix signals the wrong representation" (#19) — the pairwise certificate is the wrong water for the fish in the degenerate regime; "regimes are chosen by dimensionless numbers" (#4); "compute only surprises" (#3) — dormant quadratic setup is the opposite; "one home… delete when built" — the train deletes the quadratic tier, it does not add a mode. Worst-case sizing for the allocator ("the worst cell, not the average one") becomes O(n_c·k), bounded.

## The lens (what Taleb made me miss)
This lens looks for ruin and found it, but nearly shipped a wrong instrument twice: the λ-binned brute (fixed only because I adjudicated disputes instead of trusting either harness) and the degree-concentration story (my pre-registered mechanism was wrong; the real fragility was per-pair setup cost and dormancy). A speed lens would have caught the Wide-window cost curve earlier; it would not have run the phalanx adversarial cases where the misses live.
