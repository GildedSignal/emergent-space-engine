# Predictions (written before any run)

Question (one sentence): the q-candidate-degree result holds "work per real encounter constant at **fixed density**" on **uniform** belts — does a concentration spike (fleet in formation, debris ring, resonance pile; the board's own Extremistan example "one 10^6-ship fleet in one cell") make per-body candidate work convex, break the constant-work-per-encounter property, and does a phase-neighbor ("train") certificate with O(1) rank delete the quadratic?

Common: mu=1, rho=1e-4, H=20 orbits for steady stats. S0 control: N=2000, a~U[0.9,1.1], e~Rayleigh(0.05) cap 0.5, omega/lambda~U. S1 a-clump: 25% of bodies in a-window w around a=1 (w in {0.02, 2e-3, 2e-4, 2e-5}), rest uniform. S2 fleet/formation: 400 bodies, a half-width 1e-5, e~Rayleigh(0.001), omega within ±0.1, lambda~U, + 3600 sea. S3 rank probe: fleets n_c in {200, 800}, tight (a hw 1e-5) and loose (a hw 1e-3), lambda U and bunched (wrapped normal sigma 0.3, 0.05).

1. **Degree concentration (convexity).** S0 median candidate degree is O(10). In S1, clump-body degree saturates near n_clump (all clump pairs become candidates) already at w=0.02 (candidate needs |dU| < R + eps_u, R ~ e-scale ~ 0.05, so a-window 0.02 sits inside the cone); max/median per-body degree >= 3x at f=0.25 and scales ~1/f. If clump degree stays ~ sea degree, my fragility claim dies.
2. **Kind mix.** S1 clump-clump: majority Wide sections (arcs near ±pi/2, numeric windows); Full < 2% (random omega makes Binet VW distance ~ e >> eps_u). S2 fleet: majority Full.
3. **Setup convexity.** Median setup evals per clump-clump pair >= 10x a sea-sea pair (Wide pays conservative advancement, ~10^2-10^3 evals; Cross pays ~0). Worst-body setup grows linearly in n_clump while median-body stays flat: the allocator's least-slack input is set by the worst body, not the mean.
4. **Work per real encounter under concentration.** S2 fleet over H=20: pairwise setup is O(n_c^2) while true fleet-fleet encounters are O(n_c) per orbit (co-orbital drift through a rho-window); work per real encounter therefore grows ~n_c and is unbounded in the tight limit (few or zero encounters amortizing quadratic setup). Constant work per encounter fails by dormancy, not by misses. Method still finds every brute-force encounter (zero misses holds in the degenerate regime).
5. **The guard (decisive).** In S3, every sub-rho fleet encounter has partner rank (by current distance, min of both directions) <= 4; p99 <= 2; max rank does not grow from n_c=200 to 800; under bunching sigma=0.05 max rank may rise but stays <= 10. If max rank grows with n_c or exceeds ~10, the train certificate is killed and dormancy+deferral is the only guard.
6. **Memory arithmetic (computed, not run).** A 10^6-body formation stores ~5x10^11 sections at ~100 B ≈ 50 TB against 8 GB; the train certificate stores O(n_c * k) with k ~ 4. The regime switch is dimensionless: clump Binet diameter / eps_u <= 1 (exactly the all-Full condition) => train regime.

Kill criteria for my own claim: clump degree ~ sea degree, or clump-pair setup ~ sea-pair setup => candidate system already concentration-proof, dossier says so. Rank growing with n_c => guard dead.
