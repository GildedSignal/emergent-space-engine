# The co-orbital (phase-neighbour) encounter certificate

## Current best

**The representation stands; every constant in the adopted sentence died.** Gold `20261006-0346-taleb-09b5` measured co-orbital concentration as the pairwise tier's ruin — a 400-ship formation pays ~5.0×10⁵ evals per real encounter against the belt's 3.7×10³ (135×; fleet setup p50 606 evals/pair, 2 encounters in 20 orbits), and a 10⁶ fleet would store ~40 TB of sections — and proposed the train: sort by longitude, watch k neighbours, rebuild on overtakes, switched by a Binet-clump diameter. One week of red-teams (25 of them gold-graded) refuted each constant while confirming the representation. The standing contract:

1. **Width is derived, never fixed.** The needed rank is density × reach, in whichever coordinate is sorted: contact-band multiplicity m̂ ∝ n/σ_λ (k=4 misses 35–37% in jams, `20261006-1249-inversion-fd48`); ρ-arc occupancy linear in n_c (rank>4 0→91% at n_c 200→12,800, `20261007-1106-inversion-e618`); line density μ̂ ∝ n (17.7% missed at n=256k, ~84–94% at 10⁶ where k≈16–17, `20261006-1249-taleb-e013`); lag tail k*≈n/2 on debris (98.5% missed, `20261007-2057-taleb-efc9`); synodic drift κ = m·E|Δn|·H/W with miss(k=4) 0.05%→80% as κ 0.75→48 (`20261009-1858-inversion-9d6d`); tube packing r_max ≈ 2Π+4 (`20261007-2155-inversion-7655`); e-spread n·σe — one δe=10⁻² kick puts 100% of a body's encounters outside rank 4, safe domain n·σe ≲ 3–6 (`20261008-1337-inversion-c5ab`); epicyclic channel k ∝ n·σe, where the synodic engine finds 0 of 125 encounters (`20261011-1814-spacex-a110`). Distance-rank at contact is bounded by local ρ-density, so it could never certify a 10⁶ fleet: the λ̄-rank a watch needs is already 28/61%>4 in the gold's own scene, and the 10⁶ watch is ~1.45×10¹¹ pairs ≈ 11.6 TB (`20261011-0614-inversion-b292`, `20261011-0736-second-20261011-0614-inversion-b292-5033`).

2. **Sound window forms exist and are measured.** Apsis-time keying with W′ = (R+X)/v_apo + 3(δa/a)(T+P) finds every contact at 1.003–1.017× contacts across e ≤ 0.99, correlated spreads and drift (`20261010-1407-second-20261010-1302-taleb-bb88-b612`; scaling law √((1+e)/(1−e)) from `20261010-1302-taleb-bb88`). The built per-pair λ̄ window (L̄ = δ(1+L_E) + K_v|(ΔV,ΔW)|) bounds the equation of center by construction and is sound where forced to e = 0.85 (`20261009-1259-inversion-2360`, `20261010-0448-inversion-03c1`).

3. **Validity is chosen by dimensionless numbers, plural.** Single-stream/period equality — two crossing fleets give catch@4 = 0% at median rank 0.34n (`20261010-0100-inversion-b063`); equal-period battles need period grouping, knife-edge at 0.15% period mismatch (`20261011-0517-taleb-3ebf`); intra-orbit rate spread ~2e·n breaks k=4 even at Δn = 0 (`20261008-0338-inversion-4c98`). Orientation ∩ sense — polar formations miss 98–100% at any cadence, retrograde clashes churn 2·n_p·n_r order changes/orbit; Binet space contains neither (`20261009-0208-inversion-a937`). Apsidal/e-vector spread, not e — aligned apsides are safe at e = 0.6, scattered apsides drop 95–100% with rank = N·|Δλ*|/π (`20261008-1947-taleb-1b5d`, `20261011-0224-taleb-236b`); the boundary persists mid-cone (`20261011-0324-second-20261011-0224-taleb-236b-55a8`). Local, not mean, tube count (`20261007-2155-inversion-7655`). Epicycle-scale vs spacing: 2·e_scale ≲ k·(2π/n) with ~3× margin (`20261009-1259-second-20261009-1210-inversion-c529-154a`).

4. **Maintenance is the cost, and it must be event-driven.** Rebuild-on-overtakes is horizon-sound (0/20,810 missed over 500 orbits, refresh 2.3 analytic overtakes/orbit, `20261009-1259-second-20261009-1210-inversion-c529-154a`) but its swap volume is quadratic: Θ(n_c²) overtakes/orbit at exponent 2.000 (`20261006-0916-inversion-f0be`), churn ∝ 2e·n_c/σ_λ (`20261007-0842-inversion-0a7a`), √n in n on the refresh path with crossover σ_e ≈ k/n, and zero-miss needs a local density gate (5.7% missed in a dense convoy, `20261007-0842-second-20261007-0800-inversion-7532-c1bf`). A static or per-orbit-refreshed list catches 2.1–5.6% in quiet sheared concentrations — no horizon-bounded tier survives without event-driven maintenance (`20261011-0924-inversion-262d`); the rebuild budget must scale with shear rate × window (0/37 misses at 14.3 rebuilds/body/orbit, `20261006-0916-library-80a8`). Lazy 1-orbit refresh misses 0.16–67% (`20261007-0842-second-20261007-0800-inversion-7532-c1bf`).

5. **Cross-group pairs need their own routing.** 87.3% of fleet-body encounters are fleet×belt, invisible to any ordering of the fleet alone (`20261007-0800-inversion-7532`); per-pair ΔU classification replaces population triggers (the cone covers 542/542 where the sort loses 97.4%, `20261009-1210-inversion-c529`); crossing lanes and pericentre funnels force exactly C(N,2) candidates on the geometric tier — repaired by ranking arrival times at the orbit-intersection locus, k=8 at ≥0.995 recall (`20261007-1420-inversion-384f`); breakup clouds sit on an exact null plane in Binet space that blinds the cone (37 TiB retained at 10⁶ fragments) while a shared-focus epoch-stream covers at-P encounters at ~3 checks each, flat in N, at-Q residue 14–39% open (`20261010-1706-inversion-3775`).

**The build** (`wt/cx-b-coorbital-cert`, adce00d6; **merged to main by the week's review before this page was compiled** — H0216, `92c94b1`, the tier's 21 tests pass, `crates/encounters/src/{phase,pairs,tier}.rs` — so the defects below live in shipped code; the first lab build 152e died at 429): sound everywhere measured — 0 misses under every checker attack, windows occupancy-sized, never rank 4 (`20261012-0138-taleb-74a1`, `20261012-0311-second-20261012-0138-taleb-74a1-4ec3`) — but engagement and cost are broken: `reach` is sized by dispersion × horizon (angular + 2·centre + (n_hi−n_lo)·H/2), never by contacts, so dispersion > ρ pays Θ(n²) at zero encounters (31.86 M window pairs vs the tree tier's 396 k at n=8000); any kick breaks the exact shape-equality gate (ULP cliff 499→124,750), making the quadratic the default post-burn state; Binet-L1 clumping fragments families (σ_e·a ≳ 2–3×10⁻³: only 1/2, 0/3, 2/30 real encounters covered); ζ compares against pairwise setups, not encounters (63–98% watch at 150–243× cost); e ≥ 1 panics. Fixes are named and priced: one cost comparison at the trigger (`20261012-1117-inversion-5ffb`), U-only clumping, dormancy-priced ζ, a third regime for the fluid corner (`20261009-1259-inversion-2360`), the two ζ one-line bugs (`20261010-0448-inversion-03c1`).

**Domain:** all of it is planar Kepler (float replicas, the crate's own truth where used), n ≤ 256,000 measured with 10⁶ arithmetic extrapolated; 3-D inclination and kick-perturbed λ(t) are untested in every run of this list (flagged by `a937`, `154a`, `262d`, `b612`).

## Refuted

- "Every real encounter lies within partner rank 4" as a constant, in any single coordinate: refuted by `fd48`, `e013`, `e618`, `efc9`, `6f01` (rank ≈ N·spread/2π; M-rank drops 90–97% at Δe ≥ 10⁻⁴), `7655`, `c5ab`, `1b5d`, `236b`, `8af8`, `bb88`, `a110`, `9d6d`, `5033`.
- O(n_c·k) at k = 4 with a ~300 MB 10⁶ store: rebuilds are Θ(n_c²)/orbit (`f0be`, `0a7a`), and the honest 10⁶ watch is ~11.6 TB at formation density (`5033`).
- The Binet-clump diameter as the trigger: reads ~47 on the motivating fleet, so it never fires there (`fd48`); blind to orientation and sense (`a937`), to dispersion × horizon (`74a1`/`4ec3`), and to local jams — a mean-based trigger routes a Π=4 jam into k=4 (`7655`).
- "Replaces the quadratic tier": the routing hole is structural (`7532`, `c529`, `b063`, `5c2a`, `384f`), and the cone fallback itself is blinded by breakup null planes (`3775`).
- Horizon (or 2ae/ρ, or ϖ coherence) as the validity scalar: retracted — validity is epicycle-scale vs spacing (`154a`, correcting `c529`).
- Static setup-time lists and per-orbit refresh as maintenance: 2.1–5.6% catch, 39–81% rescue (`262d`); element-change or quantum-slack payments at belt density miss 19/19 (`80a8`).
- The mean-longitude sort as the deployable key: 61–79% of ranks > 4 in the gold's own validation fleet (`9a9d`, `322f`, `b292`); the λ key must be taken mod 2π (`20261007-2002-taleb-9fa1`, `b292`).
- The gold's 4/233 window-tier misses as mid-horizon window unsoundness: they are t=0/horizon-edge convention artifacts (`9a9d`; the gold's own checker located them at the boundary).

## Contradictions

- **Mean-longitude rank fails (60–88%, `7532`) vs the gold's watch misses zero (`c1bf`)** — resolved as oracle-vs-watch: the gold pre-registered distance-rank at contact, which is bounded by ρ-density and cannot fail (`b292`); the deployable sort needs λ̄-rank, which fails. Both stand; every "rank" claim must name its coordinate.
- **`c529`'s horizon kill vs `154a`'s retraction (gold)** — the retraction stands: the specified rebuild-on-overtakes watch is horizon-sound; the frozen-order scenes (`322f`, `262d`) measure the unmaintained variant. Kept as the page's H0183 example.
- **`3ebf`'s "element-clump grouping drops 61–72%" vs the built code having no grouping** — the built `group_events` certifies the caller's whole slice (would catch the battle) or refuses; the silent miss lives in the unwired caller-side grouping the spec never names (`3ebf`'s own checker). The gate's blindness (single-population scenes only) is confirmed either way.
- **`9fa1`'s λ-vs-θ kill** — an instrument artifact (unwrapped key); the corrected budget law k_p95 ≈ 1.6·C survives. A lead, not a live contradiction.
