## Claim

The emitters the shipped view "loses" at every mid-flight gather are **dead handed-out fleet
members** (engine outcomes, skipped by the view's alive gate by design) — the crossing search
drops **zero** emitters at every depth and keep flown — so the page's od-2 fix (the `eval → None`
clamp) cures a disease that does not exist (flown: a statistical no-op), and the keep's
completeness margin is confirmed jobless; the one-leg keep's saving is **cadence-bound** (−2.5%
on the coast, where retention is floored by the last-segment rule, not the light-distance term).

## What would make it wrong

A scene where a body's history is pruned *between* a gather's probes mid-search (this bench's
prunes happen between gathers) could still hit the `eval → None` path; the deepest none probed
183k gs back without dropping anyone, so the margin is large but this is one scene family, coast
and burn, 10⁴–10⁵ bodies, ≤ 200k gs. The attribution instrument prints deaths per gather; a
scene with wrecks expected visible would read the same numbers as "losses" until the integrator
decides the design question below.

## Cost of adoption, fit with PRINCIPLES

Nothing to adopt in the view: **delete the fix idea, keep the shipped search** ("the best part is
no part"). The one-leg keep (`ES_KEEP_LEGS=1`, one line, already written and env-gated in c8d5's
patch) is now **complete by attribution** at every depth flown — its remaining case is purely
memory arithmetic on kick-dense scenes ("compute only surprises": retention is supply the demand
never reads). Surfaced for the integrator, not decided here: dead ships vanish from the view — no
wreck is drawn — which reads against "history, not a winner" if the player is meant to see
 graveyards; one line in the view would push dead bodies at their last state, a design call.

## The lens (SpaceX) and what it missed

The idiot index sent me to fix the broken part before deleting the margin — the right order — but
the "broken part" was a misread instrument: the fastest falsifier was not the fix, it was
**attributing the loss** (one id print), which c8d5 cut for time. The lens prices mechanisms and
underweights identity; the whole result fell out of asking "which two emitters, exactly". What it
missed: the P4 failure shows the coast's memory floor lives in the *schedule's* last-segment rule
— a different subsystem than the lens went at (retention) — and the wreck-visibility question is a
product question no cost index surfaces.
