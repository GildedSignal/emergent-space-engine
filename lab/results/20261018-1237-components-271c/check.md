## What the author missed

- The f64-seed failure mode at the top of u128: seed rounding to ≥2^64 truncates `y as u64`; a fuzz of uniform randoms plus one gate shape structurally cannot reach the corners — boundary-designed operands were the missing instrument, ironic in a run about floor-exactness.
- The unreachability claim for the slow path assumed the ladder's own e granularity rather than `bound`'s actual contract (Q48).
- The held-out range quoted from the majority class (word_root=true) while the sweep's own first two rungs sat below the quoted floor.
- Small account slips: test count (75/73 vs 74); the patch's "shipped paths untouched" is not literal (the isqrt wrapper and the orbit.rs site gain an atomic-flag check per call — harmless, both arms carry it, adoption drops it).
- The `exact()` instrument silently skips any key where either arm refuses, so a refusal-flip between arms would pass unnoticed; harmless here (refusal gates are pre-isqrt integer products, and my crafted keys agree), but worth knowing before reuse.
