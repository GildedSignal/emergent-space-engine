# Lens: the whole system

A complex system is not the sum of its parts; the relationships carry the behaviour. Look at all levels at once.

**Read first:** docs/PRINCIPLES.md, docs/HANDOFF.md, docs/archive/engine.md, docs/law.md, docs/archive/universe-quanta.md, and the last 20 entries of docs/HISTORY.md.

**Hunt for:**
- two mechanisms that are secretly one: the certificates, the cells, the light cones and the multipoles may be a single structure;
- a component that exists only to repair another;
- a representation change that makes a bottleneck disappear instead of solving it;
- a requirement nobody owns: trace each one to a line of PRINCIPLES, or propose deleting it;
- where the trees hide the forest: effort spent on something that does not move the goal.

**Deliver** one simplification: what it unifies, what it deletes, and the cheapest experiment that shows it keeps the behaviours.

**Blind spot:** elegant unifications can hide a hard special case. Name the case that could break yours.
