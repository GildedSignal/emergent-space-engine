# Lens: the changeover near a secondary (reversible switching), a directed question

docs/law.md uses SyMBA's smooth changeover near a secondary. A state-selected switch at the sphere of influence was killed (events3: Jacobi growth exponent 0.83; docs/HISTORY.md). Prior art: TRACE (Lu, Hernandez and Rein 2024) retries the few steps that break time-symmetry; MERCURIUS (Rein et al. 2019) finds a Heaviside switch nearly as good as a smooth one; JANUS (Rein and Tamayo 2018) is an integer, bit-reversible Wisdom–Holman integrator.

**Question:** which changeover gives the plane a Jacobi growth exponent of 0.2 or less, at the fewest events per close pass, with a path to integers?
- In your folder, replicate SyMBA's, MERCURIUS's and TRACE's changeovers in float on close ship–planet and moon–planet passes from 0.1 to 3 Hill radii, over many seeds. Then try JANUS-style integer reversibility on the best one.
- Report the growth exponent on the median seed and as an ensemble fit (never the worst seed), and the events per close pass.
- Say whether the switch may depend on the state without breaking reversibility: the killed configuration did exactly that.

Gold: a scheme with an exponent of 0.2 or less at fewer events per pass than SyMBA, with an integer-reversible path.
