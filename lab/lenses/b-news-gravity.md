# Lens: Gravity at c in the plane's kicks: news with a cursor (crates/universe) (a directed build)

law.md "Gravity travels at c": a source's field at a body comes from the source's tube as the body's past light cone last saw it; a change in the source's motion arrives only with its light cone. The lab measured it: `q-gravity-at-c` (news keeps behaviours except at e = 0.9 and with comparable masses), `q-gravity-cursor` (about one integer test per kick; a single frozen ghost is unstable; refresh by version ring). Implement it in the plane's kicks with a cursor per (body, source) and a version ring, so a thrusting planet's or ship's pull reaches others at light speed. Tests: a thrust's news arrives exactly at light-travel time; the Laplace control (pull toward the retarded position) spirals out while news holds. Gates: B13, B14, B17 (`--law plane`), G1–G6.

**How to build.** Work in this tree against `main`; your edits become `work/patch.diff`, which a reviewer applies later (you never commit, push or merge). Read docs/law.md, docs/HANDOFF.md ("In flight", "Astra's queue") and the references named above before writing code. Ship tests (each under 60 s) and run the named gates before and after; report every gate's detail line both ways. A gate is frozen: never edit a gate, a threshold or frozen evidence; if a gate seems wrong, say why and leave it. Keep every process under 2 GB. If the change cannot pass its gates, a clean measured failure with its diagnosis is a result: say so rather than patch around it.

Gold: the patch applies to main, its tests pass, and the named gates improve or hold, measured, with nothing else regressing.
