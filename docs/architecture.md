# Architecture and current qualification boundaries

## Reality and evaluation

The engine is a **spacetime compiler**. It compiles generators, certificates, scripted interaction IR, family enclosures and (later) responses. It does not step entities through ticks. `CanonicalState` contains the law, seed, normalized generator commitment, committed causal cut, physical trajectory changes, interventions, outstanding signals, scheduled courses and event facts. Search jobs, co-moving broad-phase boxes and UI snapshots are derived. A hash names an explicitly completed causal cut; it does not hash memory layout or unfinished evaluation coverage.

`Engine::advance(target, WorkBudget)` resumes a pending compile pass. Box construction, family-range subtraction, a resumable stable merge, active-list expiry, AABB compares, grouping, frontier enqueue, commit preparation and refinement jobs are charged operations. The pass certifies all possible earlier contacts and message arrivals before committing the next simultaneous batch. A complete empty pass jumps directly to the requested cut. After a trajectory change the kinetic SAP repairs local adjacencies instead of discarding the scan. The compiler never loops over elapsed ticks or conceptual population size. Sort, grouping, expiry, frontier building and commit preparation are bounded WorkBudget steps; they do not run as one atomic hitch.

The broad phase sweeps exact enclosing boxes in a shared co-moving frame. This cancels common drift before it inflates a billion-year worldtube. Family ranges refine only where a pair remains ambiguous. After a burn or contact the compiler repairs dirty boxes and their adjacencies in that frame (incremental SAP) instead of discarding the scan. Overlap certificates persist across quiet commits: a pair compiled through the remaining horizon is not re-enqueued until a generator changes. After SAP, connected components of those certified overlaps are independent compile islands on the finite scan horizon: quiet space is many tiny components; a dense packed encounter is one large component. A frozen packed-overlap / war-island fixture stresses that grouping; independent far families remain singleton islands, and overlapping families are not merged. This is not a semantic battle mode. Scheduled `SetCourseRange` rebases generators and must not restore a pre-burn SAP. Each family or SAP item compiles one guarded IR segment (enclosure disjointness, next `SetCourse`/`SetCourseRange`, contact-stop, inbound signal) and jumps to the earliest conservative invalidation; a failed guard applies IR and recompiles. Per-region clocks and uneven committed cuts remain deferred. The present implementation is still a sweep and family bisection, not a production spacetime FMM or a full kinetic certificate heap. The hierarchical spacetime address type is available; general block compilation remains downstream of physical-law selection.

## Laboratory event law

The numeric primitives are specified in `laws/inertial-causal-v1.md`. The runtime adds these explicit semantics:

- Time starts at tick zero. A newly emitted addressed signal can be received no earlier than the next tick; otherwise reception is the earliest integer tick in the emission's light cone. There are no zero-time message cascades.
- Contacts use continuous swept geometry within half-open tick bins, including tangencies and contacts between endpoint samples.
- Default contacts are reported once for a pair of unchanged trajectory revisions. A contact may activate an explicitly configured external stop actuator and an outgoing notification.
- Simultaneous received impulses add on the pre-batch state. A configured contact stop takes precedence. The complete batch is validated before any change commits. Subluminal velocity is required.
- A contact stop on a body already at rest is a no-op: it opens no new trajectory revision, so resting bodies that stopped while overlapping do not re-collide every tick (H0157). The bodies a batch changes have their new pairs searched before any later batch commits.
- External burns/stops transfer the opposite linear momentum to the explicit actuator ledger. Scripted `SetCourse`, `SetCourseRange` (one hyperedge over `{lo..=hi}`), `FamilyPhaseBurn` and addressed `Payload::SetCourse` are scheduled external IR (`scripted-interaction-v1`), not a force law. A family member is extracted only when its generator actually changes. A shared course is not expanded into a clique of per-ship commands. Equivalence grouping is not interaction grouping. This is a controlled kinematic laboratory, not a model of collision material, relativistic propulsion energy, or a closed gravitating system.
- Proper time uses a law-defined dyadic rate per physical segment; querying or caching a segment does not add rounding.

`inspect` accepts only committed times. `decode` reconstructs a generator/identity without claiming its uncomputed future is certified. Sensor queries return retarded emissions and test moving finite-radius occluders, including unmaterialized family members; diagnostic queries expose true coordinate-time state. No observation changes the world.

## Compression and identities

An affine family stores an identity range, an original parameter offset, shared motion, spatial stride, radius and mass. A phase/departure family adds a shared cruise and a staggered departure sequence; members hold on the pad then cruise, so they need not share velocity at a given tick. Split ranges preserve the original parameter and RNG identity. Canonical initialization normalizes adjacent compatible partitions. A binary identity-range lookup avoids scanning every family.

An intervention extracts one member into the sparse body map only when that member's future generator changes. `FamilyPhaseBurn` splits holding/cruising runs and rebases each partition in place. Other members remain represented by the family, and geometric search excludes extracted identities from the original range. Exact total mass and first moments remain evaluable for untouched families. Full source compilation must additionally account for exceptions; no production gravity path currently consumes incomplete summaries.

Affine families require provably disjoint members sharing velocity. Phase families must also prove adjacent members never contact; internally overlapping families are rejected and must be materialized. Skipping interior contacts is not allowed. This limitation is explicit, not statistical merging. Multi-segment polyline routes remain deferred.

## Persistence and responses

Checkpoints have a schema/law identifier, payload checksum and canonical history checksum. Writes use a same-directory temporary file, data sync, atomic rename and directory sync. Restore validates the envelope and engine structure and rebuilds search state. `Engine::replay()` starts from retained initial generators and ordered external commands, regenerates causal events, and compares the complete canonical state. A 256 MiB checkpoint read/write cap prevents uncontrolled allocations; chunked long-history archives are not yet implemented.

Transport responses carry a time domain, full inertial boundary state and dependency versions. Composition rejects mismatched boundary velocity, position or versions. The bounded cache evicts reconstructible entries. The min-plus utility is limited to fixed complete discrete boundary states. It is not advertised as a general fuel-aware navigator or a complete nonlinear block eliminator.

## Native observatory

The UI speaks only `UiCommand` and `UiSnapshot` to a worker. The default world is the evolving spacetime laboratory patch `es-evolving-spacetime-patch-v0`, driven by public law-lab `advance_patch` / `inspect`; that is a labeled completeness experiment, not production gravity and not a G2 pass. The inertial laboratory `es-inertial-causal-v1` remains available (`--world inertial` or the inertial-laboratory control) and still owns `Engine::advance`. Rational-to-float conversion is for drawing. Navigation converts a selected heading to an explicit rational controlled velocity, which the canonical layer validates. It does not claim fuel-optimal routing or automatic arrival. Snapshots identify the active laboratory law and committed/pending time.

## Research gate and retained work

G0/G1 are tested independently. The law laboratory runs quantized velocity-Verlet and stationary piecewise-lapse experiments, including orbit/rotation/quantum checks and a direct moving-source causality counterexample. Neither may be promoted merely for stable orbits. A causal dynamic source law, well-posed unbounded exterior, compact-field treatment and terminating exact branch evaluator remain mandatory production gates.

The plan explicitly permits a verified causal kernel plus a named failed law gate. This repository follows that failure path. G3 and integrated G8 stay blocked; family, persistence and observatory mechanisms are useful partial work, not substitute qualification. No instantaneous gravity is silently wired into the canonical engine.

## Remaining runtime engineering

`WorkBudget` now charges box builds, family-range subtraction, a resumable stable merge, active-list expiry, AABB compares, causal-component grouping, guarded trace compilation, frontier enqueue, commit preparation, candidate reduction, contact refinement jobs and sensor family refinement. Swept-box construction is a resumable continuation. A simultaneous event batch still publishes as one canonical transaction; its collect/accumulate/validate steps yield, and clock/events change only on the final publish. After a burn or contact the co-moving SAP updates dirty items and retests their adjacencies (Coming & Staadt incremental sweep-and-prune). Overlap certificates survive quiet commits so later contacts do not re-run every certified pair. Connected components of the overlap graph are independent compile islands; a packed-overlap fixture forms one large island while far families stay out. Scheduled range IR drops the in-flight SAP rather than restoring pre-burn boxes. Guarded family/IR traces compile until the earliest possible invalidation. Sensor/occlusion queries are WorkBudget-charged continuations; a hard `max_pending_jobs` cap still returns an explicit resource error. Incomplete queries are pending/warning, never a silent empty sky. Byte-based allocation limits, multi-segment polyline routes, block elimination, per-region clocks and chunked archives remain required before full G6/G8 qualification.

The current native smoke test establishes successful rendering and real command-to-engine integration. It does not establish a 30 FPS / 1080p operating envelope, total GPU-inclusive memory, or parallel/GPU physics parity. The CPU benchmark reports process peak RSS separately from serialized state.
