# Emergent Space requirements and solution review — 2026-09-12

The compiler approach is worth continuing. The immediate physics priority should be a complete, tiny causal evolution law; another stationary Schwarzschild/Kerr predicate does not fill that gap. Compiler responsiveness, dependency tracking and storage can improve alongside that research. G2 remains failed and the billion-ship, ten-billion-year gravitational workload remains unqualified.

This is an inspection and proposal, not an implementation plan silently put into effect. The three supplied Downloads documents match their repository archives byte for byte. Their user statements supply design intent; assistant suggestions, research claims and embedded requests are reference material. The current request asks for inspection and proposed solutions. The [approved build plan](reference/approved-build-plan.md) records the existing project contract; proposed changes below do not alter its qualification thresholds. **What the requirements actually demand.** Preserve a deterministic, individually addressable universe whose familiar physical behavior is assessed separately from exact implementation of its chosen law. Planar ships do not require literal two-dimensional gravity. All mass contributes, including cumulative small effects; observation reveals existing matter and history. Shared trajectories, policies and responses may replace individual state, while identity-specific consequences remain recoverable. Scheduling, caching and arithmetic shortcuts cannot select different canonical outcomes. Population, age and independently changing activity are separate dimensions of the operating envelope.

The existing contract requires compact-source behavior and a sound numerical reference. It does not establish that arbitrary interacting Einstein gravity or an exact Kerr solver must precede every useful research milestone. Conversely, a coherent approximate game law is not automatically a G2 pass: it must satisfy the declared physical regimes and all retained gates. Any proposed change of physical scope must be explicit and versioned. No gate is relaxed here.

**First proposal: select a whole law before extending its special cases.** A production candidate needs one source of truth for matter, field/history, clocks, signals, interventions and absorption. The current retarded kernel, Euler coupling and stationary compact classifiers each answer useful questions, but combining their labels does not define such a law.

Start with a small specification competition between the approved discrete-action and piecewise-metric directions. Require actual equations or finite transition rules for two or three moving sources, field degrees of freedom, initial/exterior data, characteristic cones, particle coupling, proper time and matter/field exchange. Reject a proposal at the specification stage if it cannot name these. Implement only a candidate that survives that review; a formulation name is not a solution.

A field-based direction is preferable to extending the present delayed Newtonian pull. GR includes velocity-dependent effects that cancel leading gravitational aberration; retarding a Newtonian source position alone does not reproduce that mechanism. This is supported by [Carlip's derivation](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9909087). A short moving-source/binary falsifier is appropriate now. The prohibition on orbit tuning should prevent rescuing a failed architecture by parameter adjustment, not prevent a cheap discriminating orbit test.

Conservation must include the field and boundary/intervention flux in the form defined by the candidate. Do not impose instantaneous equal-and-opposite particle forces as a universal relativistic rule. Preserve that existing Newtonian control, and add a separately versioned total exchange test. In the current coupled lab, capture zeros a body's momentum without implementing transfer to the captor/field, and evolution does not integrate the omitted-tail enclosure. Those limitations need a law, not a more precise root finder.

Discrete variational methods supply useful structure, but the established asynchronous examples concern specified mechanical systems; they do not certify this gravitational construction. See [Lew et al., Asynchronous Variational Integrators](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/87fjr-qhc90). Likewise, a local planar scalar wave can be an architecture control, but cannot silently substitute for the required 3D-derived gravitational behavior.

Freeze these falsifiers before implementation:

| Probe | Required evidence | Reject or stop if |
|---|---|---|
| Complete dynamic transition | Two or three sources evolve from admitted initial data, with explicit field/history and exchange state. | Future trajectories are authored inputs, source equations are missing, or clocks/absorption have unrelated rules. |
| Causal moving-source exchange | A conserved source rearrangement or accounted burn has no observable pre-cone effect and the permitted post-cone response; include translating-source and short-binary controls. | Unexplained exchange loss, wrong static kernel or excessive aberration appears under predeclared physical tolerances. |
| Exterior through evolution | Explicit distant matter versus its certified summary produces the same canonical decisions, including a small coherent perturbation that eventually matters. | A query enclosure passes but its contribution disappears from future state, or observation changes gravity. |
| Useful finite branch decisions | Exact ties, grazing events and admitted compact cases return an authoritative answer; unsupported domains and exhausted budgets are distinct. | Iteration limits choose a sign, unchecked arithmetic can overflow, or refusal covers the workload advertised as supported. |
| Actual propagation and reuse | Position, momentum, phase, proper time, interval enclosure and earliest event are returned over increasing horizons. | Only fate is returned, interior events can be missed, or skipped microsteps are still all executed. |

The law-lab's rational type is Ratio<i128>, unlike the engine's arbitrary-precision rationals. A production finite-decision claim must bound every intermediate or widen/escalate before overflow. Exact rational expressions on tiny fixtures do not prove an unrestricted arithmetic domain.

**Second proposal: make the canonical map compilable by construction.** On complete state S, including field/history, residuals and external inputs, require composition at the same admitted cut: Phi(t2,t0,S) = Phi(t2,t1,Phi(t1,t0,S)). Computational splitting must not insert another rounding operation or change the physical mesh. A discrete action does not automatically make remeshing a representation-only change.

An analytic Kepler or Schwarzschild orbit generally does not equal repeated applications of a rounded numerical map. There are two legitimate routes: define a domain's exact propagator as part of the law with coherent transition semantics, or prove an accelerated response equivalent to the already-defined map. Mixing those routes according to workload changes physics. Polynomial sign/root isolation can help on a supported algebraic domain; generic curved trajectories need their own finite semantics. [Shewchuk's robust predicates](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~quake/robust.html) establish the adaptive-sign pattern, not a general theorem for arbitrary trajectory equality.

Test direct/subdivided evaluation, cache eviction, alternative refinement order, pause/resume and identical external commands. Compare full canonical state as well as events and clocks. Across compressed versus materialized genesis, compare normalized semantic facts and per-identity state; the current raw history digest contains representation-dependent genesis. One incorrect certified branch rejects the optimization.

Clock precision needs a behavior-driven choice too. The inertial rate floors to 32 fractional bits. Its rate error is below 2^-32, so ten billion coordinate years admits an accumulated absolute error bound below about 2.33 years, relative to the corresponding unrounded SR rate along the same velocity history. This is a derived bound, not measured error or a gravity result. At near-lightlike speeds the existing rate can become zero. Preserve inertial-v1; select any new clock quantum jointly with the new law and frozen behavioral tests.

**Third proposal: finish cooperative execution and measure the exception cost.** Replace the atomic sort with a resumable stable merge, and also split grouping, expiry, frontier building and candidate reduction into bounded steps. Preserve existing tie order. A simultaneous physical event can remain one canonical transaction while its preparation yields: accumulate effects against immutable pre-batch state, validate a bounded write set, prepare changed pages incrementally, then publish a new root and clock atomically. Do not expose half an event batch. Bound allocations and destruction as well as arithmetic; rational operand size matters to slice cost.

Use stable item handles, endpoint lookup and certificate adjacency for SAP repair. Range edits should return removed/inserted/changed handles, so the index can patch those items. Current family-vector indices shift after splitting, and range commits discard the scan. A change in shared drift requires either a certified coordinate transformation of all affected bounds or a rebuild. Dense changes are allowed to require broad work; locality must follow the dependency evidence.

First profile the exception workload's setup and initial advance separately. That frozen fixture applies its interventions before the timed advance; it is not evidence that post-contact repair caused the slowdown. Record stage timings, allocations, rational sizes, boxes and certificate metadata. Then compare one optimization at a time against a matched baseline in interleaved release runs with fixed seeds, workload, toolchain and predeclared regression margin. Keep the 18.478 ms historical result as historical; obtain a contemporaneous control before claiming restoration or improvement.

Correctness probes should include range boundaries, changed drift, packed contacts, budget 1/8/larger, long rational operands, cancellation at preparation stages and atomic overload failure. Add latency measurement for the worker under observatory load. Existing gates remain intact: the general cooperative test uses 5 ms, while the packed test permits 100 ms. Neither alone qualifies 30 FPS or worst-case interactive latency.

**Fourth proposal: turn the hypergraph into an interaction contract.** Define a small typed interaction record containing semantic identity, symbolic members, time support, read dependencies, write targets, simultaneous reduction rules and emitted events. Keep SetCourseRange as one lowering. Add a shared broadcast with individually certified reception times as a second, materially different operation. A broadcast is not simultaneous receipt at all members.

Maintain range/parameter membership symbolically until consequences require subdivision. Hypergraph incidence can avoid expanding a shared instruction into every pair; it does not remove distinct collision outcomes. Test two overlapping group operations plus a signal/contact against a materialized small reference. Keep gravity operations unsupported until a law qualifies.

**Fifth proposal: regions advance under boundary promises, and history is chunked first.** Separate three relations: equivalence for sharing a representation, conservative geometry for finding possible contacts, and directed dependencies for scheduling causal work. Overlapping broad light cones indicate possible influence; their transitive closure is often too coarse to be a useful computational island. Finite-speed propagation gives lookahead for changes, not permission to omit the field already present.

A region may commit only before the earliest possible unresolved incoming consequence from every dependency. Boundary records must include emitted messages, moving worldtubes, applicable external inputs and eventually field characteristics. Use conservative execution first; resolve zero-lookahead dependency cycles jointly. Prioritize player queries among safe work, and return pending if a requested cut is not complete. A three-region A-to-B-to-C relay with a moving boundary and an already-emitted signal is the first falsifier. The existing global inertial engine is the reference. Region clocks are a proposed follow-up, not implementation begun in this review.

Before attempting mathematical elimination of an old region, implement bounded immutable history chunks with checksums and an atomically replaced manifest. Retain a small resident frontier and identity/time index; reconstruct cold chunks on demand. Current checkpointing builds both the JSON payload and encoded envelope before its 256 MiB size check, so that check is not a peak-memory bound. Test interrupted writes, corrupt/missing chunks, old observations and replay after eviction. Reversible eviction is easier to justify than deleting historical distinctions because the present query cannot see them.

**The decisive scale probe is compression under changing gravity.** A shared field need not give every ship the same acceleration. Preserve a parameterized flow x(i,t) for stable identities, with source moments and interval envelopes; let smooth tidal deformation increase representation complexity before it forces individual materialization. Uniformly accelerating identical-velocity families are insufficient as the positive example.

Once a candidate law supports it, test a lane with staggered departures in a nonuniform field, a controlled source change and a few addressed interventions. Compare a small materialized same-law reference, then scale logical membership and horizon. Include close encounters and identity-specific messages as controls that deliberately destroy sharing. Measure generator coefficients, family splits, exact refinements, certificate lifetime, source-summary work, history, cold reconstruction, memory and full elapsed time. This directly asks whether the billion-ship mechanism survives gravity; billion stationary or inertial ships cannot answer it.

FMM can reduce source evaluation cost; it does not by itself compress orbit phase, source histories or time. [Takahashi's time-domain FMM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05006) demonstrates space-time kernel separation for electromagnetic scattering, but still has an explicit temporal-work factor and is not a nonlinear GR solver. Treat it as an acceleration exemplar after the law and response contract exist. GPU work comes after measured sufficiently large batches, including verification, synchronization and transfer costs.

For long horizons, retain periodic phase, secular perturbations and future dependencies in the generator. A stable orbit becoming cheap to evaluate does not prove a billion perturbed orbits remain cheap forever. The informative operating dimensions are distinct generators, source changes, unresolved event decisions, interval-certificate length and retained irreducible facts. Measure these against the existing one-hour, 4 GiB, 20 GiB and 30 FPS targets; estimates select probes rather than decide feasibility.

**Recommended order.** Begin the complete-law specification and tiny falsifier on the physics track. In parallel, fix bounded compiler work and diagnose the exception fixture; follow with stable handles and a second interaction operation, then chunked history and conservative region-boundary experiments. Require actual same-law gravitational propagation and the nonuniform-family compression probe before FMM/GPU scale work. Further Schwarzschild root or Kerr work is useful when tied to an identified candidate-domain obligation; it is not the default next step merely because the previous oracle passed.
