# Law `es-space-v2`: contract

Status: **DRAFT.** It freezes when r1 has a clean PASS, r3 v3 and r4 v2 are judged, and GLM has red-teamed this text. On freeze it replaces [gravity-law.md](gravity-law.md) and [universe-quanta.md](universe-quanta.md). The design is [representation.md](representation.md); the judge is `xtask behave`.

## What the law is

A fixed integer algorithm. Exact means bit-identical on every machine, thread count, evaluation order (sparse or dense) and silicon. It does not mean equal to continuum GR: GR is the oracle that judges behaviours. Floats never decide an outcome.

## State

- **Doll:** its mass M (logarithmic form), its parent doll, its centre (a tube in the parent), and its edge radius (the sphere of influence, derived from the mass ratio).
- **Tube** (one per body or photon), 16 bytes: the memo key's index (quantized E and L, 32 bits), the periapsis angle (32 bits), and the periapsis time in the doll's clock (64 bits; epoch and phase collapse into this one time). The doll is where the tube is stored; the type (mass, radius, spectrum) comes from its family's table. Position and velocity are derived when asked. The memo is keyed by (E, L) alone: a rotated orbit is the same cycle, so orientation costs nothing (found by `xtask galaxy`, 30 September: `crates/space`'s `Tube` held only epoch and phase).
- **Populations** (research crate `crates/shipmem`, 30 September): ships are stored as (population, ordinal). A population is either an exact program plus sparse scars (a scar is a ship's exact difference from its program, indexed by Elias–Fano), or packed blocks of 128 in birth order with the same scar layer; the cheaper form is chosen by exact byte count in idle time. The 16-byte Tube is the logical view that evaluating a ship returns. Creation events emit their program (launch times on a quantized grid, formation slots on a lattice), because a scheduled grid costs 0 bits per ship where Poisson launches cost about 14. Identity is provenance (birth order), never a tie-breaker. The allocator reads H_live, the live distinguishing information, as its memory budget: about +62 bits per kick and −60 per reabsorption.
- **Cycle memo,** keyed by quantized (E, L): the band-crossing table of one radial cycle; integer Δφ, Δt and Δτ; and ∂/∂E and ∂/∂L of each. It is shared by every tube with the same key.
- **Numbers:** 32-bit storage words and 64-bit products. Per band, fixed point with one shared exponent. Wide integers are allowed only at cycle creation. Increments and kicks are integers of the law, exact by construction (r2).

## Motion in a doll (one dominant mass)

1. **Bands:** r_k = 2^(k/m) in the doll's units, with m = 16 (r1: m = 8 is too coarse; m = 16 and m = 32 pass). The band index comes from integer bit operations on the Binet variable u = 1/r.
2. **The cycle is compiled once from (E, L)** (law `space-darwin16-v1`, 30 September; it replaced the in-band area-preserving map, whose band-centre tangents carried a first-order slope error in every cell holding a turning point). GR's radial function R = 2(u − u_a)(u_p − u)(u_3 − u) is factored by its roots, found from (E, L) in integers. In Darwin's phase ψ (u = c + A·cos ψ), dφ/dψ = (2(u_3 − u))^(−1/2), and dt/dψ and dτ/dψ are smooth. Band edges are cuts in ψ (integer atan). Each cut interval is integrated with 4-point Gauss–Legendre panels in integers; the outbound half is computed and the inbound half mirrors it. The exact text is in the `crates/space` docs, and that text is part of this law.
3. **Positions** are derived, never stepped: t mod the period, the mirror, the crossing table, and one Newton solve inside a panel. Precession and period errors are identical every cycle (about 10⁻⁶ at m = 16), the right error type.
4. **Time:** dt/dφ = E/(L·u²·(1 − 2M·u)) and dτ/dφ = 1/(L·u²), each integrated per band by a rule fixed by the band and by (E, L).
5. **The cycle:** after one radial cycle the crossing sequence repeats. The tube then advances by Δφ, Δt and Δτ per cycle, which is additions only.
6. **Light:** the same map without the M/L² term, parameterized by b = L/E.
7. **The horizon:** a tube whose u reaches 1/(2M) is absorbed at that event, and its E and L go to the doll's ledger.

## Events: the only places where work happens

- **Kicks.** The parent's tide and the neighbours' moments change E and L by integers. Kicks are orbit-averaged on stable orbits away from resonance: an orbit is predictable, so its average is its behaviour (Julien, 30 September: "Averaging an orbit or a population is not the same thing, an orbit is stable an predictable not a complex adaptative system unlike ships or chaotic n-body problems"). Ships and chaotic regions are never averaged. A resonance is detected from the compiled frequencies (p·Ω₁ − q·Ω₂ ≈ 0). Near a mean-motion resonance, kicks computed along the unperturbed orbit (first order in μ) are killed at any window size (r4 v1 and v2: they over-pump e by about 2× and the resonant angle circulates), because they lack the phase feedback that limits a resonance. The next configuration is the resonant normal form: average over the fast non-resonant angles only, keeping the resonant angle (a libration is a predictable behaviour), which leaves a one-degree-of-freedom integrable generator per resonant pair, compiled and memoized like a cycle. Its fallback is the dense order. A kick updates the cycle through its derivatives, by a multiply-add. A full recompile happens only when the key changes.
- **Perturbed motion: the compiled drift plus kicks** (1 October; r4 v2 and v3, r9, events3 and events4 killed the alternatives). A body's motion is a symmetric, symplectic split (Wisdom–Holman). The drift is exact along the dominant body's compiled geodesic (the tube: zero work), and the kicks carry everything else (the parent's tide, the secondaries, neighbours' moments), at a fixed step in the drift's own phase. Close approaches to a secondary use a smooth changeover function of the separation, which splits the interaction across recursive sub-steps (Duncan, Levison and Lee's SyMBA). That keeps the split symplectic, where a state-selected switch at the sphere of influence is not (events3: Jacobi growth 0.83). The binary doll as a Cartesian drift was killed (events4: Jacobi error −28 in 8 orbits). Kicks below one quantum are carried (sigma-delta) and fire nothing. Stable hierarchical orbits may take orbit-averaged secular kicks (r9: valid below τ₀ = 0.0036). Resonances need no special case, because the split keeps the resonant angle. Hill stability keeps its exact certificate: a closed neck at L1 and L2 means bound forever.
- **Gravity travels at c** (5 October; Julien: "gravity must travel at c we can't get lazy here and construct latter upon a flawed foundation"). No influence, gravitational or other, reaches a body before light from its cause could. Naive retarded gravity (pull toward where the source was) is wrong: it spirals orbits outward (Laplace's argument). In GR the pull of a freely falling source points to where it is now, up to radiation reaction (the aberration cancels; Carlip 2000). The candidate representation, under a GLM falsifier: **gravity is news.** A source's field at a body is evaluated from the source's tube as the body's past light cone last saw it; in free fall, that tube places the source where it is now. A change of the source's motion (thrust, contact, rebinding) reaches the body only when its light cone does. A fast source's field is its boosted field (B16's moving lens, for massive bodies). Radiation reaction, the first effect of the finite speed in free fall, makes tight binaries spiral in. Not built: the plane's kicks are instantaneous.
- **The doll's origin falls** (5 October; Sol's B12 adapter found the plane cannot express the gate's scene). A doll is centred on its dominant body, and that body falls toward its secondaries, so every member's kick includes the origin's fall: one uniform vector per kick epoch, the indirect term of a heliocentric split. Masses are a mantissa with the doll's exponent (fixed point with one shared exponent, as all numbers here), never a bare power of two.
- **Handoffs.** A tube crossing a doll's edge is re-expressed in the other doll's frame (a boost and a translation, at this event only). Carries are bounded (H0190 K4).
- **Two comparable masses.** They form one doll with M = m₁ + m₂. ν enters only through the effective-one-body energy map at 1PN. Confirmed by r3 v3 (float: precession 1024/1024, periods 632/632, the ν effect 500/504, assists 64/64) and by B11 on the integer law. r3 v3's patched-handoff criterion failed at a 10⁻¹¹ error level against a chaotic float reference, which is why handoffs are judged by B12's Jacobi constant. Regions without a dominant pair go to the representation where their symmetry appears (principle 32), and the dense order is the fallback.
- **Finding encounters** (research crate `crates/encounters`, 30 September; r8 v3 verifies it). Each orbit is a point (U, V, W) = (1/p, e·cos ϖ/p, e·sin ϖ/p) in Binet space, and two orbits cross only if ΔU² ≤ ΔV² + ΔW², a 2+1 Minkowski light cone (principle 32: a symmetry found by representation). At a crossing, the pair's timing is an exact integer circle rotation, so the next close conjunction comes from a continued-fraction recursion; a second-order window and an exact per-pair distance check decide it. Candidates stay implicit: bodies sorted by U, filtered by the cone. A kick re-sets only its body's candidate pairs, with no work per orbit in the steady state. Open: the setup (2.5–24 µs per candidate pair per element change) beats element-sort only if elements last more than about 0.37/σ_e orbits, and the law's kick cadence is to be measured; GR precession turns the rotation into a tilted one (r8 v3b).
- **Contacts:** the exact integer special-relativity law `es-lattice-relativistic-v1`, in the frame of one of the two bodies. Its carries are the rounding rule everywhere.
- **Decisions:** thrust changes the rapidity linearly in proper time; in a field, E and L change by the work done. AIs are deterministic parts of the law, recomputed when their view crosses a threshold. The player is the only seed.

## Causality and evaluation

- c = 1 cell per cell-update. An influence reaches a cell no earlier than its min-plus arrival time over cell crossings.
- **Sparse order:** jump to the earliest broken certificate, resolve it, notify the dependents. One queue, ordered by slack.
- **Ties (5 October, lab run 20261005-0543-redteam-6254: two orders of one same-epoch batch differ at order one).** Events at the same root-clock time that share a body are one batch, resolved together by the contact law's canonical batch rule (`es-lattice-relativistic-v1`: bodies sorted by id, pairs sorted); events that share no body commute. The queue's key is (time, smallest body id, kind), so every schedule, budget and thread count pops the same sequence. A player's decision takes effect at the canonical tick written in the input log, never at a wall-derived one.
- **Dense order:** lockstep in cache (CPU, or Metal on the GPU) for regions whose events per cell per light-crossing are near 1, inside a free-fall frame. **The frame is Fermi normal coordinates along an exact tube** (30 September, the representation that dissolves engine-judge E3 and E4). Its origin follows the members' centre-of-mass geodesic, compiled in the parent doll, not a linear epicycle about a circle. Its axes are non-rotating (parallel transport, geodetic precession included), so there are no circle moves and no Coriolis terms. Members feel only the tide x″ = −E(τ)·x, where E is the Schwarzschild tidal tensor projected along the tube (Clohessy–Wiltshire is its Newtonian, circular limit), applied as exact integer kicks at epochs. Re-anchoring is recentring plus rebinding the tube to the centre-of-mass state, with the exact remainder.
- **Both orders give identical bits.**

## Observation (never evolved)

A clock reads its tube's length. Colour is −p·u at the observer. Positions are gathered at the observer's past light cone. Lensing takes deflection(b) from the doll's memo.
